# Ears Ringing After Loud Crackers

A loud bang can leave a ring or a dull fullness in the ears. This often fades over hours to a day or two.

Move away from the noise, and rest the ears in a quiet room. Do not poke the ears.

See a doctor if ringing lasts more than a few days or hearing drops. Seek urgent care for dizziness with vomiting after a blast, or a bleeding ear.
