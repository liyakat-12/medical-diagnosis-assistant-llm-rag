# Backache With a Period

A dull lower backache with period cramps is common as the womb contracts. Pain can spread to the thighs. New, very severe back pain unlike your usual periods needs a review.

Heat on the back, gentle walking, and simple pain relief if safe for you help many people.

See a doctor if period backache stops you working or is getting worse over months. Seek urgent care for sudden one-sided pelvic pain with fainting.
