# Genital Herpes

Genital herpes is a viral infection causing clusters of painful blisters or ulcers in the genital area, tingling before they appear, painful urination, and flu-like aching in the first episode.

There is no cure, but antiviral tablets shorten outbreaks and reduce how often they come back. Condoms lower but do not remove the chance of passing it on.

See a doctor for painful genital sores, and tell a maternity team if you are pregnant, as special care is needed near delivery.
