# Itchy Bottom

An itchy anus can follow piles, a small tear, sweat, soap, or threadworms in children. Scratching makes the skin more sore.

Wash with water, pat dry, avoid scented wipes, and wear cotton. Treat constipation so stools are softer.

See a doctor if itch lasts more than a week or you see blood. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful swelling.
