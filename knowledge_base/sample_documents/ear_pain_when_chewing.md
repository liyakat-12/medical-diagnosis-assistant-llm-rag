# Ear Pain When Chewing

Pain in front of the ear when you chew can be the jaw joint or a tooth, not always an ear infection. After a cold, the ear itself can also ache.

Eat softer food, avoid chewing gum, and use a warm cloth on the joint.

See a doctor or dentist if chewing pain lasts. Seek urgent care for a swollen hot ear and fever.
