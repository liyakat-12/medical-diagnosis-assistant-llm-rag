# Itchy Eyes in Pollen Season

Itchy, watery eyes with sneezing in spring or summer are typical hay fever. The whites of the eyes look pink. Vision stays normal.

Wraparound sunglasses, staying indoors when pollen is high, and pharmacy antihistamine or eye drops if suitable can help. Do not rub the eyes.

See a doctor if eye itch is year-round or drops do not help. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye or sudden vision change.
