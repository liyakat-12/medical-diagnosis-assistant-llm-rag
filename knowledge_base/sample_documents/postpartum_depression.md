# Postpartum Depression

Postpartum depression is more than a few days of baby blues. Low mood, guilt, poor sleep even when the baby sleeps, and loss of joy last weeks.

It is treatable with support, therapy, and sometimes medicine that a clinician chooses for breastfeeding if needed.

Tell a doctor or midwife early. Seek emergency help for thoughts of harming yourself or the baby.
