# Stiffness After Sitting a Long Time

The back, hips, and knees often feel stiff after a long sit, then loosen after a few steps. Desk work, long meetings, and car journeys are typical triggers. This is usually mechanical stiffness, not automatically inflammatory arthritis.

Stand every 30 to 45 minutes, walk a short loop, and sit with both feet on the floor. A small lumbar cushion can reduce the first-step ache.

See a doctor if stiffness lasts more than 30 minutes every morning or several joints swell. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
