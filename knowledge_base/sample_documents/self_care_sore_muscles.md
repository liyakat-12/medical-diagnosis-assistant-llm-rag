# Easing Everyday Muscle Aches

Tired, aching muscles after extra walking, a new job, or poor sleep are common. A sudden snap during sport is a strain and is different from a dull next-day ache.

Gentle movement, heat, sleep, and fluids help ordinary aches. Simple pain relief can be used if it is safe for you.

See a doctor if aches last more than a week or a joint swells. Seek urgent care for dark urine after extreme exercise or a muscle that bunches after a pop.
