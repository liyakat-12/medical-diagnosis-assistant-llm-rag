# Pain Just Above the Navel

A burn or ache just above the navel after spicy or late food is often indigestion. Food sticking, black stools, or vomiting blood is more serious.

Smaller meals, less spice and late tea, and not lying down after eating help many people.

See a doctor for upper tummy pain most days. Seek emergency care for vomiting blood, black stools, or crushing chest pain.
