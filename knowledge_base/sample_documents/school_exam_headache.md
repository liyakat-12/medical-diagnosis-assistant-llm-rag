# Headache During Board Exams

Long study, missed meals, screens, and worry commonly cause a tight exam-week headache. A sudden worst-ever headache is not exam tension.

Keep meals, water, and short outdoor breaks. Ease extra late-night caffeine.

See a doctor if exam headaches become daily. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache, confusion, or a stiff neck with fever.
