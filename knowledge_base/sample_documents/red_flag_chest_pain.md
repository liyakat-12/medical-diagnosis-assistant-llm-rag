# Chest Pain Warning Signs

Crushing pressure in the chest, pain spreading to the arm, neck, or jaw, sweating, and feeling sick can be a heart attack. Sudden breathlessness with chest pain can also be a clot in the lung. Do not wait to see if it passes.

This is a warning note, not a diagnosis. Stop activity, sit or lie still, and call emergency services. Chew aspirin only if a clinician has already told you this is safe for you.

Call emergency services now for severe chest pain, pain with breathlessness or collapse, or pain after a blood-clot risk such as a long flight and a swollen leg.
