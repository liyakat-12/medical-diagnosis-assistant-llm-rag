# Sore Gum Over a Wisdom Tooth

A flap of gum over a coming wisdom tooth can get sore and swollen, especially if food traps. Pain can spread to the ear or throat on that side.

Rinse with warm salt water, clean gently, and see a dentist. Simple pain relief can help if safe.

See a dentist soon. Seek urgent care for facial swelling, difficulty opening the mouth, or a high fever.
