# Seborrhoeic Dermatitis

Seborrhoeic dermatitis causes greasy yellowish scales with redness on the scalp, eyebrows, sides of the nose, and chest. It often flares in winter and with stress.

Antifungal shampoos containing ketoconazole or zinc, used twice weekly, control it well, with mild steroid cream for flares. It tends to return, so maintenance treatment helps.

See a doctor if the rash spreads widely, weeps, or does not respond after a month of treatment.
