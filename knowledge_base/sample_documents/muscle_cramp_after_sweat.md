# Muscle Cramps After Sweating

A calf or foot cramp after a hot workout or a long day on your feet can follow fluid and salt loss plus tired muscle. Night cramps can follow the same day of extra walking.

Gently stretch the cramped muscle, walk it out, and drink water. Salty food or an oral rehydration drink can help if you sweated heavily, unless a doctor has limited your salt.

See a doctor if cramps happen most days or you have weakness. Seek urgent care for a hot swollen calf that is not a brief cramp.
