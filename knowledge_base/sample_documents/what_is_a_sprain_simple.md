# What a Sprain Is

A sprain is a stretched or torn ligament around a joint, often after a twist. The joint swells and is hard to use. A strain is in the muscle or tendon.

Rest, ice, a snug bandage, and raise the part. Do not play on it the same day.

See a doctor if you cannot take weight. Seek urgent care if the joint looks deformed or the limb is numb.
