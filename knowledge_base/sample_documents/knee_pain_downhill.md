# Knee Pain Going Downhill

Pain under the kneecap when walking downhill or down stairs often relates to how the kneecap tracks. The front of the knee takes more load on the way down than on the way up.

Take shorter steps, use a handrail or stick on slopes, and avoid deep knee bends for a while. Quadriceps strengthening usually helps over weeks.

See a doctor if downhill pain is getting worse or the knee gives way. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot straighten the knee.
