# Side Pain When Laughing

A catch in the side when you laugh, cough, or take a deep breath can be a strained chest-wall muscle. Crushing central pain is a different problem.

Support the side with a hand when you laugh, and rest from twisting sport for a few days.

See a doctor if the catch lasts. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or breathlessness.
