# Lymphoma Warning Signs

The classic signs are painless swollen lymph nodes in the neck, armpit, or groin lasting more than a few weeks, along with drenching night sweats, unexplained fever, itching, and weight loss.

Most swollen glands are due to infection and settle within two to three weeks. Nodes that are painless, rubbery, growing, and persistent need a scan and often a biopsy.

See a doctor for a lump lasting more than three weeks, especially with night sweats or weight loss.
