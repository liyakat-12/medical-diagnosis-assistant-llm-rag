# Itchy Eyes After Screen Time

Itch and burn after screens is often dryness from blinking less, not always allergy. Allergy itch is usually worse outdoors in pollen season and comes with sneezing.

Use the 20-20-20 rest, blink, and lubricating drops if suitable. Raise the screen.

See an optometrist if itch lasts. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye or vision loss.
