# Sweaty Smelly Feet

Feet that sweat and smell after a day in closed shoes are common. Bacteria on damp skin cause the odour. This is not usually a sign of serious disease.

Wash daily, dry between the toes, rotate shoes, and wear cotton or moisture-wicking socks. An antifungal powder can help if a pharmacist agrees.

See a doctor if the skin is very sore, peeling, or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a spreading red foot with fever.
