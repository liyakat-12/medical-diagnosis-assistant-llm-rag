# Chemical Burn to the Eye

A chemical splash to the eye causes immediate severe pain, redness, watering, blurred vision, and inability to keep the eye open. Alkali substances such as lime, cement, and cleaners are the most damaging.

Start rinsing immediately with clean running water for at least twenty minutes, holding the eyelid open. Do not wait for transport, and do not try to neutralise the chemical.

Call emergency services or go to hospital while continuing to rinse. Take the chemical container or its name with you.
