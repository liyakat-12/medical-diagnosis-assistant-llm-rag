# Keloid Scar

A keloid is a raised shiny scar that grows beyond the edges of the original wound, often after piercings, acne, or surgery. It can itch, feel tight, and be tender.

Keloids do not go away by themselves. Treatments include steroid injections, silicone sheets, pressure dressings, and sometimes laser or surgery combined with injections.

See a dermatologist early if a scar starts growing outwards, especially before having further piercings or elective surgery.
