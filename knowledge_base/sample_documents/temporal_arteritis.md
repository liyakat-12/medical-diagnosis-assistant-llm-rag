# Giant Cell Arteritis (Temporal Arteritis)

Giant cell arteritis is inflammation of arteries at the temples in people over fifty. It causes a new severe one-sided headache, tenderness over the temple, jaw aching when chewing, scalp soreness, and blurred or lost vision.

It can cause permanent blindness within days. High-dose steroids are started immediately, before biopsy results are back.

Seek emergency care the same day for a new temple headache with jaw pain or any vision change in an older adult.
