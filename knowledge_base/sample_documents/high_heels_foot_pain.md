# Foot Pain After High Heels

Heels shift weight onto the ball of the foot and squeeze the toes. The ball, toes, and calf often ache the next day.

Switch to a lower pair, use a cushioned insole, and stretch the calf. Limit how long you wear the highest pair.

See a doctor if the ball of the foot stays numb or a toe joint looks changed. Seek urgent care for a suddenly hot swollen joint.
