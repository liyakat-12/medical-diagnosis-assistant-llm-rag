# Back Ache After a Long Bike Ride

A long ride in a bent posture can ache the low back and the neck. Numb hands can come from leaning on the bars.

Change hand position, raise the handlebar if you can, and stand on the pedals every so often.

See a doctor if ride pain lasts into the week. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
