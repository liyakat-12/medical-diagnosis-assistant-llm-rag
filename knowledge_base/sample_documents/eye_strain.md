# Eye Strain from Screens

Screen eye strain causes tired, dry, or burning eyes, blurred near vision, and a dull forehead ache after long computer or phone use.

Use the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Blink more, raise the screen, and use lubricating drops if needed.

See an eye clinician if pain is severe, vision stays blurred, or one eye is very red.
