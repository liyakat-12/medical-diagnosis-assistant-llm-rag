# When Ordinary Pain Needs a Doctor

Most everyday aches ease in days. Pain that lasts more than a few weeks, wakes you every night, or comes with swelling, fever, weight loss, or weakness needs a clinician.

Write when it started, what sets it off, and what eases it. That history helps more than guessing a disease name.

See a doctor for lasting, worsening, or unexplained pain. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, sudden weakness, or pain after a major injury.
