# Acne Flare on the Face

Spots, blackheads, and tender red bumps on the face, chest, or back are acne. Hormones, some cosmetics, and sweat can flare it. It is not caused by poor hygiene alone.

Wash gently twice a day, avoid picking, and try a pharmacy gel containing benzoyl peroxide or similar if a pharmacist agrees. Results take weeks, not days.

See a doctor if acne scars, is very painful, or over-the-counter care fails. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful red face.
