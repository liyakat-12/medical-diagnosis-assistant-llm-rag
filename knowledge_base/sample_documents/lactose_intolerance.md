# Lactose Intolerance

Lactose intolerance means the gut cannot digest milk sugar well. Bloating, gas, and loose stools follow milk, ice cream, or soft cheese.

Lactose-free milk, smaller servings, or lactase drops help many people. Hard cheese and yoghurt are often better tolerated.

See a doctor before cutting all dairy long term, so calcium needs and other gut diseases are considered.
