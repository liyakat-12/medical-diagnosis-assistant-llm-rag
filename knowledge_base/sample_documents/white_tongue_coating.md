# White Coating on the Tongue

A white coat on the tongue after a dry mouth, a fever, or not eating is often debris, not always thrush. Thrush is more likely if the coating scrapes off leaving a sore red surface, or after antibiotics.

Brush the tongue gently, drink water, and clean the teeth. See a clinician before treating yourself for thrush if you are unsure.

See a doctor if a white coating lasts, is painful, or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a rapidly swelling tongue.
