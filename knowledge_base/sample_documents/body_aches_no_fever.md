# Body Aches Without a High Fever

Aching muscles without a high temperature can be a mild virus, extra exercise, poor sleep, or tension. Flu-like illness more often has fever, but overlap is common.

Rest, fluids, and simple pain relief if safe. Gentle stretching can ease tension aches.

See a doctor if aches last more than a week or you have a rash and joint swelling. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck with fever or a non-blanching rash.
