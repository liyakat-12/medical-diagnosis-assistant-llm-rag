# Headache After Drinking Alcohol

A throbbing headache the morning after alcohol is common. Alcohol dehydrates, disturbs sleep, and can widen blood vessels. This does not mean you have a brain disease.

Sip water, eat a simple meal, and rest. Do not take extra alcohol. Check that any pain relief is safe for you and that you do not take more than the label allows.

See a doctor if headaches come after small amounts of alcohol or you cannot cut down. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache unlike a usual hangover, or vomiting that will not stop.
