# Herniated Disc (Slipped Disc)

A herniated disc is when the soft centre of a spinal disc bulges and presses on a nerve. It causes back pain plus sharp pain, numbness, or weakness running down one leg or arm, often worse on coughing.

Most improve within six to eight weeks with gentle movement, physiotherapy, and pain relief. Complete bed rest actually slows recovery.

See a doctor for leg pain with weakness. Seek emergency care for numbness around the groin, loss of bladder or bowel control, or weakness in both legs.
