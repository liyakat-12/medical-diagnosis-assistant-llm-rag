# Vitamin A Deficiency

Vitamin A deficiency first causes difficulty seeing in dim light, known as night blindness. Later the eye surface becomes dry and wrinkled, and there is increased infection risk in children.

It is treated with high-dose vitamin A under medical supervision, plus foods such as dark green leafy vegetables, carrots, eggs, and milk.

See a doctor if a child struggles to see at dusk or has dry dull eyes. Seek urgent eye care for eye pain or clouding, which can cause permanent blindness.
