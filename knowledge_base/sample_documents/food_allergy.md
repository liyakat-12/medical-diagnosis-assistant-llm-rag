# Food Allergy

A food allergy is an immune reaction, often to nuts, milk, egg, or shellfish. Hives, swelling, vomiting, or wheeze can start soon after eating.

An allergy clinic confirms it. The food must be avoided. Some people need an epinephrine injector prescribed for emergencies.

See a doctor after a suspected reaction. Call emergency services for trouble breathing or feeling faint after a food.
