# Leptospirosis

Leptospirosis is a bacterial infection caught from water or soil contaminated by animal urine, common after flooding. It causes sudden high fever, severe headache, intense calf and thigh muscle pain, and red eyes.

It responds well to antibiotics started early. A small number of people develop a severe form with jaundice, kidney failure, and bleeding.

See a doctor urgently for fever with severe muscle pain after wading through flood water. Seek emergency care for yellow eyes, reduced urine, breathlessness, or coughing blood.
