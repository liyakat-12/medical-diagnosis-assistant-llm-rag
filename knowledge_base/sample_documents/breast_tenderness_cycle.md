# Sore Breasts Before a Period

Both breasts feeling heavy or tender before a period is common hormonal breast pain. It eases after the period. A single new hard lump is a different issue and needs a check.

A supportive bra, heat or a cool pack, and simple pain relief if safe can help. Reduce caffeine if you notice it worsens tenderness.

See a doctor for a new lump, nipple change, or pain in one spot that does not follow the cycle. Seek urgent care for a red hot swollen breast with fever.
