# Changed Smell After a Viral Illness

Smell can vanish or distort after viral illnesses including colds and COVID-19. Distorted smell, such as everything smelling burnt, can last weeks to months. Safety at home matters while smell is off.

Use smoke and gas alarms, label food dates, and a clinician can advise on smell training. Do not use unproven concentrated oils in the nose.

See a doctor if smell has not improved after a few weeks or loss was sudden without a cold. Seek urgent care after a head injury with fluid from the nose.
