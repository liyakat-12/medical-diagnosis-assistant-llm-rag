# Aching Joints When Walking

People say it hurts when I walk and mean the knees, hips, or feet. Extra distance, hard pavements, and unsupportive shoes commonly cause an ache that eases with rest. Pain that starts after a few minutes and eases, then returns, can be circulation-related and needs a check.

Shorter walks, better shoes, and building up distance slowly help many people. A walking stick can unload a sore hip or knee.

See a doctor if walking pain lasts more than a few weeks or you limp most days. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold pale foot or being unable to take weight after a fall.
