# Cauda Equina Syndrome

Cauda equina syndrome is pressure on the nerve bundle at the base of the spine. Warning signs are saddle numbness, loss of bladder or bowel control, and weak legs.

Emergency MRI and surgery can save nerve function. Do not wait for a routine back-pain clinic.

Go to emergency care the same hour for back pain with new saddle numbness or incontinence.
