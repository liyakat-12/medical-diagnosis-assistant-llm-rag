# Cough With Mucus

A chesty cough that brings up clear or yellow mucus often follows a cold or bronchitis. Green mucus alone does not prove you need antibiotics. Fever and feeling very unwell matter more.

Fluids, rest, and sitting upright help mucus move. Steam from a hot shower can loosen a tight chest. Do not give cough medicine to young children without advice.

See a doctor if mucus is bloody, the cough lasts more than three weeks, or you have a high fever and feel very ill. Seek emergency care for blue lips or severe breathlessness.
