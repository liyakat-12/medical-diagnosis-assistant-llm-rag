# Sour Taste on Waking

A sour or bitter taste first thing can be overnight reflux. You may also have a hoarse voice or a morning cough. A dry mouth from mouth breathing can taste stale without being acid.

Avoid a late meal, raise the head of the bed, and clean the teeth after breakfast rather than immediately after acid if you have reflux.

See a doctor for a sour taste most mornings or food sticking. Seek emergency care for chest pain with sweating.
