# Whiplash Injury

Whiplash is a neck strain from a sudden jolt, most often a car collision. Pain and stiffness usually start six to twelve hours later, with headache at the back of the head and difficulty turning the head.

Keep the neck gently moving rather than using a collar, use pain relief, and apply heat. Most people recover within a few weeks.

See a doctor if pain is severe or not improving. Seek emergency care for pins and needles in both arms, weakness, severe headache, or problems with balance after the injury.
