# Achilles Tendon Rupture

An Achilles rupture usually happens during a sudden push-off in sport. People describe a loud snap and a feeling of being kicked in the back of the ankle, then cannot push off or stand on tiptoe.

There is often a gap you can feel above the heel. The leg must be kept still with the foot pointed down until assessed, and treatment is a cast or surgery.

Go to an emergency department the same day. Delayed treatment makes repair harder and recovery longer.
