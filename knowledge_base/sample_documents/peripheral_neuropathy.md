# Peripheral Neuropathy

Peripheral neuropathy is damage to the nerves of the hands and feet. It causes numbness, pins and needles, burning pain that is worse at night, and a glove-and-stocking pattern that creeps upwards.

Finding the cause matters, with diabetes, low B12, alcohol, and some medicines being common. Treatment addresses the cause plus nerve-pain medicine and careful foot care.

See a doctor for persistent numbness or burning in the feet. Seek emergency care for rapidly spreading weakness or difficulty walking.
