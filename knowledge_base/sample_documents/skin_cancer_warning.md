# Skin Cancer Warning Signs

Use the ABCDE guide for moles: Asymmetry, irregular Border, more than one Colour, Diameter over six millimetres, and Evolving or changing. Also watch for a sore that will not heal, a pearly lump, or a bleeding spot.

Early skin cancer is usually curable with a simple removal, so speed matters far more than the size of the spot.

See a doctor promptly for any changing mole or a sore that has not healed in four weeks, especially with a history of sunburn or outdoor work.
