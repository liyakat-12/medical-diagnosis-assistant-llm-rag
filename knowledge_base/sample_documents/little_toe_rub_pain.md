# Sore Little Toe From Shoes

The little toe often rubs in a narrow shoe and forms a corn or a red sore. The pain is on the outer edge or on top of the toe.

Choose a wider pair, use a soft pad, and do not cut a corn yourself with a blade.

See a doctor or podiatrist if a corn keeps coming back, especially if you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a spreading red hot foot.
