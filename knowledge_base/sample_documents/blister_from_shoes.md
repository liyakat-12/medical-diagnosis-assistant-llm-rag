# Blister From New Shoes

A fluid bump on the heel or toe after rubbing is a friction blister. The fluid protects the skin underneath. Most heal if the roof is left intact.

Cover with a blister plaster, wear socks that fit, and ease the rubbing shoe. Do not burst the blister if you can avoid it.

See a clinician if you have diabetes and a blister on the foot, or if the blister fills with pus. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
