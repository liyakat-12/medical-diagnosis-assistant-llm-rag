# Snoring

Snoring is a rattling sound made when air passes relaxed tissues at the back of the throat during sleep. It is more common with weight gain, alcohol at night, and sleeping on the back.

Sleeping on your side, losing extra weight, avoiding alcohol before bed, and clearing a blocked nose usually reduce it a lot.

See a doctor if you also stop breathing in your sleep, gasp awake, or feel very sleepy during the day, as this may be sleep apnoea rather than simple snoring.
