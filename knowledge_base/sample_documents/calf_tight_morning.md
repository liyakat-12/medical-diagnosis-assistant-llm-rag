# Tight Calves on Waking

Calves that feel wooden first thing can follow yesterday's walking, night cramps, or tight Achilles tendons. A single calf that is swollen and hot is not ordinary tightness.

Gentle stretches, a short walk, and enough fluid usually ease ordinary tightness.

See a doctor if tightness is only in one calf with swelling. Seek urgent care for a hot swollen calf or tightness with chest pain and breathlessness.
