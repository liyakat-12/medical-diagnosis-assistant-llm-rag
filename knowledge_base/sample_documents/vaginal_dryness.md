# Vaginal Dryness

Dryness and discomfort with sex can follow low oestrogen after childbirth or around menopause, some medicines, or not enough arousal. It is common and treatable. It is not something you have to put up with.

A pharmacist can advise on suitable lubricants or moisturisers. Avoid scented soaps. A clinician can discuss other options if needed.

See a doctor for lasting dryness, bleeding, or pain. Seek urgent care for severe pelvic pain and fainting.
