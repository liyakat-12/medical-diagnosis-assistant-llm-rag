# Shoulder Ache After Badminton

Overhead smashes and serves load the rotator cuff. A sore arc the next day is common; a pop and a dead arm is not ordinary ache.

Ease overhead shots, ice, and keep the shoulder moving in a small range.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm. Seek urgent care after a pop if the shoulder looks square or out of place.
