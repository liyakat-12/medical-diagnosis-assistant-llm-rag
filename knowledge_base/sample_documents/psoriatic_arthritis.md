# Psoriatic Arthritis

Psoriatic arthritis causes joint pain, swelling, and morning stiffness in people with psoriasis. A whole finger or toe may swell like a sausage, and the nails often show pitting or lifting.

Early treatment prevents permanent joint damage. Medicines range from anti-inflammatories to disease-modifying drugs and biologics prescribed by a rheumatologist.

See a doctor for joint pain and swelling with a history of psoriasis, even if the skin is currently clear.
