# Drug Overdose

An overdose may cause extreme drowsiness or unresponsiveness, very slow or stopped breathing, blue lips or fingertips, pinpoint or very wide pupils, vomiting, seizures, or a weak pulse.

Call emergency services immediately, place the person on their side in the recovery position, and stay with them. If opioids are suspected and naloxone is available, give it.

Never leave someone to sleep off a suspected overdose. Tell responders what was taken and how much, as this changes treatment and is not about blame.
