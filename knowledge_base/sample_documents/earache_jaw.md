# Earache That Is Actually the Jaw

Pain in front of the ear that is worse when chewing can be the jaw joint rather than an ear infection. The ear canal can look normal. A true ear infection more often follows a cold and may reduce hearing.

Eat softer food, avoid gum, and use a warm cloth on the jaw. Notice whether the ear also feels blocked.

See a dentist or doctor if pain lasts. Seek urgent care for a swollen bone behind the ear or a high fever with a stiff neck.
