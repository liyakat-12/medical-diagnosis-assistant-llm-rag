# Clostridioides difficile Infection

C. difficile is a gut infection that usually follows a course of antibiotics. It causes frequent watery diarrhoea with a distinctive smell, abdominal cramping, fever, and loss of appetite.

It needs a specific prescribed antibiotic, and the original antibiotic is usually stopped. Strict handwashing with soap and water is needed, as alcohol gel does not kill the spores.

See a doctor for diarrhoea during or within weeks of antibiotics. Seek emergency care for a swollen painful abdomen, high fever, or blood in stool.
