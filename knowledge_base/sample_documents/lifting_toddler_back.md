# Back Pain After Lifting a Toddler

Lifting a child from the floor with a bent back is a common everyday strain. The ache often starts that evening.

Bend the knees, keep the child close, and sit to lift when you can. Ice after a flare.

See a doctor if the pain shoots down the leg. Seek emergency care for numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
