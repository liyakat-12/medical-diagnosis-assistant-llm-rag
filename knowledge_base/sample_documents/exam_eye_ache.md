# Eye Ache After Long Study

A heavy ache in the eyes and forehead after long notes or screens is typical eye strain. Vision that stays blurred needs a glasses check.

Use the 20-20-20 rule, raise the book or screen, and blink more. Daylight breaks help.

See an optician if study headaches are frequent. Seek urgent care for sudden vision loss or a painful red eye.
