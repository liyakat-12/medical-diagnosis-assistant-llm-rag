# Period Cramps

Cramping in the lower tummy and back around the start of a period is common. The womb lining is shedding. Pain can also radiate to the thighs. Most period pain is not dangerous, but pain that is new and very severe needs a review.

Heat on the tummy, gentle movement, and simple pain relief if safe for you help many people. A warm bath can ease the first day.

See a doctor if period pain stops you working, is getting worse over months, or you have very heavy bleeding. Seek urgent care for sudden one-sided pelvic pain with fainting.
