# Wrist Pain After Push-Ups

Push-ups bend the wrists hard. A new routine or stiff shoulders can dump extra load into the hands.

Do push-ups on fists or handles, and shorten the set. Ice after training.

See a doctor if the wrist stays swollen. Seek urgent care after a fall if the wrist looks deformed.
