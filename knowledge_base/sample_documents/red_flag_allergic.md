# Severe Allergic Reaction Warning

Swelling of the lips or tongue, a widespread hive rash, wheeze, and feeling faint after a food, sting, or medicine can be anaphylaxis. It can worsen in minutes.

Use an adrenaline auto-injector if one has been prescribed, then call emergency services. Lie flat with legs raised unless breathing is very hard, in which case sit up.

Call emergency services for breathing difficulty or faintness after an allergen, even if a first injection has been given.
