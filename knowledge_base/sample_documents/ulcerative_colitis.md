# Ulcerative Colitis

Ulcerative colitis is inflammation of the colon lining. Bloody diarrhoea, urgency, and cramps come in flares.

A gastroenterologist confirms it with tests and prescribes anti-inflammatory medicine. Flares need prompt review.

See a doctor for bloody stools lasting more than a few days. Seek emergency care for severe pain, high fever, or fainting.
