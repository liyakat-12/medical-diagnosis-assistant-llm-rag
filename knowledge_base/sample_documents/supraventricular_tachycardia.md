# Supraventricular Tachycardia

SVT is a sudden burst of a very fast regular heartbeat, often over one hundred and fifty beats per minute. It starts and stops abruptly and brings palpitations, chest fluttering, breathlessness, and dizziness.

Simple manoeuvres such as bearing down or blowing against a closed mouth can stop an episode. Frequent episodes are treated with medicine or a catheter ablation procedure.

See a doctor to have episodes recorded on an ECG. Call emergency services for a fast heartbeat with chest pain, fainting, or severe breathlessness.
