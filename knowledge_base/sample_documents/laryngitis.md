# Laryngitis (Hoarse Voice)

Laryngitis is swelling of the voice box, often after a cold or shouting. The voice becomes hoarse or drops to a whisper.

Rest the voice, drink fluids, and avoid whispering which strains the cords. Steam can soothe. Most cases settle in one to two weeks.

See a doctor if hoarseness lasts more than three weeks, you cough blood, or breathing feels tight.
