# Sore Muscles One Day After Exercise

Muscles can feel stiff and sore 24 to 48 hours after a new or hard workout. This delayed soreness is different from a snap during the exercise.

Walk, stretch gently, sleep, and ease the next session. The soreness should fade in a few days.

See a doctor if urine is dark, swelling is extreme, or weakness is severe. Seek urgent care for a joint that will not move.
