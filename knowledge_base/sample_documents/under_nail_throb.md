# Throbbing Pain Under a Nail

A throb under a nail can follow a crush, a tight shoe, or an infection beside the nail. Pus and spreading redness need a check.

Raise the finger or toe, ice a fresh crush, and do not pierce the nail yourself.

See a doctor for a very tight, throbbing nail or spreading redness. Seek urgent care if the whole finger is red and you have fever.
