# Haemophilia

Haemophilia is an inherited clotting factor deficiency, mainly in males. There is prolonged bleeding after small cuts or dental work, large unexplained bruises, and painful swollen joints from internal bleeding.

Treatment is replacing the missing clotting factor, often given at home preventively. Aspirin and intramuscular injections must be avoided.

Seek emergency care for any head injury, severe headache, or bleeding into a joint, even if it seems minor at first.
