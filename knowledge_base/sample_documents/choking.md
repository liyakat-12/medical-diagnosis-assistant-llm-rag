# Choking

Choking means the airway is blocked. The person cannot speak, cough, or breathe well, and may clutch the throat.

Call emergency services. Give back blows and abdominal thrusts if you are trained. Do not do blind finger sweeps.

Even after the object comes out, hospital review is wise if breathing was difficult.
