# Blistering After Bridal Mehndi

Some mehndi mixes inflame the skin a few days later. Blisters in the pattern of the design are a typical contact reaction.

Wash gently, do not pick blisters, and avoid further dye on that skin.

See a doctor for blistering or a spreading rash. Seek emergency care for facial swelling or trouble breathing.
