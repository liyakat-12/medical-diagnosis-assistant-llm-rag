# What Wind and Gas Pain Feel Like

Trapped wind can cramp, bloat, and move around the tummy. It often eases after you pass gas or a stool. Pain that stays in one spot and worsens is less likely to be simple wind.

Walk, sip water, and ease fizzy drinks and chewing gum. A warm cloth can help a cramp.

See a doctor for daily bloating with weight loss. Seek emergency care for a rigid tummy, green vomit, or severe worsening pain.
