# Knee Pain After Kneeling

A swollen, tender front of the knee after tiling, gardening, or praying can be irritation of the bursa in front of the kneecap, sometimes called housemaid's knee. The skin may look puffy.

Kneel on a pad, ice the front of the knee, and avoid repeated kneeling until the swelling settles. Do not drain the swelling at home.

See a doctor if the front of the knee is hot, red, and very tender, or swelling lasts. Seek urgent care for fever with a hot knee.
