# Pericarditis

Pericarditis is inflammation of the sac around the heart, often after a viral infection. The pain is sharp, central, worse lying flat and on deep breaths, and clearly better sitting forward.

Most cases settle over weeks with anti-inflammatory medicine and rest from strenuous exercise, guided by a clinician.

See a doctor the same day for this pattern of chest pain. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness, fainting, or swelling of the neck veins.
