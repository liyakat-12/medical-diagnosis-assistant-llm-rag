# Sneezing Fits on Waking

A burst of sneezes when you open the bedroom or step outside can be dust-mite or pollen allergy. There is usually no fever. A cold more often brings a sore throat as well.

Wash bedding regularly, keep pets off the bed if they trigger you, and consider a pharmacy antihistamine if suitable.

See a doctor if morning sneezing wrecks your day or you wheeze. Seek emergency care for lip swelling or trouble breathing.
