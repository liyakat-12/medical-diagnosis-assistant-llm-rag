# Stiff Neck From a New Pillow

A pillow that is too high or too flat can strain the neck overnight. The first mornings in a hotel or a new pillow are typical.

Try a medium height so the neck is in line with the spine, and give it a few nights before you judge it.

See a doctor if pillow pain lasts more than a week. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever.
