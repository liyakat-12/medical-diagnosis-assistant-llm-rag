# Pain From a Cracked Tooth

A sharp pain when biting that then eases can be a cracked tooth. You may not see the crack. Sweet or cold drinks can also set it off.

Avoid chewing on that side and see a dentist. A night guard may be discussed later if you grind.

See a dentist promptly. Seek urgent care for facial swelling or a tooth that feels very high after trauma.
