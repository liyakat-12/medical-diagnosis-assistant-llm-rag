# Stiff Neck From Sleeping Under a Cooler

A cooler or fan blowing on one side of the neck can tighten the muscles by morning. This is a common hot-weather stiffness.

Turn the flow away from the neck, cover the shoulders, and use gentle heat.

See a doctor if you cannot turn the head after a week. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck with fever.
