# Pain in the Temples

A tight ache at both temples after screens, missed meals, or a tight hairstyle is often a tension-type headache. One-sided temple pain with a red eye is a different problem.

Loosen hair, drink water, rest the eyes, and ease the neck and jaw. A short walk and a regular meal often help.

See a doctor if temple pain becomes daily. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache, or temple pain with fever and a stiff neck.
