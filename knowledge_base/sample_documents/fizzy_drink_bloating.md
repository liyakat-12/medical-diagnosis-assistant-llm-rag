# Bloating After Fizzy Drinks

Carbonated drinks add gas. Burps and a tight waist after cola or soda water are common.

Sip slower, choose still water, and do not drink fizz on a very full stomach.

See a doctor if bloating is daily with weight loss. Seek urgent care for severe pain or vomiting.
