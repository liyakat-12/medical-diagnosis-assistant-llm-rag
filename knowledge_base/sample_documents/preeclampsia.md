# Preeclampsia

Preeclampsia is high blood pressure in later pregnancy with signs that organs are stressed. Headache, vision spots, swelling, and upper belly pain can appear.

It can become life-threatening for parent and baby. Hospital assessment, blood tests, and blood pressure treatment are needed.

Seek emergency maternity care for headache, flashing lights, or sudden swelling in pregnancy.
