# Bursitis

Bursitis is inflammation of a small fluid cushion near a joint, often the shoulder, elbow, or hip. The area is sore to press and hurts with movement.

Rest from the trigger activity, ice, and suitable pain relief help. A clinician may inject the bursa if it lingers.

See a doctor if a bursa is hot and you have fever, which can mean infection.
