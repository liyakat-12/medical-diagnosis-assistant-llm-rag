# Bowel Obstruction

A blocked bowel causes cramping pain, bloating, vomiting, and no wind or stool. Prior surgery raises risk because of adhesions.

Hospital scans and drips are needed. Some blockages need surgery. Do not eat or drink while waiting for care.

Go to emergency care for vomiting and a silent, swollen abdomen.
