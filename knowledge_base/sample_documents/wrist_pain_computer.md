# Wrist Pain After Using a Computer

Aching wrists after typing or using a mouse are common. People say my wrist hurts after using a computer. Strain of the tendons, a bent wrist on a laptop, or early carpal tunnel tingling can all play a part.

Keep the wrist straighter with the keyboard at elbow height, take typing breaks, and stretch the fingers backward gently. Night-time tingling into the thumb and first fingers needs a medical check.

See a doctor if numbness or weakness develops, or pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care after a fall onto the hand if the wrist looks bent or you cannot grip.
