# Sore Rash Under the Breast

Sweat in the fold under the breast can make the skin red, sore, and sometimes smelly. Yeast can add to the itch.

Keep the fold dry, use a soft cotton bra, and wash gently. A pharmacist can advise a suitable cream.

See a doctor if the skin breaks or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
