# Common Reasons for a Mild Rash

Itchy bumps after heat can be heat rash. A dry winter itch is often dryness. Moving weals can be hives. A rash that does not fade when a glass is pressed on it, with fever, is an emergency.

Note whether the rash itches, blanches, and where it started. Moisturiser helps dry itchy skin.

See a doctor for a spreading rash or one that lasts. Seek emergency care for a non-blanching rash with fever.
