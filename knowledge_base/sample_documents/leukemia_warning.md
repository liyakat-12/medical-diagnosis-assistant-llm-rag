# Leukaemia Warning Signs

Warning signs include persistent tiredness and pallor, repeated infections, easy bruising or bleeding gums, tiny red pinpoint spots on the skin, drenching night sweats, bone pain, and swollen glands.

These symptoms have many common causes, but together and persisting for weeks they need a simple blood count, which is quick and widely available.

See a doctor without delay for bruising plus repeated infections and tiredness. Seek emergency care for high fever with shivering in anyone on chemotherapy.
