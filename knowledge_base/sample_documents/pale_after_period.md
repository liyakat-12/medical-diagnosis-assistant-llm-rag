# Looking Pale and Tired After a Heavy Period

Feeling washed out and looking paler after very heavy periods can happen when iron stores drop. Breathlessness on stairs and dizziness can occur. A blood test, not a guess, confirms anaemia.

Eat iron-rich foods such as beans, leafy greens, and meat if you eat it. Do not start high-dose iron tablets unless a clinician advises it.

See a doctor for heavy periods and lasting tiredness. Seek urgent care for fainting, chest pain, or soaking through pads every hour.
