# Vitamin D Deficiency

Low vitamin D can cause tiredness, bone ache, and muscle weakness. Many people have no clear symptoms. A blood test measures it.

Safe sun, food, and a clinician-advised supplement correct it. Do not take very high doses on your own.

See a doctor if you have bone pain or are at risk (little sun, darker skin in winter, gut disease).
