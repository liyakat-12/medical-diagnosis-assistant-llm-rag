# Stomach Ache After Too Many Sweets

A run of rich sweets can cramp the tummy, loosen the stool, or burn the chest. This is ordinary overeating for most people.

Sip water, walk slowly, and return to simple food. Avoid more sweets the same day.

See a doctor if sweet-aches come with unintended weight loss or thirst. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood or a rigid tummy.
