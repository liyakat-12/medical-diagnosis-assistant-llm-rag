# Tooth Pain With Cold Drinks

A short sharp zing with cold water often means sensitive dentine or a small area of decay. Pain that lingers after the cold is gone can mean the nerve is irritated.

Use a toothpaste for sensitive teeth if a dentist has not advised against it, avoid very cold drinks, and book a dental check. Do not put aspirin on the gum.

See a dentist soon. Seek urgent care for facial swelling, difficulty swallowing, or a high fever with a dental swelling.
