# Neck That Clicks When You Turn

Painless clicks when you turn the head are common. Forcing someone to crack your neck can injure joints or arteries and is not a treatment.

Move the neck gently through a comfortable range. Improve screen height if you sit a lot.

See a doctor if clicking is painful or you have arm tingling. Seek emergency care for neck pain after a crash or with a severe headache and fever.
