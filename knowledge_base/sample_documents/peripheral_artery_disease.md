# Peripheral Artery Disease

PAD is narrowing of leg arteries. Walking brings calf pain that eases with rest (claudication). Feet may feel cold.

Stopping smoking, walking programmes, and medicines reduce heart and limb risk. A clinician checks pulses and sometimes a Doppler test.

See a doctor for walking pain in the calves. Seek emergency care for a suddenly pale, cold, painful foot.
