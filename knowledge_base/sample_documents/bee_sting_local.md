# Painful Bee or Wasp Sting

A sting hurts at once and a small area swells and itches. A bee may leave a sting in the skin. Large local swelling over a day can still be a local reaction, not automatically anaphylaxis.

Remove a visible sting by scraping, wash, cool with a pack, and avoid scratching. Pharmacy antihistamines may help if suitable.

Call emergency services for wheeze, faintness, or lip or tongue swelling. See a doctor if redness spreads over days with fever.
