# Diarrhoea After Antibiotics

Loose stools during or after antibiotics are common because gut bacteria change. Most mild cases settle. Very frequent watery diarrhoea, especially with fever or in older adults, can be a more serious gut infection and needs a clinician.

Drink extra fluid and tell the prescribing clinician. Do not take leftover anti-diarrhoea tablets if you have fever or blood without advice.

See a doctor for diarrhoea after antibiotics that is severe or lasts. Seek urgent care for dizziness, blood, or a very high fever.
