# Epiglottitis

Epiglottitis is rapid swelling of the flap above the voice box. Signs are a severe sore throat, high fever, drooling, a muffled voice, and sitting leaning forward to breathe.

The swelling can block the airway within hours. Do not try to look inside the throat, as this can make the blockage worse.

Call emergency services immediately. Keep the person sitting upright and calm, and do not lay them flat while waiting for help.
