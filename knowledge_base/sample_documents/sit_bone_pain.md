# Pain in the Sit Bones

The bones you sit on can ache after a hard chair, a long desk day, or a bike saddle. Piles can also make sitting sore.

Use a cushion, stand every half hour, and ease constipation with water and fibre.

See a doctor if sitting pain lasts or you notice blood on the paper. Seek urgent care for heavy rectal bleeding or fainting.
