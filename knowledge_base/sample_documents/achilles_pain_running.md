# Achilles Pain When Running

A stiff, sore Achilles tendon first thing or at the start of a run is common tendon overload. The pain is just above the heel. A sudden snap and difficulty pointing the foot is a rupture, which is different.

Reduce hills and speed, ice after activity, and avoid stretching into sharp pain. Do not inject the tendon yourself. A clinician can guide loading exercises.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if Achilles pain lasts more than a week or two. Seek urgent care if you felt a snap and cannot push off.
