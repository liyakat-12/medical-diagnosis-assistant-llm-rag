# Red Eyes After the Swimming Pool

Chlorine and pool water can make the eyes red and gritty. Sticky yellow discharge the next day can be an infection.

Rinse the eyes with clean water after you swim, and use your own towel. Leave contact lenses out for swims.

See a doctor if pain is severe or vision drops. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye in a contact-lens wearer.
