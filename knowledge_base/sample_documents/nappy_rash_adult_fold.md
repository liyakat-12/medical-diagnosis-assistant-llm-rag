# Sore Skin in a Body Fold

Red, sore skin under the breasts, in the groin, or under the tummy fold is intertrigo. Heat, sweat, and rubbing cause it. Yeast can add itch.

Keep the fold dry, use a soft cloth between skin surfaces, and ask a pharmacist about a suitable cream.

See a doctor if the rash lasts or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
