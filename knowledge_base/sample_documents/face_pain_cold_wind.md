# Face Pain in Cold Wind

Aching cheeks or forehead in cold wind can be sensitive sinuses after a cold or simple nerve sensitivity. Tooth pain in the cold can feel like sinus pain.

A scarf over the face, saline if you are congested, and time after a cold help. See a dentist if one tooth is the clear source.

See a doctor if facial pain lasts more than three weeks. Seek emergency care for a swollen red eyelid with vision change.
