# Itchy Scrotum

An itchy groin or scrotum can be sweat rash, a fungal infection, or irritation from soap. The skin may look red in the folds. Sexually transmitted infections can also itch and need a different check.

Wash with water, dry carefully, wear cotton, and ask a pharmacist about an antifungal cream if a typical fungal rash is likely.

See a clinic or doctor if the rash lasts, you have sores, or a partner has symptoms. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful red area.
