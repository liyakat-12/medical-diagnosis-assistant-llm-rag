# Hives With Breathing Trouble Warning

Itchy weals plus wheeze, lip swelling, or feeling faint after a food, sting, or medicine can be anaphylaxis. A hive rash alone without breathing trouble is less urgent but still needs advice if it is new.

Use an adrenaline auto-injector if one has been prescribed, then call emergency services.

Call emergency services for breathing difficulty or faintness with a rash after an allergen.
