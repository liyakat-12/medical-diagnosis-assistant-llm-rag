# Pain Under the Right Rib Cage

A wave of pain under the right rib cage after fried food can be a gallbladder attack. Ordinary indigestion is usually milder and shorter.

Avoid a very fatty meal while this is being checked. Sip water if you are not vomiting.

See a doctor for repeated attacks. Seek emergency care for fever, yellow eyes, or pain that will not ease.
