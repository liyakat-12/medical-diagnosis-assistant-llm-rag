# Constipation From Pain Tablets

Codeine and some other strong pain tablets slow the bowel. Stools become hard and you strain. This is a known effect, not a new gut disease by itself.

Ask a clinician or pharmacist about fibre, a stool softener, and whether the pain tablet is still needed. Drink more fluid and walk if you can.

See a doctor if you cannot pass stool or have severe tummy pain. Seek urgent care for vomiting and a swollen tummy with no wind.
