# Feeling Sick in the Morning

Morning nausea can be pregnancy, reflux, a hangover, anxiety, or a medicine. In people who could be pregnant, a test is the first step. Morning sickness is common in early pregnancy but severe vomiting needs care.

Small snacks, water, and sitting up can help. Do not start leftover anti-sickness tablets without advice.

See a doctor if morning nausea lasts or you cannot keep fluids down. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood or severe headache with vomiting.
