# HIV Infection

HIV is a virus that weakens immune defence if untreated. Early infection can look like a flu with rash and swollen glands, then years may pass with few symptoms.

A test is the only way to know. Antiretroviral treatment lets most people live a long life and stops sexual transmission when the virus is undetectable.

Get tested after risk or if you have unexplained infections. This is not diagnosed from symptoms alone.
