# When a Headache Needs a Check

Most headaches are tension-type or migraine and settle. A check is needed for a new headache after age 50, a headache that wakes you, a change from your usual pattern, or a headache after a head knock.

Write down how often headaches come, what helps, and any visual changes. This helps a clinician more than a single bad day.

See a doctor for new or changing headaches. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache, weakness, confusion, or a headache with fever and a stiff neck.
