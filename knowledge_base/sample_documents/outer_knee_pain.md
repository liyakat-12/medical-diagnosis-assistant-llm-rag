# Pain on the Outer Side of the Knee

Outer knee pain that builds during a run or long walk is often iliotibial band irritation. A sharp outer pain after a twist can be a ligament or cartilage strain instead.

Reduce hills and cambered roads, ice the outer knee, and stretch the outer thigh carefully. Soften the training load rather than pushing through a sharp pain.

See a doctor if outer knee pain lasts more than a few weeks or there was a pop and swelling. Seek urgent care if the knee looks deformed.
