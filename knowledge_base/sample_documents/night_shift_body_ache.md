# Body Aches After a Night Shift

A night shift can leave the body aching from missed sleep, standing, and odd meals. This is not automatically an infection if there is no fever.

Sleep in a dark quiet room, eat a simple meal, and stretch the back and legs. Limit extra caffeine so the next sleep is possible.

See a doctor if night-shift exhaustion lasts on your days off. Seek emergency care for chest pain or fainting at work.
