# Neck Pain After Desk Work

A stiff, aching neck after looking at a screen is very common. Holding the head forward for hours strains the muscles at the back of the neck and can send pain into the shoulders or a dull headache.

Raise the screen to eye level, stand every half hour, and gently turn the head side to side. Heat and simple pain relief can ease a tight neck. Avoid forcing a crack.

See a doctor if neck pain lasts more than a few weeks or travels down an arm with tingling. Seek emergency care for neck pain after a crash, or with fever, a severe headache, or confusion.
