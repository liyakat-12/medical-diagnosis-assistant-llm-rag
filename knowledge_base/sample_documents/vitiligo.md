# Vitiligo

Vitiligo causes smooth white patches where the skin loses its pigment, often on the hands, face, and around body openings. The patches are not itchy or painful but burn easily in the sun.

It is an immune condition, not an infection, and cannot be caught from someone. Treatments include steroid or calcineurin creams, light therapy, and sun protection.

See a dermatologist early, as treatment works better on newer patches. A thyroid blood test is often done since the conditions are linked.
