# What a Migraine Is

A migraine is more than an ordinary headache. It is often one-sided, throbbing, and comes with light bothering you or nausea. Some people see zigzags first.

Rest in a dark quiet room, sip water, and use medicine a clinician has already said is suitable for you.

See a doctor if headaches are frequent. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache, fever and a stiff neck, or weakness.
