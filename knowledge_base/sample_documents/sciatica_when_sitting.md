# Leg Pain From the Back When Sitting

Pain from the buttock down the back of the leg that worsens when you sit can be sciatica, irritation of a nerve from the lower back. Tingling in the foot can occur. Most mild cases improve over weeks.

Keep gently walking, avoid long slumped sitting, and use heat on the back. Bed rest for days usually slows recovery.

See a doctor if leg pain lasts more than a few weeks or the foot is weak. Seek emergency care for numbness between the legs or new bladder or bowel trouble.
