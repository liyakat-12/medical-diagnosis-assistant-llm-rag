# Red Eyes After Swimming

Red, stinging eyes after a chlorinated pool are surface irritation. Vision should clear. Contact lenses in the pool raise infection risk.

Rinse the eyes with clean water or lubricating drops if suitable, and leave lenses out. Wear goggles next time.

See a clinician if redness lasts more than a day or the eye is painful. Seek urgent care for reduced vision or a chemical that is not pool water.
