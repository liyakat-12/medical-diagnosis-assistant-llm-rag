# Hydrocephalus

Hydrocephalus is a build-up of fluid in the brain. In babies the head grows too fast with a bulging soft spot and downward-looking eyes; in adults there is headache, vomiting, blurred vision, poor balance, and drowsiness.

Pressure must be relieved, usually with a shunt that drains fluid away. Delay can cause permanent brain damage.

Seek emergency care for severe headache with vomiting and drowsiness, or for a baby whose head is enlarging rapidly.
