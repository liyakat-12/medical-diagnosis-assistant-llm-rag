# Substance Use Disorder

Substance use disorder means continuing to use alcohol or drugs despite harm. Signs are needing more for the same effect, failed attempts to cut down, cravings, neglecting work or family, and withdrawal symptoms.

It is a medical condition, not a lack of willpower. Treatment combines counselling, medicines, and peer support, and relapse is treated as part of recovery rather than failure.

See a doctor or addiction service. Seek emergency care for alcohol withdrawal with shaking, confusion, or seizures, as this can be fatal without treatment.
