# Gastritis

Gastritis is inflammation of the stomach lining. It causes a gnawing or burning upper abdominal pain, feeling full quickly, nausea, and loss of appetite, often worse after spicy food, alcohol, or painkillers.

Stopping anti-inflammatory tablets, reducing alcohol, and acid-reducing medicine usually help. If H. pylori bacteria are found, a clinician prescribes a specific antibiotic combination.

See a doctor for pain lasting more than a week. Seek emergency care for vomiting blood, black tarry stools, or sudden severe abdominal pain.
