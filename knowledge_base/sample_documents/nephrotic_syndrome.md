# Nephrotic Syndrome

Nephrotic syndrome is when damaged kidneys leak large amounts of protein into the urine. It causes puffy eyelids in the morning, swollen legs and abdomen, frothy urine, and weight gain from fluid.

Urine and blood tests confirm it. Treatment includes steroids or other immune medicines, a low-salt diet, and water tablets, guided by a kidney specialist.

See a doctor for new facial or leg swelling with frothy urine. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, severe abdominal pain, or fever.
