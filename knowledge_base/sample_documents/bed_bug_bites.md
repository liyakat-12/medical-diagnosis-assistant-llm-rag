# Itchy Bites in a Line After Sleeping

Bed bugs can leave itchy bites in a line or cluster on skin that was uncovered in bed. The room may need treating, not just the skin.

Wash the bites gently, avoid scratching, and wash bedding hot if the fabric allows. A pharmacist can advise itch relief.

See a doctor if bites look infected. Seek emergency care for trouble breathing or facial swelling after bites.
