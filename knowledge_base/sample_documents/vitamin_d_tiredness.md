# Low Mood and Aches in Winter

Aches, tiredness, and low mood in darker months can have many causes, including low vitamin D, less daylight, and less activity. Blood tests, not a guess, decide if vitamin D is low.

Sensible daylight on skin when safe, oily fish, and fortified foods support vitamin D. Do not take very high supplement doses unless a clinician advises it.

See a doctor for lasting tiredness or bone pain. Children with bowed legs or delayed walking need a prompt review.
