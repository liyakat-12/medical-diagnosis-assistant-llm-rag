# Tooth Pain When Biting Down

A sharp jab when you bite can be a crack, a high filling, or an abscess starting. Sweet or cold pain that lingers can be deeper decay.

Avoid chewing on that side and keep the mouth clean. Do not put aspirin on the gum.

See a dentist promptly for bite pain. Seek urgent care for a swollen face, fever, or trouble swallowing.
