# What Everyday Body Pain Is

Aches after work, sport, a long sit, or a virus are common. The sore area usually still moves. Crushing chest pain or a sudden weak face is not everyday body pain.

Rest the sore part, keep gentle movement, and use ice or heat. Water and sleep help viral aches.

See a doctor if pain lasts more than a few weeks or a joint swells. Seek emergency care for chest pressure, sudden weakness, or pain after a major injury.
