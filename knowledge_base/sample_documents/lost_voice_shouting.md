# Lost Voice After Shouting

A whispery or gone voice after a concert, a cold, or a lot of talking is laryngitis. The vocal cords are swollen. Most cases settle with rest.

Rest the voice, drink fluids, and avoid whispering and smoke. Steam can soothe. Do not shout to be heard.

See a doctor if the voice has not returned after three weeks. Seek urgent care for noisy breathing or being unable to swallow saliva.
