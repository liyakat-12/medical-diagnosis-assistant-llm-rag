# Stiff Neck After Sleeping Awkwardly

Waking with a crooked, painful neck after an odd pillow or a cold draught is common. The neck muscles go into spasm. Turning the head one way hurts more than the other.

Gentle heat, slow turns within comfort, and a supportive pillow usually help over a few days. Avoid forcing a click. Simple pain relief can ease spasm if it is safe for you.

See a doctor if a wry neck lasts more than a week or pain shoots down an arm. Seek emergency care for neck stiffness with fever, a severe headache, or confusion.
