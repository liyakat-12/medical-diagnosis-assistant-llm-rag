# Pinworm (Threadworm)

Pinworms are tiny white thread-like worms that cause intense itching around the bottom, especially at night. They are very common in children and spread easily within a household.

Over-the-counter worm treatment for everyone in the house, plus hot washing of bedding and nightwear, short nails, and strict handwashing usually clears it.

See a doctor if itching continues after treatment, if the child is under two, or if there is bleeding, discharge, or weight loss.
