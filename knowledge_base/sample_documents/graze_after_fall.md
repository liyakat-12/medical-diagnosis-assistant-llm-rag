# Graze After a Fall

A scraped knee or palm after a trip is a graze. Grit can sit in the wound. Bleeding is usually a ooze rather than a spray.

Rinse with clean water until grit is gone, pat dry, and cover. Change the dressing if it soaks.

See a clinician for a graze that is very dirty, will not stop bleeding, or looks infected. Seek urgent care after a head bump with vomiting or confusion.
