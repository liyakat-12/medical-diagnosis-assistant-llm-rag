# Transient Ischaemic Attack (Mini-Stroke)

A TIA has the same signs as a stroke, with face drooping, arm weakness, or speech difficulty, but the symptoms clear within minutes to hours. It is caused by a brief blockage in a brain artery.

Even though it resolves, it is a serious warning that a full stroke may follow within days. Urgent assessment and preventive treatment greatly reduce that risk.

Call emergency services immediately for any stroke sign, even if it has already gone away. Do not wait until the next day to see a doctor.
