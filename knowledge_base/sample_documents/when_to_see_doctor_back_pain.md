# When Back Pain Needs a Check

Most simple back strains improve within a few weeks if you keep moving. A check is needed if pain lasts more than a few weeks, is getting worse, or you have had cancer, unexplained weight loss, or a fall.

Write down what eases and worsens the pain. This helps a clinician more than staying in bed.

See a doctor for back pain lasting more than a few weeks. Seek emergency care for numb buttocks, new bladder trouble, or weak legs.
