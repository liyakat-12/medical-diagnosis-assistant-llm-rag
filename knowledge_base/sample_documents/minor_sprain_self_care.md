# Minor Sprain Self-Care

A minor sprain is a stretched ligament after a twist. The area is sore, mildly swollen, and you can still take some weight. Ankles and wrists are common sites.

Protect the joint, rest from sport, ice for short spells, a light bandage, and elevation for the first two days. Then start gentle movement so the joint does not stiffen.

See a doctor if you cannot take four steps, there is a large bruise and swelling, or pain is not improving after a week. Seek urgent care if the joint looks crooked.
