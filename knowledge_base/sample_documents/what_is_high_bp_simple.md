# What High Blood Pressure Is

High blood pressure usually has no obvious feeling. A headache or a nosebleed is not a reliable sign. It is found with a cuff measurement, not a guess.

Reduce extra salt, keep active, and have your pressure checked. Do not start or stop tablets on your own.

See a doctor for readings that stay at or above 140/90. Seek emergency care for a reading around 180/120 with chest pain, severe headache, or breathlessness.
