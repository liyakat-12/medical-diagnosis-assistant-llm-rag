# Upset Stomach After Eating Out

Cramps, nausea, or diarrhoea a few hours after restaurant food can be foodborne illness or a very rich meal. Timing varies by the germ. Most mild cases pass with fluids.

Sip rehydration drinks, rest, and avoid preparing food for others until you have had no diarrhoea for 48 hours. Keep a note of what you ate if others are also ill.

See a doctor if you have bloody diarrhoea, a high fever, or symptoms last more than three days. Seek urgent care for severe dehydration or confusion.
