# Headache After Missing Coffee

A dull or throbbing headache with tiredness after missing your usual tea or coffee is a common withdrawal headache. It often starts within a day and settles in a few days if you stay off caffeine, or sooner if you have a smaller usual drink.

Drink water and eat. Reduce caffeine slowly if you want to cut down, rather than stopping suddenly.

See a doctor if headaches are daily and you need more and more pain tablets. Seek emergency care for a thunderclap headache.
