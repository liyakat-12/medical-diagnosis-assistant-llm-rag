# Weakness While Recovering From a Virus

Feeling weak and wobbly for days after a cold or flu is common. The body is still recovering. Pushing back into full sport too soon can prolong tiredness.

Rest, regular meals, and short walks that do not wipe you out. Increase activity gradually over days, not hours.

See a doctor if weakness lasts more than a few weeks or you have chest pain. Seek emergency care for sudden one-sided weakness.
