# Cold Feet in the Evening

Feet that feel icy on the sofa can be ordinary in a cold room, anxiety, or Raynaud's. Lasting colour change, pain, or a sore that will not heal needs a check, especially if you smoke or have diabetes.

Warm socks, move the feet, and avoid sitting still in a cold room. Do not use a scalding hot-water bottle on numb skin.

See a doctor if one foot is cold and pale or you have rest pain. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold, pale, pulseless foot.
