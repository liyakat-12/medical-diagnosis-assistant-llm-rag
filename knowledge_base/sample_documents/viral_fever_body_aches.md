# Fever With Body Aches

A virus often brings fever, sore muscles, and tiredness for a few days. This is the everyday 'viral fever' people describe. Breathlessness or a non-fading rash is not ordinary.

Rest, fluids, and age-appropriate paracetamol if suitable. Stay home while feverish if you can.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days or you are at higher risk. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, a non-blanching rash, or confusion.
