# Bronchiolitis (Infants)

Bronchiolitis is a chest infection in babies under two, usually caused by a virus. It starts like a cold then brings a wheezy cough, fast breathing, and poor feeding.

Most babies get better at home in two to three weeks with small frequent feeds and an upright position. Cough medicines and antibiotics do not help.

See a doctor if feeding drops below half the usual amount, there are fewer wet nappies, or breathing becomes fast. Seek emergency care for pauses in breathing, grunting, or blue lips.
