# Fever After Getting Soaked in the Rain

Getting wet does not by itself cause infection, but monsoon crowds and mosquitoes do. A fever a few days after an outing can be a virus or, in some areas, dengue or malaria.

Rest, fluids, and mosquito protection. Do not treat it as 'just a chill' if fever lasts.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than two days after the rains. Seek emergency care for bleeding, severe pain, or fainting.
