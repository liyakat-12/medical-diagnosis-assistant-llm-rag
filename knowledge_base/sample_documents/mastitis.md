# Mastitis

Mastitis is inflammation of the breast, often while breastfeeding. A wedge of the breast is red, hot, and painful, and you may have fever.

Frequent feeding or expressing, rest, and a clinician-prescribed antibiotic if infection is likely, are used. Do not stop feeding unless advised.

See a doctor for a red painful breast with fever. Seek urgent care if you feel very unwell or a lump with pus forms.
