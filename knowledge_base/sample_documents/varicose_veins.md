# Varicose Veins

Varicose veins are twisted, raised leg veins. They can ache, itch, or swell by evening. Standing a long time makes them worse.

Walking, raising the legs, and compression stockings if fitted, help. Procedures can treat larger veins.

See a doctor for skin darkening or an ulcer near the ankle. Seek urgent care for a suddenly swollen painful calf.
