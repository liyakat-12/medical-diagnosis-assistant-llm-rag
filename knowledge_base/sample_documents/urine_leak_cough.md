# Leaking Urine When You Cough

A small leak when you cough, sneeze, or lift is stress incontinence. It is common after childbirth and as the pelvic floor weakens. It is not an inevitable part of ageing that cannot be helped.

A clinician or physiotherapist can teach pelvic-floor exercises. Reduce extra caffeine if it worsens urgency as well.

See a doctor for leaks that affect daily life. Seek urgent care if you suddenly cannot pass urine, which is different from leaking.
