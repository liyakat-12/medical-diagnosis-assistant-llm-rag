# A Painful Bump Below a Teen's Knee

Active teens can get a tender bump below the kneecap where a tendon pulls on growing bone. It often flares with running and jumping.

Ease sport that hurts, ice after activity, and keep the thighs strong. It usually settles when growth slows.

See a doctor if the knee swells or the teen cannot sport at all. Seek urgent care after a sudden snap or if they cannot walk.
