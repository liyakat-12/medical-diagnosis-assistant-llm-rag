# Occasional Erection Difficulty

Trouble getting or keeping an erection can follow tiredness, stress, alcohol, or relationship worry. Lasting change can also relate to blood vessels, hormones, or medicines. One off night is common and not a disease.

Cut back alcohol, talk with a partner if you can, and see a clinician rather than buying unlicensed tablets online. Heart-health checks are often part of the review.

See a doctor if the problem lasts more than a few weeks or you have chest pain with sex. Seek emergency care for an erection lasting more than four hours after medicine.
