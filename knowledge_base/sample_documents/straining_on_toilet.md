# Straining to Pass Stool

Pushing hard because stools are dry can cause piles and a small tear. A few days of straining after travel or pain tablets is common.

Drink more water, add fruit and oats, and do not ignore the urge. A pharmacist can advise on a stool softener.

See a doctor if straining lasts more than three weeks or you see blood. Seek urgent care for no stool and no wind with a swollen tummy.
