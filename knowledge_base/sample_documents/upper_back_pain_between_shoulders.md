# Upper Back Pain Between the Shoulders

A burning or tight band between the shoulder blades often follows rounded posture, carrying a bag on one side, or long laptop use. Muscle fatigue in the mid-back is a common explanation, not automatically a disc problem.

Open the chest by sitting taller, squeeze the shoulder blades gently, and take short walking breaks. Heat and simple pain relief can settle a tired mid-back.

See a doctor if the pain is constant, follows a fall, or you also have unexplained weight loss. Seek emergency care for upper back pain with chest tightness, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the jaw.
