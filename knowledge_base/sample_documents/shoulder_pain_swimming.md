# Shoulder Pain After Swimming

Aching in the front or side of the shoulder after extra lengths is common tendon overload. Freestyle and butterfly load the rotator cuff. Pain may be worse when lifting the arm out of the water.

Reduce distance, mix strokes, and rest from overhead sets. Ice after swimming and gentle pendulum swings help. Return with shorter sessions.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm or night pain lasts. Seek urgent care after a sudden pop in the shoulder with weakness.
