# Sore Dry Nose With Crusts

Dry, crusty nostrils in winter can bleed a little when you blow. The lining is cracked. Picking makes it worse.

A little plain petroleum jelly at the entrance, if suitable, and saline can help. Humidify a very dry room if you can.

See a doctor for frequent bleeding or crusts that last. Seek urgent care for a nosebleed that will not stop after 20 minutes.
