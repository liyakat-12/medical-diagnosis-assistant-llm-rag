# Hard Stool Then Loose Stool

Passing a hard stool then looser stool in the same day can be IBS or overflow around constipation. Blood or weight loss is not typical IBS and needs a check.

Regular meals, fluid, and fibre introduced slowly can help some people. A clinician can sort out constipation versus IBS.

See a doctor if this pattern lasts more than a few weeks or you have blood. Seek urgent care for severe pain and vomiting.
