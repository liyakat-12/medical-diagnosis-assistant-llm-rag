# Trouble Breathing Warning Signs

Severe breathlessness, gasping, blue lips, or being unable to finish a sentence is an emergency. It can happen in asthma attacks, severe infection, heart problems, or an allergic reaction.

Sit upright, use your own reliever inhaler if you have one and know how, and call for help. Do not drive yourself if you can barely breathe.

Call emergency services for severe breathlessness, noisy breathing, or swelling of the tongue with wheeze.
