# Smoke Inhalation

Smoke inhalation causes coughing, a hoarse voice, sooty mouth or nose, chest tightness, breathlessness, headache, and confusion. Symptoms can worsen for several hours after the fire.

Move to fresh air immediately and call for help. Airway swelling can develop later, so anyone exposed to smoke in an enclosed space needs assessment even if they feel fine.

Call emergency services for any smoke exposure with hoarseness, facial burns, soot around the nose or mouth, or breathing difficulty.
