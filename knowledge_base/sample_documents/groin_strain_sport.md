# Groin Strain After Sport

A sudden pull in the inner thigh when kicking or changing direction is a groin muscle strain. Walking and bringing the knees together can hurt.

Rest from kicking, ice, and gentle movement as pain allows. Do not stretch hard into a fresh strain.

See a doctor if you cannot walk or there is a large bruise and bulge. Seek urgent care for sudden severe groin pain with fainting, especially in women of childbearing age.
