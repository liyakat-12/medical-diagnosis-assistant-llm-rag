# Pain in the Ball of the Foot

Aching or burning under the ball of the foot after standing or in thin shoes is common. The small joints and nerves in the forefoot are loaded. A feeling of a pebble in the shoe can occur.

Choose shoes with a wider toe box and more cushioning. Avoid high heels for a while. Pads under the ball of the foot help some people.

See a doctor if you have diabetes and a sore on the ball of the foot, or pain with numbness. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold or very swollen foot.
