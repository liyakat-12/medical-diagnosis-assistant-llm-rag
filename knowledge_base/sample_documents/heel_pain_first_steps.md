# Heel Pain on First Steps

Sharp heel pain with the first steps in the morning, or after sitting, is a classic description of plantar fasciitis. People say my heel hurts when I take my first steps. The band of tissue under the foot is irritated where it meets the heel bone.

Calf stretches, supportive shoes instead of walking barefoot on hard floors, and rolling a cold bottle under the arch often help over weeks. Avoid long runs until the morning stab eases.

See a doctor if heel pain lasts more than a few weeks or you have numbness in the foot. Seek urgent care if you cannot push off after a sudden snap in the Achilles area.
