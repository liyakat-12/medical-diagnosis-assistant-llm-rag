# Upset Stomach When Stressed

Worry can speed the gut, cause butterflies, loose stools, or a tight upper tummy. These feelings are real even when tests are later normal. New severe pain still needs a medical check.

Eat regular small meals, cut extra caffeine, and use a short walk or slower breathing when the stomach tightens.

See a doctor if tummy symptoms last more than a few weeks or you have blood or weight loss. Seek emergency care for sudden severe pain or fainting.
