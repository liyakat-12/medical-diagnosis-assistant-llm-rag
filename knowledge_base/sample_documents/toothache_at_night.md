# Toothache That Wakes You at Night

A throbbing tooth that is worse when you lie down often means an inflamed nerve or an abscess starting. Lying flat can increase pressure in the tooth.

Sleep slightly upright, use simple pain relief if safe, and see a dentist as soon as you can. Antibiotics alone do not fix decay.

See a dentist urgently. Seek emergency care for a swollen face or eye, or trouble swallowing.
