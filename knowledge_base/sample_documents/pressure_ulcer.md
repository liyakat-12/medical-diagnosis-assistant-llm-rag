# Pressure Ulcer (Bedsore)

Pressure ulcers form where skin is squeezed against a bone for too long, such as the heels, hips, and tailbone in people who cannot move easily. The first sign is a red patch that does not fade when pressed.

Prevention is the key treatment: change position at least every two hours, use pressure-relieving mattresses, keep skin clean and dry, and maintain good nutrition and fluids.

See a nurse or doctor as soon as a non-fading red patch appears. Seek urgent care for broken skin, a deep crater, bad smell, or fever.
