# Dry Skin (Xerosis)

Dry skin feels tight, rough, and itchy, especially on the legs and arms in cold weather or after hot showers.

Use lukewarm (not hot) water, a gentle cleanser, and a thick moisturiser right after bathing. A humidifier helps in dry rooms.

See a doctor if the skin cracks and bleeds, itches all night, or a red scaly rash spreads.
