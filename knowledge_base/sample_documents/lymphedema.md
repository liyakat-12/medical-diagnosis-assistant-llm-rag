# Lymphoedema

Lymphoedema is long-lasting swelling, usually of an arm or leg, caused by blocked lymph drainage after surgery, radiotherapy, or infection. The limb feels heavy and tight and the skin thickens.

Care is daily skin hygiene and moisturising, compression garments, limb elevation, and specific massage taught by a therapist. Treating it early prevents it worsening.

See a doctor for persistent one-sided limb swelling. Seek urgent care if the limb becomes hot, red, and painful with fever, which suggests infection.
