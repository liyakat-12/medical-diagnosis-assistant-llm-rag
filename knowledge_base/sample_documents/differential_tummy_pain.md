# Common Reasons for a Tummy Ache

Cramps that ease after wind or stool are often gut-related. Burning after meals can be indigestion. Pain with burning urine can be a urine infection. Sudden worsening pain with fever is more urgent. These are patterns, not a diagnosis.

Note meals, stool, urine, and periods. Heat and rest help some simple cramps.

See a doctor for pain lasting more than a few days. Seek emergency care for sudden severe pain, fainting, or a rigid tummy.
