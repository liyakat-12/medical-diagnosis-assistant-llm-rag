# Plantar Fasciitis

Plantar fasciitis is heel pain that is worst with the first steps in the morning. The band under the foot is tight and sore.

Calf stretches, supportive shoes, and not going barefoot on hard floors help most people over weeks.

See a doctor or podiatrist if you cannot walk or pain lasts more than a few months.
