# Brain Tumour Warning Signs

Warning signs include a headache that is worse in the morning or on coughing and is new for that person, repeated vomiting, seizures starting in adulthood, progressive weakness or numbness, vision changes, and personality change.

Most headaches are not tumours, but this specific pattern needs imaging. A brain scan is the only way to confirm or exclude it.

See a doctor promptly for a new persistent headache pattern. Call emergency services for a first seizure, sudden confusion, or a severe sudden headache.
