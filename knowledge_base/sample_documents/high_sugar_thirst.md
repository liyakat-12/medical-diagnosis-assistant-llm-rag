# Thirst, Tiredness, and Passing Lots of Urine

Unusual thirst, tiredness, blurred vision, and passing lots of urine can happen when blood sugar is high. These symptoms overlap with other illnesses, so a blood test is needed. This is not a diagnosis from thirst alone.

Drink water and arrange a medical check rather than guessing. If you already have diabetes, follow the sick-day plan you have been given.

See a doctor soon for this cluster of symptoms. Seek emergency care for vomiting, deep sighing breaths, confusion, or drowsiness.
