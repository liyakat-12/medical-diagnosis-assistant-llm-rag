# Burning Tummy After Alcohol

A burning upper tummy or chest after drinks is common because alcohol can irritate the stomach and loosen the reflux valve. A hangover headache can add to the misery.

Eat food with alcohol, sip water, and avoid lying flat. Cut back if this happens often.

See a doctor for frequent burning or trouble swallowing. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or vomiting blood.
