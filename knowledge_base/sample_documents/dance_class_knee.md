# Knee Pain After a Dance Class

Turns, lunges, and hard floors can irritate the kneecap. Delayed soreness is common after a first class.

Wear supportive shoes, reduce jump volume, and ice after class.

See a doctor if the knee swells or locks. Seek urgent care if you cannot straighten it.
