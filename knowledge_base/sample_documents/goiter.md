# Goitre (Enlarged Thyroid)

A goitre is a swelling at the front of the neck from an enlarged thyroid. It may move when swallowing, feel tight, cause a cough, and in larger cases make swallowing or breathing uncomfortable.

Causes include iodine deficiency and thyroid autoimmune disease. Blood tests and an ultrasound guide treatment, which may be iodine, thyroid medicine, or surgery.

See a doctor for any new neck lump. Seek urgent care for difficulty breathing, a hoarse voice, or a rapidly growing hard lump.
