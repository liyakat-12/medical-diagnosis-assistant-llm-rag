# Intestinal Gas and Bloating

Gas and bloating after beans, fizzy drinks, or gulping air are common. The belly feels tight and you pass wind more often.

Eat more slowly, skip extra carbonated drinks, and try smaller meals. A short walk after eating can help.

See a doctor for bloating with weight loss, blood in stool, or pain that wakes you at night.
