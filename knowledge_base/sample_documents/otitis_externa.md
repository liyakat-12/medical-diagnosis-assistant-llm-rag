# Swimmer's Ear (Otitis Externa)

Swimmer's ear is infection of the outer ear canal. The ear hurts when the pinna is pulled, and it may itch or drain.

Keep the ear dry. A clinician may prescribe ear drops. Do not poke the canal with buds.

See a doctor for ear pain after swimming. Seek care promptly if you have diabetes or the swelling spreads.
