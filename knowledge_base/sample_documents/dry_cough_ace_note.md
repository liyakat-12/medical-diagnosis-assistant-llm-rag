# Tickly Cough After Starting a Blood Pressure Tablet

Some blood-pressure tablets in the ACE-inhibitor group can cause a dry tickly cough. Do not stop a prescribed tablet on your own. Many other coughs are leftover viruses, not the tablet.

Tell the clinician who prescribed the tablet. They can decide if a change is needed. Note when the cough started relative to the new medicine.

See the prescribing clinician for a new cough after a new tablet. Seek emergency care for breathlessness or swelling of the lips.
