# Fifth Disease (Slapped Cheek)

Fifth disease starts with mild fever and cold symptoms, then a bright red rash on both cheeks that looks like slapped skin, followed by a lacy pink rash on the arms and body.

It is mild in most children and needs only rest and fluids. Once the rash appears the child is no longer infectious and can return to school.

Tell a doctor if you are pregnant, have sickle cell disease, or have weak immunity and have been exposed, as these groups need extra checks.
