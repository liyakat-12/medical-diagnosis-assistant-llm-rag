# Cracked Heels

Dry heels that split can hurt when you walk and may bleed. Open-back shoes and dry skin make cracks worse. Deep cracks can get infected.

Moisturise thickly, wear closed supportive shoes, and gently file hard skin. Do not cut deep cracks yourself.

See a clinician if you have diabetes and cracked heels, or if cracks look infected. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
