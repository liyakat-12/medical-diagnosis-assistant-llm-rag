# Wind and Tummy Cramps

Cramping that eases after passing wind is often gas. Beans, onions, fizz, and eating quickly add air. A sensitive gut can amplify ordinary gas.

Eat slowly, reduce the trigger foods you notice, and walk after meals. Do not skip meals then eat a huge plate.

See a doctor if cramps and wind are daily with weight loss or blood. Seek urgent care for a swollen painful tummy with no wind or stool.
