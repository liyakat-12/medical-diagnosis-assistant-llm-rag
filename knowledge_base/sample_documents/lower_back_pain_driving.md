# Lower Back Pain After Driving

A long drive in a slumped seat can leave the lower back tight or one-sided. The lumbar joints and discs take more load when the hips are bent for hours.

Stop every hour or two to walk, sit with the seat slightly reclined, and use a small lumbar cushion. A short walk after you arrive loosens the back better than lying still.

See a doctor if driving pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
