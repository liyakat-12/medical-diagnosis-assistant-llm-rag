# Soothing a Dry Tickly Cough

A dry cough after a cold can last three weeks. Honey, warm drinks, and sitting upright at night are simple measures. Cough medicines have a limited role and are not for young children.

Avoid smoke and very dry air. Honey is not for children under one year old.

See a doctor if a cough lasts more than three to four weeks. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or coughing blood.
