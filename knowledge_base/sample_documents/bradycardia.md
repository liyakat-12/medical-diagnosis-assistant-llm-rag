# Bradycardia (Slow Heart Rate)

Bradycardia is a resting heart rate below sixty beats per minute. In trained athletes this is normal, but when the heart is too slow to supply the body it causes tiredness, dizziness, breathlessness, and fainting.

Causes include ageing of the heart's wiring, an underactive thyroid, and certain medicines. A clinician may adjust medicines or fit a pacemaker.

See a doctor for a slow pulse with dizziness or tiredness. Call emergency services for fainting, chest pain, or confusion with a very slow pulse.
