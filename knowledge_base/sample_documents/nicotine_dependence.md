# Nicotine Dependence

Nicotine dependence shows as smoking or vaping within thirty minutes of waking, failed quit attempts, and withdrawal symptoms such as irritability, restlessness, poor concentration, and strong cravings.

Combining nicotine replacement or prescribed medicine with behavioural support roughly triples the chance of quitting compared with willpower alone. Cravings usually peak in the first week.

See a doctor or a stop-smoking service for a quit plan. Benefits to the heart and lungs begin within days of stopping.
