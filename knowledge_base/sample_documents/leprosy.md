# Leprosy (Hansen Disease)

Leprosy is a slow bacterial infection of the skin and nerves. The hallmark is a pale or reddish skin patch with loss of sensation in it, along with numb hands or feet, weakness, and thickened nerves.

It is curable with free multidrug therapy and stops being infectious within days of starting treatment. Early treatment prevents permanent nerve damage and disability.

See a doctor for any skin patch that has lost feeling. Seek prompt care for new weakness, as nerve damage can become permanent.
