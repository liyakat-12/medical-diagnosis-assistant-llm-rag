# Gonorrhea

Gonorrhea can cause discharge, burning urine, or sore throat after oral sex. Many infections have no symptoms and can spread.

A clinician tests and treats with the current recommended antibiotics. Sex should wait until treatment is complete.

Get tested after unprotected sex or symptoms. Seek urgent care for pelvic or testicular pain with fever.
