# Nosebleed in Dry Air

A short nosebleed from one side is common in dry heated rooms or after nose picking. Blood runs from the front of the nose. Bleeding after a head injury or that will not stop is more serious.

Sit forward, pinch the soft part of the nose for 10 to 15 minutes, and breathe through the mouth. Do not tip the head back.

See a doctor for frequent nosebleeds. Seek urgent care if bleeding will not stop after 20 minutes, or you feel faint or vomit blood.
