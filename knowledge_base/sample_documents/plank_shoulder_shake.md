# Shaking Sore Shoulders After Planks

A long plank can tire the shoulders and the wrists. A sharp click with weakness is not ordinary tiredness.

Drop to the knees, shorten holds, and keep the shoulders over the elbows.

See a doctor if the shoulder stays weak. Seek urgent care if it looks out of place.
