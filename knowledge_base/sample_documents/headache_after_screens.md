# Headache After Looking at Screens

A tight band around the head after long screen time often goes with dry eyes, a forward neck, and skipped water or meals. This is usually a tension-type or eye-strain headache, not a sign of a serious brain problem on its own.

Use the 20-20-20 eye rest, blink more, raise the screen, and drink water. A short walk outdoors can reset both the neck and the eyes.

See a doctor if screen headaches become daily or you need glasses checked. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache unlike your usual ones.
