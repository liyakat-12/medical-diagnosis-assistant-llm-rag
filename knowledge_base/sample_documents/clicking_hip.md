# Clicking Hip When Walking

A snap on the outer hip or in the groin when you walk can be a tendon sliding over bone. It is common and often painless. Painful clicking after a fall needs a check.

Avoid forcing the hip to click. Gentle strengthening and not sitting cross-legged can reduce a snappy tendon.

See a doctor if the click is painful or the hip feels unstable. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot walk.
