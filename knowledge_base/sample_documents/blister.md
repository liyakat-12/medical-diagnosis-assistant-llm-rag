# Skin Blister

A blister is a fluid bump from friction, a burn, or a pinch. The roof protects the raw skin underneath.

Leave an intact blister closed, cover it with a clean dressing, and reduce rubbing. If it bursts, do not peel the skin; keep it clean.

See a doctor if the blister is very large, from a burn or chemical, looks infected, or you have diabetes and a foot blister.
