# What Dehydration Feels Like

Dehydration means you do not have enough fluid. You may have a dry mouth, dark urine, a headache, and dizziness on standing. Heat, vomiting, and diarrhoea make it more likely.

Sip water or an oral rehydration drink. Rest in the shade if you are hot.

See a doctor if you cannot keep fluids down. Seek emergency care for confusion, no urine, or fainting.
