# Sickle Cell Pain Crisis

A sickle cell crisis is sudden severe pain in bones, chest, or abdomen when red cells sickle and block flow. Fever or chest pain can mean danger.

People with known sickle cell disease need urgent fluids, oxygen, and strong pain care in hospital. Home rest is not enough for a severe crisis.

Go to emergency care for a severe crisis, chest pain, or fever if you have sickle cell disease.
