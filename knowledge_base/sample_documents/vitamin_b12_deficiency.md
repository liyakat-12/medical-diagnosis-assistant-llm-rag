# Vitamin B12 Deficiency

Low B12 can cause tiredness, a sore tongue, pins and needles, and memory fog. It is more common with vegan diets or after stomach surgery.

A blood test confirms it. Tablets or injections prescribed by a clinician rebuild stores.

See a doctor for lasting tingling and tiredness. Do not self-inject without advice.
