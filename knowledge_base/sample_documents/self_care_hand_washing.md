# Hand Washing to Reduce Colds

Colds spread on hands and droplets. Washing with soap and water, especially after being out or before eating, reduces how often you pass a cold on. This does not stop every virus.

Wash for the time it takes to sing a short song, and dry your hands. Stay home when feverish if you can.

See a doctor if you are at higher risk and become very unwell. Seek emergency care for breathlessness or a non-blanching rash with fever.
