# Trouble Falling Asleep

Lying awake for a long time, or waking too early, is common during stress, late caffeine, irregular bedtimes, or pain. A few poor nights do not mean a lifelong sleep disease.

Keep a regular get-up time, dim screens before bed, and use the bed for sleep rather than scrolling. Short daytime activity and less caffeine after mid-afternoon help many people.

See a doctor if poor sleep lasts more than a few weeks, you snore and stop breathing, or low mood is also present. Do not start sleeping tablets without advice.
