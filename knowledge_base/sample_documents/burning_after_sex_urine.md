# Burning Urine After Sex

A sting when you urinate in the day after sex can be a urine infection or irritation. Passing urine after sex and avoiding harsh soaps can reduce some attacks. This is not proof of a sexually transmitted infection, but those can cause similar symptoms.

Drink extra water and see a clinician rather than guessing treatment. A sexual-health check is wise if you have a new partner or discharge.

See a clinic or doctor the same day if burning lasts or you are pregnant. Seek urgent care for fever, shaking, or back pain.
