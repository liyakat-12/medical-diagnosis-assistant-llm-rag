# Cataract

A cataract clouds the lens of the eye. Vision becomes misty, glare at night increases, and colours look faded. It is common with age.

New glasses help for a while. Surgery to replace the lens is the lasting treatment when daily life is affected.

See an eye clinician for gradual cloudy vision. Sudden vision loss is not a typical cataract and needs urgent care.
