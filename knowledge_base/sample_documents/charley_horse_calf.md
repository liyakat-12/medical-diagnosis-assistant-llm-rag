# A Sudden Calf Cramp at Night

A sudden knot in the calf in bed is a common cramp. The muscle feels rock-hard and then aches. A swollen calf after travel is not a cramp.

Pull the toes up toward you, stretch the calf, and walk a few steps. Extra fluids and a regular stretch before bed help some people.

See a doctor for nightly cramps that wreck sleep. Seek emergency care for a swollen painful calf, especially after a flight.
