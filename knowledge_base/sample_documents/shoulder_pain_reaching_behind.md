# Shoulder Pain Reaching Behind the Back

Pain or stiffness when fastening a bra, tucking in a shirt, or reaching a back pocket often means the shoulder capsule or rotator cuff is irritated. Frozen shoulder makes this movement especially limited.

Keep the shoulder gently moving within a comfortable range. Avoid forcing the hand up the back. A warm shower can ease morning stiffness.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if you cannot reach behind your back after a few weeks. Seek urgent care if the shoulder looks square after a fall.
