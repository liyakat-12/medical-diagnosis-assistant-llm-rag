# Ankylosing Spondylitis

This is inflammatory arthritis of the spine, usually starting before age forty-five. The key clue is back pain and stiffness that is worse after rest and in the early morning, lasts over thirty minutes, and improves with exercise.

Daily stretching and exercise are central to keeping the spine mobile. Anti-inflammatory medicines and biologic treatments control the inflammation.

See a doctor for back pain lasting more than three months in a young adult that is worse with rest and better with movement.
