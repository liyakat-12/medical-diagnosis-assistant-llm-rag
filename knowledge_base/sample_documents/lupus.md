# Lupus (SLE)

Lupus is an autoimmune disease. People may have a facial rash in sun, joint pain, extreme tiredness, and mouth ulcers. It can affect kidneys.

A rheumatologist uses blood tests and clinical signs. Sun protection and prescribed medicine reduce flares.

See a doctor for a lasting butterfly rash with joint pain. Seek urgent care for chest pain or little urine with swelling.
