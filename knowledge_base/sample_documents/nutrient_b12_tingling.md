# Tingling and Tiredness That Lasts

Lasting tiredness with tingling in the feet or a sore tongue can happen when vitamin B12 is low. Causes include diet without animal products and problems absorbing B12. A blood test is needed; do not guess high-dose injections.

A clinician can advise on diet and whether tablets or injections are needed. Untreated low B12 can harm nerves over time.

See a doctor for tingling with tiredness or a sore tongue. Seek urgent care for sudden weakness or confusion.
