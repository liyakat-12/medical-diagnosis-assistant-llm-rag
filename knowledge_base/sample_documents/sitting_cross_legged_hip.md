# Hip Pain After Sitting Cross-Legged

Sitting cross-legged stretches the outer hip and can ache after a floor meal or a long pooja. Getting up is often stiff.

Change sides, use a cushion, and stand between sits. Stretch the hip gently after.

See a doctor if the hip stays painful. Seek urgent care if you cannot walk.
