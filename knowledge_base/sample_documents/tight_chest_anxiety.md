# Tight Chest With a Panic Wave

A sudden tight chest, racing heart, tingling fingers, and fear during a panic wave can feel like a medical emergency. New crushing pain still needs emergency assessment because heart pain and panic can overlap.

If you have already been assessed and told this is panic, slower breathing and grounding can help a wave pass. Do not ignore a first episode of chest pain.

Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, pain into the arm or jaw, or faintness. See a doctor for repeated panic waves so other causes can be considered.
