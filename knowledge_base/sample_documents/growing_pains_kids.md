# Growing Pains in Children

Growing pains are an ache in both legs, often in the evening or at night, in otherwise well school-age children. The child is usually fine by morning and can run about.

Gentle massage, a warm cloth, and reassurance help. Pain in one joint, a limp, or fever is not typical growing pain.

See a doctor for one-sided pain, a limp, swelling, or fever. Seek urgent care if a child cannot take weight.
