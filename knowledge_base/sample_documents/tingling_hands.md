# Tingling in the Hands

Pins and needles in the fingers can come from a bent wrist in sleep, leaning on an elbow, or pressure on a nerve in the neck. Carpal tunnel often affects the thumb, index, and middle fingers and may wake you at night.

Change hand position, shake the hands out, and avoid prolonged pressure on the wrist. A night splint can help if a clinician thinks carpal tunnel is likely.

See a doctor if tingling lasts, is only on one side, or comes with weakness. Seek emergency care for sudden face, arm, or speech changes, which can be a stroke warning.
