# Light-Headed in a Hot Crowded Room

Feeling woozy, sweaty, and sick in a hot packed room is a common faint warning. Blood pools in the legs and you may yawn or have tunnel vision.

Sit or lie down at once, loosen tight clothes, and sip water. Step outside for cooler air. Do not stay standing in the crowd if you feel faint.

See a doctor if fainting happens more than once. Seek emergency care if you hit your head, have chest pain, or do not recover quickly.
