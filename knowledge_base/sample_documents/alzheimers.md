# Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's is the most common dementia. Short-term memory, finding words, and managing daily tasks slowly decline.

A clinician rules out treatable causes and can discuss medicines and support. It is not normal forgetfulness of names only.

See a doctor for memory loss that affects work or safety. Sudden confusion over hours is delirium and needs urgent review.
