# Bruised Eye After a Bump

A bruise around the eye after a knock can look dramatic. Vision should stay normal. Double vision, blood on the coloured part of the eye, or a blow with loss of consciousness needs urgent care.

A cold pack wrapped in cloth and sitting upright reduce swelling. Do not press on the eyeball.

See a clinician the same day for any vision change. Seek emergency care for double vision, vomiting after a head bump, or a pupil that looks odd.
