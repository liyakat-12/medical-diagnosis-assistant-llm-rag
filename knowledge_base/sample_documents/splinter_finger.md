# Splinter in a Finger

A small wood or thorn splinter that you can see and grasp can often be removed at home. Dirt left under the skin can infect.

Wash, use clean tweezers, and cover with a plaster. Check tetanus status if the splinter was dirty.

See a clinician if you cannot get the splinter out, it is under a nail, or redness spreads. Seek urgent care for a red streak with fever.
