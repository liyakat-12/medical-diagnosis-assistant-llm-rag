# Itchy Rash in the Groin

A red itchy rash in the groin crease, worse in heat and sweat, is often a fungal infection. The skin at the edge can look scaly.

Keep the area dry, wear loose cotton, and use an antifungal cream a pharmacist can help you choose if it is suitable. Do not share towels.

See a doctor if the rash spreads, is very sore, or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading red hot area with fever.
