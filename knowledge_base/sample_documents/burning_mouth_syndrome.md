# Burning Mouth Syndrome

This is a persistent scalding or tingling feeling on the tongue, lips, or palate with no visible sore. Taste may change and the mouth may feel dry, often worsening through the day.

A clinician checks for causes such as low iron or B12, thyroid problems, diabetes, oral thrush, or dry mouth from medicines. Treating the cause usually settles the burning.

See a doctor or dentist if the burning lasts more than two weeks, especially with weight loss, a visible ulcer, or numbness.
