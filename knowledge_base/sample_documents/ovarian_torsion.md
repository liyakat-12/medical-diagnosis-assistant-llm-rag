# Ovarian Torsion

Ovarian torsion is when an ovary twists on its blood supply. It causes sudden severe one-sided lower abdominal pain, often with nausea and vomiting, and the pain may come in waves.

The ovary can be permanently lost within hours without surgery to untwist it, so this cannot wait for a routine appointment.

Call emergency services or go to hospital for sudden severe one-sided pelvic pain with vomiting, especially with a known ovarian cyst.
