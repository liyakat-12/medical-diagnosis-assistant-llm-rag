# Stevens-Johnson Syndrome

SJS is a rare severe reaction, often to a new medicine. The skin blisters, the mouth and eyes become raw, and fever starts.

Stop the suspected drug if a clinician agrees it is safe to stop, and go to hospital. This can progress to a burn-like emergency.

Seek emergency care for blistering rash with sore mouth or eyes after starting a new medicine.
