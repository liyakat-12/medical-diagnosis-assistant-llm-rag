# Epididymitis

Epididymitis is infection or inflammation of the coiled tube behind the testicle. Pain and swelling build over a day or two on one side, with redness, warmth, fever, and sometimes burning urine.

It is treated with a course of antibiotics, rest, scrotal support, ice, and anti-inflammatory pain relief. Full recovery can take a few weeks.

See a doctor the same day. Seek emergency care if pain started suddenly and severely over minutes, which suggests testicular torsion instead.
