# Painful Cold Fingers While Typing

Fingers that blanch and ache in a cold office can be Raynaud's. Typing in a cold room makes it worse. Numbness from a bent wrist is a different tingling pattern.

Fingerless gloves, a warmer room, and moving the hands help. Do not smoke.

See a doctor if colour changes are new or sores appear on fingertips. Seek urgent care for a finger that stays white and painful.
