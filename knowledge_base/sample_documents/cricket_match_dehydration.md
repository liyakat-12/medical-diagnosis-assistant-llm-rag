# Feeling Wiped After a Hot Match

A long game in the sun can leave you thirsty, dizzy, and aching. Heat exhaustion is more than ordinary tiredness.

Sit in shade, sip water or an oral rehydration drink, and cool the skin. Do not keep playing if you feel faint.

See a doctor if you cannot keep fluids down. Seek emergency care for confusion, collapsing, or very hot dry skin.
