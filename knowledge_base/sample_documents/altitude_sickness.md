# Altitude Sickness

Altitude sickness starts six to twelve hours after going above about 2,500 metres. It causes headache, nausea, tiredness, dizziness, and poor sleep, feeling rather like a hangover.

Stop ascending, rest, drink fluids, avoid alcohol, and take simple pain relief. Symptoms usually settle in one to two days as the body adjusts. Descend if they worsen.

Descend immediately and seek emergency care for confusion, unsteady walking, breathlessness at rest, or a cough with frothy sputum, which indicate dangerous brain or lung swelling.
