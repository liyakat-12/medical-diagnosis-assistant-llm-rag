# Blepharitis (Eyelid Inflammation)

Blepharitis is inflammation of the eyelid edges. Lids feel gritty and itchy, look red and crusty in the morning, and lashes may stick together.

Warm compresses for five minutes then gentle lid cleaning with diluted baby shampoo or a lid wipe, done daily, controls most cases. It tends to come back, so keep the routine going.

See an eye clinician if the eye becomes painful, vision blurs, or there is thick pus rather than simple crusting.
