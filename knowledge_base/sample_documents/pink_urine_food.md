# Pink Urine After Beetroot

Beetroot, some berries, and certain medicines can colour urine pink or red. True blood in urine can look similar and must not be ignored, especially without a food explanation or if it happens again.

Think about recent foods. If you are not sure it was food, treat it as blood until a clinician checks.

See a doctor for red or pink urine that is not clearly from food. Seek urgent care for blood with severe pain, clots, or no urine.
