# Hip Pain After Carrying a Child on One Side

Parking a toddler on one hip day after day can ache that hip and the low back. Switching sides helps.

Use a carrier that spreads the load, switch hips, and stretch the outer hip after a long carry.

See a doctor if the hip stays painful. Seek urgent care if you cannot walk after a fall.
