# Febrile Seizure

A febrile seizure is a fit triggered by a rapid rise in temperature in a child aged six months to five years. The child stiffens, jerks, may wet themselves, and is drowsy afterwards.

During a seizure, lay the child on their side, clear the area, and do not put anything in the mouth. Time the seizure. Most stop within a couple of minutes and cause no lasting harm.

Call emergency services if it is the first seizure, lasts more than five minutes, repeats, or the child does not wake up properly afterwards.
