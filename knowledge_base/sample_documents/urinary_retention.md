# Acute Urinary Retention

Acute retention means you suddenly cannot pass urine and the bladder is painfully full. It is more common in older men with prostate enlargement.

A catheter in hospital relieves it. The cause is then treated. Waiting at home can damage the kidneys.

Go to emergency care if you cannot pass urine and the lower belly is painful and swollen.
