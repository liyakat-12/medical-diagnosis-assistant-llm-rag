# Painful Mouth Ulcer

A small round sore on the inner lip or cheek that hurts with salty or acidic food is a common mouth ulcer. Most heal within one to two weeks. A single ulcer after a bite is common.

Avoid sharp crisps and acidic drinks, use a soft toothbrush, and try a pharmacy gel if suitable. Do not touch the ulcer often.

See a dentist or doctor if an ulcer lasts more than three weeks, you have many ulcers, or you lose weight. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading mouth swelling.
