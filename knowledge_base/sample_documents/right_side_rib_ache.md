# Ache Under the Right Ribs

Discomfort under the right ribs after a fatty meal can be gas, indigestion, or a gallbladder pattern. Yellow eyes or fever with this pain needs urgent review.

Eat smaller, less fatty meals and avoid lying flat right after food.

See a doctor for repeated right-upper pain. Seek emergency care for pain with fever, yellow skin, or vomiting that will not stop.
