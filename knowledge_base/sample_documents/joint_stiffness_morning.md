# Morning Joint Stiffness

Stiff joints that loosen after you move around can be early osteoarthritis. Stiffness that lasts more than 30 to 60 minutes and affects many joints, especially the hands, is more typical of inflammatory arthritis and needs a medical review.

Warmth, gentle movement before getting out of bed, and staying active through the day help mechanical stiffness. Do not ignore swelling of several joints.

See a doctor if morning stiffness lasts more than half an hour most days, or joints look swollen. Seek urgent care for a single hot joint with fever.
