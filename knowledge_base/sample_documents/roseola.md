# Roseola

Roseola affects babies and toddlers. There is a sudden high fever for three to five days while the child is otherwise fairly well, then as the fever breaks a pink rash appears on the trunk and spreads.

The rash does not itch and fades within a couple of days. Care is fluids, light clothing, and age-appropriate fever relief.

See a doctor for a febrile seizure, a rash that does not fade when pressed, drowsiness, a stiff neck, or fever lasting more than five days.
