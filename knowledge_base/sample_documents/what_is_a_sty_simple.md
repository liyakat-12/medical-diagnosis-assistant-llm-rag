# What a Stye Is

A stye is a small painful lump on the eyelid, often at the lash line. It looks like a pimple and can make the lid swell.

Use a clean warm cloth several times a day. Do not squeeze it. Leave contact lenses out.

See a doctor if the stye lasts more than two weeks or vision drops. Seek urgent care for a very swollen red eyelid with fever.
