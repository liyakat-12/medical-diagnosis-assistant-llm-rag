# Pressure Behind the Eyes

A heavy pressure behind the eyes can be a tension headache, sinus congestion, or eye strain. It is usually not a sign of high pressure in the brain on its own. Pain with a red eye and seeing halos needs urgent care.

Rest the eyes, drink water, and treat a blocked nose with saline if a cold is present. A short walk can help a tension-type pressure.

See a doctor for new pressure headaches that keep returning. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye, sudden vision loss, or a thunderclap headache.
