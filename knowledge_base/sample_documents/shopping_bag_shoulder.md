# Shoulder Pain After Carrying Shopping

A heavy bag on one shoulder can strain the neck and the rotator cuff. The ache often starts that evening.

Split the load, use a backpack or a trolley, and ice after a long shop.

See a doctor if the arm will not lift after a few days. Seek emergency care for shoulder pain with chest pressure.
