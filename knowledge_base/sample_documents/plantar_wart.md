# Plantar Wart

A plantar wart is a rough, grainy lump on the sole caused by a virus. It may have tiny black dots and hurt when you stand.

Salicylic acid pads from a pharmacy, used for weeks, often clear it. Do not share socks. Cushion the area while it heals.

See a doctor if the lump bleeds, grows fast, or you are not sure it is a wart, especially if you have diabetes.
