# Flashes of Light in Vision

Brief flashes in the side vision can mean the jelly in the eye is tugging on the retina. A sudden increase in flashes with new floaters needs same-day eye care. Migraine aura flashes usually last minutes and then a headache may follow.

Note whether flashes are in one eye, how long they last, and whether a curtain appears. Do not wait a week if flashes are new and many.

See an eye clinic the same day for new flashes with floaters. Seek emergency care for a dark curtain over vision.
