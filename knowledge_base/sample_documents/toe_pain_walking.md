# Toe Pain When Walking

A sore big toe when you push off can be a stiff toe joint, a bunion, or a stubbed toe. Smaller toes hurt in tight shoes or with a claw shape.

Wear wider shoes, use a toe protector if a shoe rubs, and rest from running until the ache eases.

See a doctor for a hot, shiny, very painful big toe, which can be gout. Seek urgent care for a toe that looks black after injury or if you have diabetes and a toe wound.
