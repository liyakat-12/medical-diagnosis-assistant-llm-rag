# Knee Pain After Using a Squat Toilet

A deep squat can hurt knees that are already stiff or worn. Getting up is often the worst part.

Hold a stable support when you rise, and avoid bouncing in the squat. A raised seat helps some people at home.

See a doctor if squat pain lasts or the knee locks. Seek urgent care if you cannot straighten the knee.
