# Soreness After the First Gym Week

New exercises often make muscles sore one or two days later. The muscles feel stiff but you can still walk. A snap during a lift is not delayed soreness.

Ease the next session, walk, stretch gently, and sleep. Soreness should fade in a few days.

See a doctor if urine turns dark after a crushing workout or swelling is extreme. Seek urgent care for a joint that will not move after a pop.
