# Brain-Freeze Headache

A sudden sharp pain in the forehead after ice cream or an iced drink is a cold-stimulus headache. It fades within a minute or two. It is common and not dangerous.

Slow the next cold mouthful or press the tongue to the roof of the mouth. You do not need tests for a typical brain freeze.

See a doctor if headaches after cold drinks last or are different from a brief freeze. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache that does not fade.
