# Loud Rumbling Tummy

Loud gurgles are gas and fluid moving through the gut. They are louder when you are hungry or have just eaten. Painful rumbling with vomiting and no stool is more serious.

Eat regular meals and notice trigger foods. A rumbling tummy without pain is usually harmless.

See a doctor if rumbling comes with weight loss or lasting pain. Seek urgent care for vomiting and a swollen tummy with no wind.
