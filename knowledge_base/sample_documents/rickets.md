# Rickets

Rickets is softening of growing bones from a lack of vitamin D or calcium. Signs are bowed legs, a widened wrist, delayed walking, bone pain, poor growth, and teeth that come through late.

Treatment is vitamin D and calcium supplements prescribed by a clinician, along with safe sunlight exposure and fortified foods. Bones usually straighten as the child grows.

See a doctor for bowed legs, bone pain, or delayed walking. Seek urgent care for muscle twitching or a seizure, which can follow very low calcium.
