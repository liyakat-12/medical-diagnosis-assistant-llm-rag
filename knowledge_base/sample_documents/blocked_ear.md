# Blocked Ear from Wax

Earwax can block the canal and cause fullness, muffled hearing, or ringing. Cotton buds often push wax deeper.

Pharmacy wax-softening drops for a few days help many people. Do not syringe if you have a hole in the eardrum or ear discharge.

See a clinician if hearing drops suddenly, you have pain, dizziness, or drops do not help.
