# Sore Cracked Nipples While Feeding

A shallow latch can crack the nipple and make feeding very painful. A fever and a red wedge on the breast can be mastitis.

Ask a feeding helper to check the latch, and start on the less sore side. Keep the skin clean and aired.

See a midwife or doctor for cracks that bleed or a red painful breast. Seek urgent care for fever and spreading redness.
