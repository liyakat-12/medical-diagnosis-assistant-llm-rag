# Pain Behind the Eyes

A dull ache behind the eyes after screens or a stuffy nose is common. Sharp pain with a red eye, light hurting the eye, or vision change needs a check.

Use the 20-20-20 eye rest, blink more, and treat a blocked nose with saline if suitable.

See a doctor if eye-socket pain lasts or vision blurs. Seek urgent care for sudden vision loss, a red painful eye, or flashing lights.
