# Sore Throat From Dry Air

A scratchy throat in heated or air-conditioned rooms, without fever, can be dryness. Mouth breathing at night makes it worse first thing.

Sip water, use a humidifier if you can, and breathe through the nose if it is clear. Honey can soothe if you are over one.

See a doctor if the throat is still sore after a week or you have fever. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
