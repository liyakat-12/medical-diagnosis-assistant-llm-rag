# Nausea After a Fatty Meal

Feeling sick after a greasy takeaway can be indigestion, a sensitive gallbladder, or simply an oversized meal. Pain under the right ribs after fatty food needs a medical review if it keeps happening.

Eat a smaller, plainer next meal, sip water, and walk gently. Avoid lying flat.

See a doctor for repeated pain after fatty meals. Seek emergency care for severe right-upper pain with fever or yellow skin.
