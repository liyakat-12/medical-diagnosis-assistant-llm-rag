# Aching Muscles After Sitting a Long Time

Hips, back, and shoulders often ache after a long sit because muscles stay shortened and then suddenly stretch when you stand. This is a common everyday complaint, not automatically arthritis.

Stand every 30 to 45 minutes, walk a short loop, and change position before pain starts. A firmer chair and both feet on the floor reduce the ache.

See a doctor if sitting pain is paired with unexplained weight loss, fever, or night sweats. Seek emergency care for back pain with weak legs or loss of bladder control.
