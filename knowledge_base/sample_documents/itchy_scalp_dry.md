# Itchy Dry Scalp

An itchy scalp with white flakes is often dandruff or a dry scalp. The flakes are loose, not thick silvery plaques stuck to the skin, which can be psoriasis.

Wash regularly with an anti-dandruff shampoo if a pharmacist agrees, and rinse well. Do not scratch until the scalp bleeds.

See a doctor if the scalp is very red, sore, or hair is falling in patches. Seek urgent care for a spreading painful swelling on the scalp with fever.
