# Prickly Heat on the Neck

Tiny itchy bumps on the neck and chest after sweating are often heat rash. A vest or backpack strap can trap sweat.

Cool the skin, wear loose cotton, and avoid thick creams that block sweat.

See a doctor if bumps become pus-filled or you have a fever. Seek urgent care for rapidly spreading painful redness.
