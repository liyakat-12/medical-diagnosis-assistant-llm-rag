# Calf Strain After a Sprint

A sudden grab in the calf during a sprint or push-off is often a muscle strain. You may feel as if you were kicked. Walking on tiptoe is painful.

Rest from sprinting, ice, and gentle movement as pain allows. Crutches for a few days help some people. Do not stretch hard into a fresh tear.

See a doctor if you cannot walk or bruising is extensive. Seek urgent care for a swollen, hot calf without a remembered strain, which may be a clot.
