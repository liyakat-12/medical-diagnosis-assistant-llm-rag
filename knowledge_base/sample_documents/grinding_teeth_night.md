# Waking With a Sore Jaw After Grinding

A tired jaw, worn-feeling teeth, and a temple headache on waking can mean you grind or clench in sleep. Stress and an uneven bite can play a part. A partner may hear grinding.

A dentist can advise on a night guard. Daytime, rest the teeth slightly apart and cut evening caffeine if it makes clenching worse.

See a dentist if teeth look worn or the jaw locks. Seek urgent care for sudden facial weakness.
