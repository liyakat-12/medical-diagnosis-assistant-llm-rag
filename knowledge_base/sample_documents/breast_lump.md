# Breast Lump

A breast lump is any new thickening or mass in the breast or armpit. Most lumps are not cancer, but features such as a hard fixed lump, skin dimpling, nipple turning inwards, or bloody discharge need prompt checking.

Assessment usually involves an examination plus ultrasound or mammogram, and sometimes a needle sample. Knowing what is normal for you makes change easier to notice.

See a doctor for any new breast lump that lasts beyond one menstrual cycle, and promptly for skin or nipple changes at any age.
