# Guillain-Barre Syndrome

Guillain-Barre syndrome causes rapidly progressing weakness that starts in the feet and climbs upwards over days, often with tingling and loss of reflexes. It frequently follows a diarrhoeal or respiratory infection.

Weakness can reach the breathing muscles, so hospital monitoring is essential. Treatment with immunoglobulin or plasma exchange speeds recovery.

Call emergency services for weakness spreading up both legs over hours to days, especially with breathing or swallowing difficulty.
