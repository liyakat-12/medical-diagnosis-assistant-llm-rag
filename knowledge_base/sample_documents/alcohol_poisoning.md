# Alcohol Poisoning

Alcohol poisoning is a medical emergency after a large amount of alcohol. Vomiting, slow breathing, cold skin, and unresponsiveness occur.

Call emergency services. Roll the person on their side. Do not leave them to 'sleep it off' or give more alcohol or coffee.

This can be fatal. Stay until help arrives.
