# Physical Feelings of Mild Anxiety

Anxiety can cause a racing heart, a tight chest, shaky hands, a dry mouth, and a need to use the toilet. These feelings can mimic some medical problems, so new or severe chest pain still needs a medical check.

Slow breathing, naming five things you can see, and reducing extra caffeine can settle a mild wave. Regular sleep and daylight help the next day.

See a doctor to rule out other causes if symptoms are new, and for talking therapies if worry is daily. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or fainting.
