# Absent Periods (Amenorrhoea)

Amenorrhoea means periods have not started by age fifteen, or have stopped for three months or more in someone who was having them. It may come with headaches, vision change, milky nipple discharge, or extra hair growth.

Pregnancy is checked first. Other causes include PCOS, thyroid problems, very low body weight, heavy exercise, stress, and pituitary conditions.

See a doctor if periods stop for three months or more, as long-term absence can affect bone strength and fertility.
