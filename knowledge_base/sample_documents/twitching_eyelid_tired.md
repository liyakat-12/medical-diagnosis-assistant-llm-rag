# Twitching Eyelid When Tired

A flickering eyelid after screens, coffee, or poor sleep is a common harmless twitch. It can last hours or come and go for days. The whole face twitching is different.

Rest the eyes, cut extra caffeine, and get sleep. The twitch usually stops on its own.

See a doctor if twitching spreads, the eyelid droops, or it lasts many weeks. Seek urgent care for facial droop or speech change.
