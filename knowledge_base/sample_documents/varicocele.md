# Varicocele

A varicocele is a group of enlarged veins in the scrotum, usually on the left. It feels like a bag of worms, causes a dull dragging ache that worsens after standing all day, and may affect fertility.

Supportive underwear and simple pain relief help mild cases. A small operation or embolisation is offered for ongoing pain or fertility problems.

See a doctor for scrotal swelling or ache. Seek emergency care for sudden severe testicular pain, which may be torsion rather than a varicocele.
