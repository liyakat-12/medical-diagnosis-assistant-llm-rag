# Hepatitis C

Hepatitis C is a blood-borne virus that can silently scar the liver for years. Tiredness and jaundice may appear late.

A blood test finds it. Modern antiviral tablets can cure most people when a clinician prescribes them.

Ask for a test if you have risk factors. Seek urgent care for swelling of the belly, confusion, or bleeding.
