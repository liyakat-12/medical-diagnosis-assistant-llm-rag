# Runny Nose From Spicy Food

Hot spice can make the nose run and the face flush within minutes. This is a nerve reflex, not a cold, and it stops after the meal.

Sip water, and choose a milder dish if it bothers you. Tissues are enough; you do not need a cold medicine.

See a doctor if a runny nose is daily without spice. Seek emergency care for lip swelling or trouble breathing after food.
