# Pain in the Palm When Gripping

The palm can ache after carrying bags, wringing cloths, or using tools. A click into a bent finger can be a trigger finger.

Rest the grip, ice after heavy use, and loosen handles with padding.

See a doctor if the finger locks or the palm stays numb. Seek urgent care after a deep cut or a hand that is cold and pale.
