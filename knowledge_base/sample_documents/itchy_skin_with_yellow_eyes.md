# Itchy Skin With Yellow Eyes

Itching all over with yellow eyes can go with jaundice. Ordinary dry winter itch does not turn the eyes yellow.

Keep the skin cool and moisturised, and see a clinician rather than using strong steroid creams on a guess.

See a doctor promptly for itch plus yellow eyes. Seek emergency care for confusion or vomiting blood.
