# Ache on the Left Side of the Tummy

Pain on the left can be wind, constipation in the descending colon, a kidney stone, or, in women, an ovary. Location plus stool, urine, and period symptoms matter more than the side alone.

Note whether pain relates to meals or stool. Heat can ease simple cramps. Do not take leftover strong pain tablets.

See a doctor for pain lasting more than a few days. Seek emergency care for sudden severe pain, fainting, or a rigid tummy.
