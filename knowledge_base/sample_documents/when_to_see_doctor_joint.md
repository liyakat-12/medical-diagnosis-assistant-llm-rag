# When Joint Pain Needs a Check

A single joint that is sore after extra use can settle with rest. Several swollen joints, morning stiffness lasting more than half an hour, or a hot joint with fever needs a medical review.

Keep a note of which joints, how long stiffness lasts, and any rash or fever.

See a doctor for lasting swelling or long morning stiffness. Seek urgent care for a single hot joint with fever.
