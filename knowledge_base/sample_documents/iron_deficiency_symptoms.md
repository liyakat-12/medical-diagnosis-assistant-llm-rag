# Possible Iron Deficiency Symptoms

Tiredness, looking paler than usual, breathlessness on stairs, and restless legs can happen when iron stores are low. Heavy periods and a low-iron diet are common reasons. Symptoms overlap with many other conditions, so a blood test is needed.

Do not start high-dose iron tablets on a guess, because too much iron can be harmful. A clinician can advise on diet and whether tablets are needed.

See a doctor for lasting tiredness, heavy periods, or black stools. Seek urgent care for chest pain or fainting with severe paleness.
