# Huntington Disease

Huntington disease is an inherited condition causing involuntary jerky movements, difficulty with balance and speech, mood and personality changes, and gradual memory problems, usually starting between thirty and fifty.

There is no cure. Medicines control movements and mood, and genetic counselling is offered to families because each child has a fifty percent chance of inheriting it.

See a neurologist for unexplained involuntary movements with mood change, especially with a family history.
