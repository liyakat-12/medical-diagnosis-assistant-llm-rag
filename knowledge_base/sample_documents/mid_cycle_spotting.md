# Spotting Between Periods

A little spotting around ovulation or after starting a new contraceptive can happen. Bleeding between periods that is new, heavy, or happens after sex needs a clinical check. Do not assume it is harmless.

Note dates, how much blood, and any pain. Use a pad rather than guessing.

See a doctor for new bleeding between periods. Seek urgent care for soaking pads with dizziness, or sudden severe pelvic pain.
