# Simple Rest and Fluids for a Mild Illness

For a mild cold or a short tummy bug, rest and fluids are the main treatment. This does not replace emergency care or antibiotics when a clinician says they are needed.

Drink water or an oral rehydration drink, sleep, and eat as you can. Stay home while feverish if you can.

See a doctor if you are not improving in the usual time. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, confusion, no urine, or a non-blanching rash.
