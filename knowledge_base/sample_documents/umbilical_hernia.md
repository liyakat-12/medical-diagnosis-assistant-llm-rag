# Umbilical Hernia

An umbilical hernia is a soft bulge at the navel where tissue pushes through a weak spot. It often appears when coughing or straining and may ache after standing a long time.

Most hernias in babies close by themselves. In adults a surgeon repairs the gap, especially if the bulge grows or becomes uncomfortable.

See a doctor to confirm the diagnosis. Seek emergency care if the bulge becomes hard, very painful, or discoloured, or if you start vomiting.
