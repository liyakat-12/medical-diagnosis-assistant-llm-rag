# Diphtheria

Diphtheria causes sore throat, mild fever, swollen neck glands giving a bull-neck appearance, and a thick grey membrane across the throat that can block breathing.

It needs urgent hospital treatment with antitoxin and antibiotics, plus isolation. Vaccination prevents it, and close contacts also need protection.

Call emergency services for a severe sore throat with a grey throat coating, noisy breathing, or neck swelling, especially if vaccination is incomplete.
