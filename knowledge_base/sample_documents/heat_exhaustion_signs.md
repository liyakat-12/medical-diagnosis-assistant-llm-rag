# Feeling Faint in the Heat

Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, and a headache in hot weather can be heat exhaustion. The skin may feel cool and clammy. It can become heatstroke if the person becomes confused or stops sweating.

Move to a cool place, lie down, loosen clothes, and sip water. Cool the skin with a damp cloth. Rest the rest of the day.

Seek emergency care if the person becomes confused, has a very hot dry skin, or faints and does not recover quickly.
