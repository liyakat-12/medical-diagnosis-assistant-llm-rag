# Heavy Pelvic Pressure

A dragging or heavy feeling in the pelvis can follow childbirth, constipation, or a prolapse. Period pain and urine infections can feel similar. A new bulge in the vagina needs a check.

Avoid heavy straining, treat constipation, and a clinician can teach pelvic-floor exercises.

See a doctor for a lasting heavy feeling or a bulge. Seek urgent care for sudden severe pelvic pain and fainting.
