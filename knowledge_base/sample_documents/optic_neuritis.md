# Optic Neuritis

Optic neuritis is inflammation of the nerve from the eye to the brain. Vision in one eye blurs or dims over hours to days, colours look washed out, and moving the eye hurts.

It often improves on its own, but a clinician may give steroids to speed recovery. It can be an early sign of multiple sclerosis, so brain imaging is often arranged.

Seek same-day eye assessment for vision loss in one eye with pain on eye movement.
