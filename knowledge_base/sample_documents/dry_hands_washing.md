# Dry Cracked Hands From Washing

Sore, cracked hands after frequent washing or sanitiser are irritant dryness. The skin barrier is stripped. Itch and small splits around the fingers are common.

Use lukewarm water, pat dry, and put on a plain moisturiser every time you wash. Cotton gloves over cream at night can help.

See a doctor if the hands are very cracked, weep, or a rash spreads. Seek urgent care for rapidly spreading redness and fever.
