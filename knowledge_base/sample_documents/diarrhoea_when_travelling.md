# Diarrhoea When Travelling

Loose stools on a trip are often from food or water with unfamiliar germs. Cramps and urgency are common. Most mild cases last a few days.

Sip oral rehydration drinks, eat small plain meals, and wash hands. Avoid preparing food for others. Be cautious with anti-diarrhoea tablets if you have fever or blood.

See a doctor if diarrhoea lasts more than a few days, you have blood, or you have a high fever. Seek urgent care for dizziness and no urine.
