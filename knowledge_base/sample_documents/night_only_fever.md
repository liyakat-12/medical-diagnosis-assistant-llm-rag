# Fever That Comes Only at Night

A fever that appears in the evening can still be a simple virus. Night sweats with a long cough, weight loss, or weeks of fever need a medical review.

Take the temperature and write the times down. Rest and fluids help a short viral illness.

See a doctor for night fevers lasting more than a few days. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck, a non-blanching rash, or severe breathlessness.
