# Pain in the Big Toe Joint

A red, hot, very tender big-toe joint that starts overnight can be gout. A slower ache after tight shoes can be a bunion or wear-and-tear.

Rest the toe, raise the foot, and avoid a tight toe box. Do not start strong painkillers that a clinician has not advised.

See a doctor for a sudden hot big toe or a growing bunion. Seek urgent care if you cannot walk or you have fever with a hot joint.
