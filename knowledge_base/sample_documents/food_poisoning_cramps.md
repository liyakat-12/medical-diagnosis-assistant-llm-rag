# Cramps a Few Hours After a Meal

Cramps, nausea, or diarrhoea a few hours after a picnic or buffet can be foodborne illness. Others who ate the same food may also be ill. Most mild cases pass with fluids.

Sip rehydration drinks and rest. Do not prepare food for others until you have had no diarrhoea for 48 hours.

See a doctor for bloody diarrhoea, a high fever, or symptoms lasting more than three days. Seek urgent care for severe dehydration.
