# Sore Muscles After Gardening

Aching shoulders, back, and knees the day after digging or weeding is delayed muscle soreness from unfamiliar work. It eases over a few days. A sudden snap during a lift is a strain instead.

Gentle walking, heat, and lighter tasks the next day help. Kneel on a pad and change position often next time.

See a doctor if soreness lasts more than a week or a joint swells. Seek urgent care for back pain with numb buttocks after lifting.
