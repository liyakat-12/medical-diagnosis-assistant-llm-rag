# Aching Top of the Shoulders

Tight bands on top of the shoulders after desk work or stress are overworked upper trapezius muscles. A dull headache can start from the same area.

Drop the shoulders, raise the screen, and take walking breaks. Heat and gentle shoulder rolls help.

See a doctor if the ache lasts more than a few weeks or an arm becomes weak. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever and a severe headache.
