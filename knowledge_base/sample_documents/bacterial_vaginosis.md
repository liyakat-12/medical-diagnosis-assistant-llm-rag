# Bacterial Vaginosis

Bacterial vaginosis is an imbalance of vaginal bacteria. Discharge may be thin and grey with a fishy smell, especially after sex.

A clinician can confirm it. Prescribed antibiotic gel or tablets treat it. Douching makes it more likely to return.

See a clinic rather than guessing, because other infections can look similar.
