# Rectal Prolapse

Rectal prolapse is when part of the bowel slides out through the back passage, usually after straining. It feels like a lump coming out, with mucus leakage, bleeding, and poor bowel control.

Treating constipation and doing pelvic floor exercises help early cases. Larger or repeated prolapse usually needs surgical repair.

See a doctor for any tissue coming out of the back passage. Seek emergency care if it cannot be pushed back, turns dark, or becomes very painful.
