# Stiff Neck From Cold Air Conditioning

A cold vent on one side of the neck can tighten the muscles overnight or at a desk. This is a common everyday stiffness, not automatically an infection.

Turn the vent, cover the neck, and use gentle heat. Keep the neck moving in a small range.

See a doctor if stiffness lasts more than a week. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck with fever or a severe headache.
