# Stress Urinary Incontinence

Stress incontinence is leaking urine when coughing, sneezing, laughing, lifting, or exercising. It happens when the pelvic floor muscles are weakened, often after childbirth or with age.

Pelvic floor exercises done correctly for at least three months improve or cure most cases. Weight loss, treating constipation, and reducing caffeine also help.

See a doctor or a continence physiotherapist if leaking affects daily life, or if there is also pain, blood in urine, or sudden onset.
