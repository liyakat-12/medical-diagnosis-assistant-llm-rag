# Dry Skin in Cold Weather

Tight, flaky skin on the shins and hands is common in winter heating and cold wind. The skin barrier loses water. Itch follows dryness.

Shorter lukewarm showers, a plain moisturiser after washing, and cotton layers help. Avoid very hot water and strong soap.

See a doctor if the skin cracks and bleeds, or a rash spreads. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful red area.
