# Easing a Blocked Nose at Home

Saline drops or rinses, steam from a shower, and extra pillows at night can open a stuffy nose from a cold. This is everyday care, not a cure for every cause of blockage.

Do not use decongestant sprays longer than a few days. Keep rooms from becoming very dry if you can.

See a doctor for blockage lasting more than three weeks or only one side. Seek urgent care for a blocked nose with facial swelling and fever.
