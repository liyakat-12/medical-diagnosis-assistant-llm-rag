# Creepy Legs When You Try to Sleep

An urge to move the legs with a crawling feeling at rest, worse in the evening, is restless legs. Walking or stretching briefly eases it. Low iron can play a part in some people.

A regular sleep time, less evening caffeine, and stretching before bed help some people. Do not start prescription medicines without a clinician.

See a doctor if restless legs wreck sleep. A blood test for iron may be useful. Seek urgent care for a swollen painful calf, which is not restless legs.
