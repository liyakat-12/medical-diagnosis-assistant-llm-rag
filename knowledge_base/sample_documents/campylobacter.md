# Campylobacter Infection

Campylobacter is a common cause of food poisoning from undercooked chicken or untreated water. It causes cramping abdominal pain, fever, and diarrhoea that is sometimes bloody.

Most cases settle within a week with fluids and rest. Antibiotics are used only for severe or prolonged illness.

See a doctor for bloody diarrhoea or symptoms beyond a week. Seek urgent care for new weakness or tingling in the limbs after the infection.
