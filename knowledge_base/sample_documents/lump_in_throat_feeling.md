# Feeling of a Lump in the Throat

A ball or tightness in the throat that comes and goes, especially with stress, is often globus. You can still swallow food. Painful swallowing or food sticking is a different problem.

Sip water, ease throat clearing, and treat reflux habits if you have heartburn. Stress reduction can help globus.

See a doctor if the feeling lasts, you lose weight, or food sticks. Seek urgent care for being unable to swallow saliva or noisy breathing.
