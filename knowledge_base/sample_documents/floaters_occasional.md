# Occasional Specks in Vision

A few drifting specks against a bright sky are common floaters. A sudden shower of new floaters, flashes of light, or a curtain over vision can be a retinal tear and needs same-day care.

Ordinary long-standing floaters often need no treatment. An eye test is still wise if they are new to you.

See an optometrist or eye clinic for new floaters. Seek emergency eye care for a sudden shower of floaters, flashes, or a dark curtain.
