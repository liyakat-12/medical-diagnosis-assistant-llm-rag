# Pain When Turning a Doorknob

A twist such as a doorknob, tap, or key can flare the elbow tendons. The pain is often on the outer elbow and into the forearm.

Use two hands, rest the twist, and ice after a flare. A thicker grip can reduce strain.

See a doctor if you cannot hold a cup. Seek urgent care if the elbow is hot, swollen, and you have fever.
