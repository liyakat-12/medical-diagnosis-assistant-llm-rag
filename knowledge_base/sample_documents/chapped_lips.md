# Chapped Painful Lips

Dry, split lips in wind or after licking them a lot are common. The corners can crack. This is usually not an infection at the start.

Use a plain lip balm, stop licking, and drink water. Avoid flavoured balms that make you lick more.

See a doctor if cracks last many weeks or the lips swell suddenly. Seek urgent care for lip swelling with breathing difficulty.
