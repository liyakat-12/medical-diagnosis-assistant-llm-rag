# Knee Pain After a Squat Workout

Deep or high-volume squats can irritate the kneecap or a tendon. Delayed soreness a day later is common; a twist with a pop is not ordinary soreness.

Reduce depth, ease load, and ice after training. Keep walking as it settles.

See a doctor if the knee swells or gives way. Seek urgent care if you cannot straighten the knee after a pop.
