# Pain Over the Pubic Bone

A low ache over the pubic bone can follow a full bladder, cystitis, or a strain from sport. Pain with burning urine is a typical bladder pattern.

Drink water, do not hold urine for long, and avoid bladder irritants such as extra caffeine if they worsen it.

See a doctor for pubic pain with burning urine or blood in the urine. Seek urgent care for fever and back pain with this ache.
