# Sore Foot After Stepping on a Stone

A sharp step onto a stone can bruise the fat pad or a small bone in the foot. The sore spot hurts when you press it.

Rest, ice, and use a cushioned shoe. Avoid barefoot walking on hard ground for a few days.

See a doctor if you cannot take weight the next day. Seek urgent care for a wound that is deep or still has debris in it.
