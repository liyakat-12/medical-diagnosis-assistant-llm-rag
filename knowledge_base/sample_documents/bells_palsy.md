# Bell Palsy

Bell palsy is sudden weakness of one side of the face from nerve inflammation. The eyelid will not close, the mouth droops, taste changes, and sound may seem loud on that side. The forehead is also affected.

Steroid tablets started within seventy-two hours improve recovery. The eye must be protected with drops and taping at night because it cannot blink.

Any sudden facial droop needs same-day assessment to rule out stroke. Call emergency services if there is also arm weakness, slurred speech, or a normal forehead on the weak side.
