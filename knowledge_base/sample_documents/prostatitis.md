# Prostatitis

Prostatitis is inflammation of the prostate. Pelvic pain, burning urine, and a frequent need to go can occur. Fever suggests infection.

A clinician may test urine and prescribe antibiotics if bacteria are likely. Chronic pelvic pain needs a longer plan.

See a doctor for pelvic pain with urinary symptoms. Seek emergency care if you cannot pass urine or have high fever.
