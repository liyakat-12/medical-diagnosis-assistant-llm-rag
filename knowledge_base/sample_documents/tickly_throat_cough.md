# Tickly Throat Cough

A dry tickle that makes you cough in bursts is common after a virus or with post-nasal drip. There is little or no mucus. Talking and cold air can set it off.

Sip warm drinks, try honey if you are over one, and suck a sugar-free lozenge if it is safe for you. Avoid smoke.

See a doctor if a tickly cough lasts more than three to four weeks. Seek emergency care for breathlessness or coughing blood.
