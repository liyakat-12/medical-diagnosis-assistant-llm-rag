# Poor Growth in Children (Failure to Thrive)

Failure to thrive means a child is not gaining weight or growing as expected. There may be poor appetite, fussy feeding, tiredness, delayed milestones, and crossing downwards through growth chart lines.

Causes include feeding difficulty, not enough calories, food intolerance, chronic infection, and hormone problems. Treatment starts with a feeding and growth assessment.

See a doctor if a child is losing weight, not gaining for three months, or dropping across growth centiles.
