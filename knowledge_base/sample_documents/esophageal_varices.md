# Esophageal Varices

Varices are swollen veins in the food pipe caused by liver scarring. They usually cause no symptoms until they bleed, which produces large amounts of vomited blood or black tarry stools and lightheadedness.

Bleeding varices are life-threatening and need immediate hospital treatment with endoscopy. People with known cirrhosis are often given preventive medicine.

Call emergency services for vomiting blood, black stools, or fainting in anyone with liver disease. Do not wait to see if it settles.
