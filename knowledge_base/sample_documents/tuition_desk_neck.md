# Neck Pain After Long Tuition Hours

Hours bent over notes stiffen the nape and the shoulders. The ache can travel to the temples.

Raise the book, plant both feet, and stand every 30 minutes. Stretch the nape after class.

See a doctor if the arms tingle. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever.
