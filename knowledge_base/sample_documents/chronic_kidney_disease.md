# Chronic Kidney Disease

CKD is a gradual loss of kidney function. Early stages often have no symptoms. Later, people notice swelling, tiredness, and foamy urine.

Blood and urine tests diagnose it. Blood pressure and diabetes control slow damage. Some medicines need dose changes.

See a doctor for swelling, high blood pressure, or if you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for little or no urine.
