# Cushing Syndrome

Cushing syndrome is caused by too much cortisol, often from long-term steroid medicine. Signs are weight gain around the trunk and face, purple stretch marks, thin easily bruised skin, muscle weakness, high blood pressure, and mood change.

Treatment depends on the cause and may mean slowly reducing steroid medicine under supervision, or surgery if a gland tumour is responsible.

See a doctor for this cluster of changes. Never stop long-term steroid tablets suddenly, as that can cause a dangerous crash in blood pressure.
