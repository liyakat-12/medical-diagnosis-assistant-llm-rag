# Deep Buttock Pain After Sitting

A deep ache in one buttock after a long sit can be a tight deep muscle or nerve irritation. Pain may travel toward the thigh. It often eases when you walk.

Stand often, avoid sitting on a hard wallet, and walk. Gentle figure-four stretches help some people if they do not cause sharp pain.

See a doctor if buttock pain lasts or the foot is numb. Seek emergency care for saddle numbness or bladder change.
