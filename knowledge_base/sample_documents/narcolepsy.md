# Narcolepsy

Narcolepsy causes overwhelming daytime sleepiness and sudden sleep attacks. Some people also get brief muscle weakness triggered by laughter, vivid dreams on falling asleep, and inability to move on waking.

A sleep study confirms it. Prescribed stimulant or wake-promoting medicine, planned short naps, and a fixed sleep schedule manage it well.

See a doctor for falling asleep during conversations, meals, or driving. Do not drive until a clinician advises it is safe.
