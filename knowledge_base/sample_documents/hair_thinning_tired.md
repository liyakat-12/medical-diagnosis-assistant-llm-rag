# Thinning Hair and Feeling Tired

More hair in the brush plus tiredness can follow illness, low iron, thyroid problems, or stress. Hair shedding a few months after a fever is common and often regrows. Sudden bald patches are different.

Be gentle with hair and see a clinician for tests if tiredness or shedding lasts. Do not start strong hormone treatments without advice.

See a doctor for lasting thinning with tiredness. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful scalp.
