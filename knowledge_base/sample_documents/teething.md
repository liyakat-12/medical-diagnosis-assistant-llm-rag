# Teething

Teething usually starts around six months. Babies dribble more, chew on things, have sore red gums, flushed cheeks, and are irritable and unsettled at night.

A chilled teething ring, gentle gum rubbing with a clean finger, and age-appropriate paracetamol if needed all help. Keep the chin dry to prevent a dribble rash.

See a doctor for high fever, diarrhoea, or a very unwell baby, as teething does not cause these and another illness may be present.
