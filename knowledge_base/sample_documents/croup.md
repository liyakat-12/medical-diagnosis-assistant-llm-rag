# Croup

Croup is a viral swelling of the voice box in young children. A barking cough and hoarse voice are typical, worse at night.

Sit the child upright, keep them calm, and use cool night air or steam if it helps them settle. A clinician may give steroid medicine.

Seek emergency care if the child struggles to breathe, is drooling, or the breastbone sucks in with each breath.
