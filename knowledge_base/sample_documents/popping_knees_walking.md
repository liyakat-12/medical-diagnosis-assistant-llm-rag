# Knees That Pop When Walking

A painless pop or crack when you stand or walk is often gas in the joint or a tendon flicking. Painful popping after an injury, or popping with swelling, is a different problem.

Stay active if the pop does not hurt. Strengthen the thighs. Do not force the knee to crack.

See a doctor if popping came after a twist and the knee swells or gives way. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight.
