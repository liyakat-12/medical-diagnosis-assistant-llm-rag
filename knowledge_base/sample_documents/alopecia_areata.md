# Alopecia Areata

Alopecia areata causes sudden round smooth bald patches on the scalp or beard. The skin in the patch looks normal, with no scaling or scarring, and nails may develop small pits.

It is an immune condition and hair often regrows within a year. Steroid injections or creams can speed regrowth in some people.

See a dermatologist for rapidly spreading patches, total scalp loss, or loss of eyebrows and lashes.
