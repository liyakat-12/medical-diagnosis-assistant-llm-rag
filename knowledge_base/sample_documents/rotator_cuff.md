# Rotator Cuff Problem

The rotator cuff is a group of shoulder tendons. Pain when lifting the arm, night pain when lying on that side, and weakness are typical.

Physiotherapy is first for many tears and tendinopathy. A scan is used if weakness is sudden after an injury.

See a doctor after a fall if you cannot lift the arm. Seek urgent care for a cold numb arm.
