# Groin Ache From Tight Jeans

A tight waistband can ache the groin and the lower tummy, and can irritate the skin. A sudden sport strain is a different sharp pull.

Wear a looser pair, and wash chafed skin gently. Do not keep squeezing a swollen groin.

See a doctor for a groin lump or pain that lasts. Seek urgent care for a painful bulge that will not reduce.
