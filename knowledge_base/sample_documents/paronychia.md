# Paronychia (Nail Fold Infection)

Paronychia is infection of the skin beside a nail, usually after biting nails, picking cuticles, or frequent water exposure. The area becomes red, swollen, warm, and throbbing, sometimes with visible pus.

Soak the finger in warm salty water three times a day and keep it dry in between. Avoid nail biting. Most early cases settle within a week.

See a doctor if pus builds up and needs draining, if redness spreads up the finger, or if you have diabetes.
