# Snake Bite

A venomous snake bite can cause severe pain, swelling, and later bleeding or paralysis. Any bite from an unknown snake should be treated as serious.

Call emergency services. Keep the person still, immobilise the limb, and remove rings. Do not cut, suck, or apply a tight tourniquet.

Go to hospital even if you feel well at first, because venom effects can be delayed.
