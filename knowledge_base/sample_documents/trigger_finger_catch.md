# Finger That Catches When You Bend It

A finger that sticks and then pops straight can be trigger finger, a tendon catching in its tunnel. It is often worse in the morning. Diabetes makes it more common.

Avoid repeated gripping. A clinician may suggest a splint or other treatment. Do not force the finger if it is locked.

See a doctor if a finger keeps catching. Seek urgent care for a finger that is white, cold, or will not move after an injury.
