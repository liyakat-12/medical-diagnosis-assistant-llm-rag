# Tingling Feet After Sitting

Feet that buzz after sitting cross-legged or on a toilet for a long time are a squashed nerve. The feeling usually fades when you walk. Lasting tingling in both feet is different and needs a check.

Change position, walk around, and avoid sitting on a hard edge. Shake the feet out.

See a doctor if foot tingling lasts or you have diabetes. Seek emergency care for sudden one-sided weakness or a foot that is cold and pale.
