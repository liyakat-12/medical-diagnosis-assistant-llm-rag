# Pain When Lifting an Arm to a Shelf

People say I have shoulder pain when lifting my arm to a cupboard. Pain in an arc as the arm goes out to the side is a common rotator-cuff or impingement pattern. A stiff shoulder that will not turn out can be a frozen shoulder.

Avoid repeated overhead reaching for a few days, use ice after activity, and try gentle pendulum swings. Sleeping with a pillow under the arm can reduce night pain.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm after two weeks. Seek urgent care if the arm is numb or the shoulder looks out of place, and emergency care for chest pain with the shoulder ache.
