# Leg Cramps at Night

People ask why do my legs cramp at night. A sudden tight knot in the calf or foot that wakes you is a common night cramp. Tired muscles, dehydration, pregnancy, and some medicines can play a part. Most night cramps are not dangerous.

Straighten the knee and pull the toes toward you, then walk around. Regular calf stretches before bed, enough fluid in the day, and loose bedding that does not point the toes can reduce attacks.

See a doctor if cramps happen most nights, you have muscle weakness, or swelling in one leg. Seek urgent care for a swollen, hot, painful calf, which may be a clot rather than a cramp.
