# Vitamin C Deficiency (Scurvy)

Scurvy is severe vitamin C deficiency. It causes tiredness, aching legs, swollen bleeding gums, easy bruising, tiny red spots around hair follicles, and wounds that will not heal.

It is seen with very limited diets, alcohol dependence, and eating disorders. It improves within days of vitamin C supplements plus fruit and vegetables.

See a doctor for bleeding gums with bruising and tiredness, especially if the diet has had no fresh fruit or vegetables for months.
