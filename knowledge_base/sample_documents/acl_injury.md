# ACL Injury

An ACL tear typically happens with a sudden change of direction or landing. There is often an audible pop, immediate swelling within a few hours, severe pain, and a feeling that the knee will give way.

Initial care is rest, ice, compression, elevation, and crutches. Physiotherapy rebuilds stability, and reconstruction surgery is offered to people returning to pivoting sports.

See a doctor promptly after a knee injury with rapid swelling and instability, and go to emergency care if the knee looks deformed or you cannot bear any weight.
