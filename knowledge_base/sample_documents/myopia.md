# Myopia (Short-Sightedness)

Myopia means distant things look blurred while close work stays clear. Children often squint, sit close to the screen, or struggle to read the board at school.

Glasses or contact lenses correct it. Time spent outdoors each day and regular breaks from close work can slow it getting worse in children.

Have an eye test if distance vision is blurred. Seek urgent eye care for a sudden shower of floaters, flashes, or a dark curtain across vision.
