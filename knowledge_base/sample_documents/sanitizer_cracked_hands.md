# Cracked Hands From Hand Sanitiser

Alcohol gel dries the skin. After many uses the knuckles crack and sting. This is irritation, not always an allergy.

Use soap and water when you can, then moisturise. Gel is still useful when you cannot wash.

See a doctor if cracks look infected. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
