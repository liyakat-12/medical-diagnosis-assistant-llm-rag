# Heel Pain After a Run

A bruised feeling under the heel after a longer run or hard surfaces can be plantar fascia overload or a bruised heel fat pad. Morning first-step pain points more toward plantar fasciitis.

Rest from impact, calf stretches, and supportive shoes help. Ice a bottle under the arch after runs.

See a doctor if heel pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care for a sudden snap in the Achilles.
