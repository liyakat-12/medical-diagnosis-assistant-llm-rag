# Back Pain After a Bumpy Auto Ride

Jolts on a hard bench can jar the low back. The ache often appears that evening.

Sit with the feet planted, and walk after the ride. Heat can ease a jarred back.

See a doctor if the pain shoots down the leg. Seek emergency care after a crash or for numb buttocks.
