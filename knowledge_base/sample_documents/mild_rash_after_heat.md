# Heat Rash

Tiny itchy bumps in skin folds after sweating are often heat rash. The sweat ducts are blocked. It is common on the neck, chest, and groin in hot weather.

Cool the skin, wear loose cotton, and avoid thick creams that trap heat. The rash usually fades in a few days.

See a doctor if the bumps become painful, filled with pus, or you have a fever. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading painful red area.
