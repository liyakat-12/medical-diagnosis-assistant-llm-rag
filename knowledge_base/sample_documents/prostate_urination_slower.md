# Slower Urine Flow in Older Men

A slower stream, waiting to start, and getting up at night to urinate becomes more common as the prostate enlarges with age. This is often not cancer, but the same symptoms still need a check.

Reduce evening caffeine and alcohol, and do not rush the bladder. Do not start prostate supplements instead of seeing a clinician.

See a doctor for a changing stream, blood in urine, or getting up many times at night. Seek urgent care if you suddenly cannot pass urine and the bladder is painful.
