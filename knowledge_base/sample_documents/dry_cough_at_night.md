# Dry Cough at Night

A tickly dry cough that gets worse at night is common after a cold, with post-nasal drip, reflux, or dry air. People say I have a dry cough at night. A dry cough after a viral illness can linger for weeks.

Sit more upright to sleep, sip warm drinks, use honey if you are over one year old, and avoid smoke. A humidifier can help in a dry room. Antibiotics do not treat a simple viral cough.

See a doctor if a cough lasts more than three to four weeks, you cough blood, or you have breathlessness, chest pain, or night sweats. Seek emergency care if you cannot catch your breath.
