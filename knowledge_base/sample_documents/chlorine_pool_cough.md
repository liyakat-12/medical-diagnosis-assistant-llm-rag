# Cough After a Chlorine Pool

Strong pool smell can tickle the throat and start a cough, especially in indoor pools. People with asthma may wheeze.

Take air breaks, and use a prescribed inhaler only if you already have one.

See a doctor for a lasting pool cough or new wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness.
