# Pills Feeling Stuck

A tablet that feels stuck in the chest is common if you swallow with too little water. The feeling usually eases. Food regularly sticking, or choking, needs a medical review.

Take tablets with a full glass of water, standing up, and stay upright for a few minutes. Ask a pharmacist if a liquid form exists.

See a doctor if food sticks often or you lose weight. Seek emergency care if you cannot swallow saliva or are choking.
