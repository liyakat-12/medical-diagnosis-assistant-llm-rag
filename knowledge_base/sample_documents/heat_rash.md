# Heat Rash

Heat rash is a prickly, itchy cluster of small bumps where sweat is trapped, often on the neck, chest, or skin folds in hot weather.

Move to a cooler place, wear loose cotton, and keep the skin dry. Cool compresses ease itch. Avoid thick ointments that block sweat.

See a doctor if the rash becomes painful, pus-filled, or you develop a fever.
