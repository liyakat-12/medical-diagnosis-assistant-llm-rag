# Calf Pain After Skipping Rope

Skipping loads the calves and the Achilles. New sessions often make the calves tight the next day.

Shorten the session, land softly, and stretch after a warm-up. Ice after a flare.

See a doctor if you felt a snap in the heel cord. Seek urgent care if you cannot point the foot or take weight.
