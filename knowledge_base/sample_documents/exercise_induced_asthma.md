# Exercise-Induced Asthma

This is airway narrowing that starts during or a few minutes after exercise. It causes coughing, wheeze, chest tightness, and feeling far more out of breath than the effort deserves.

A clinician usually prescribes a reliever inhaler to take before exercise. Warming up slowly and covering the mouth in cold air also help.

See a doctor for breathing trouble that repeats with exercise. Seek emergency care if an inhaler does not relieve symptoms or you cannot finish a sentence.
