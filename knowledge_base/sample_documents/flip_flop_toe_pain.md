# Toe Pain From Flip-Flops

The toes grip a flip-flop with every step. The big-toe joint and the arch can ache after a long walk.

Switch to a strapped sandal for longer walks, and rest the toes that evening.

See a doctor if a toe joint stays swollen. Seek urgent care for a hot red joint with fever.
