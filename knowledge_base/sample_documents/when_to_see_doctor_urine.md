# When Burning Urine Needs a Same-Day Check

Burning urine with frequency should be assessed the same day, especially in pregnancy, men, children, and people with catheters or kidney problems. Do not wait a week hoping leftover antibiotics will work.

Drink extra water while you arrange care. Note fever, back pain, or blood to tell the clinician.

See a clinic or doctor the same day for burning urine. Seek urgent care for fever, shaking, back pain, or vomiting.
