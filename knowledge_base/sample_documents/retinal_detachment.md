# Retinal Detachment

A detaching retina causes a sudden shower of floaters, flashes, or a dark curtain across part of the vision, usually in one eye.

This is an eye emergency. Surgery or a laser procedure may save sight if done quickly. Do not wait to see if it 'clears'.

Go to an emergency eye service the same day. Do not drive yourself if vision is badly reduced.
