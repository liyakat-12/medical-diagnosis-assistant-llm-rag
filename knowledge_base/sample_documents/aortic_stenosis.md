# Aortic Stenosis

Aortic stenosis is narrowing of the main valve out of the heart, usually with age. The classic triad is chest tightness on exertion, fainting or near-fainting on effort, and breathlessness.

A heart scan measures the narrowing. Severe cases are treated by replacing the valve, either with surgery or a catheter procedure.

See a doctor for exertional breathlessness or dizziness, especially over age sixty-five. Seek emergency care for fainting during exertion or chest pain at rest.
