# Restless Legs Syndrome

RLS is an urge to move the legs, worse at rest and in the evening, with crawling or aching feelings that ease when you walk.

Regular sleep, less caffeine, and treating low iron if a clinician finds it, help many people.

See a doctor if nights are ruined. Do not start dopamine tablets on your own.
