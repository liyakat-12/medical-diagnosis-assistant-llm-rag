# Sudden Loss of Vision Warning

Sudden loss of vision in one or both eyes, like a curtain or a black-out, needs emergency care. It can be the eye or a circulation problem. Do not wait to see if it returns.

Note the time it started. Do not eat if you may need urgent treatment. This is a warning note, not a diagnosis.

Call emergency services or go to emergency eye care now for sudden vision loss.
