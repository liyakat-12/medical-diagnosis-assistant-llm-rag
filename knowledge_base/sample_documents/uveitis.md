# Uveitis

Uveitis is inflammation inside the eye. It causes a deep red eye, aching pain, strong discomfort in bright light, blurred vision, and new floaters, usually in one eye.

It needs prescribed steroid eye drops and treatment of any underlying cause. Untreated uveitis can permanently damage sight.

See an eye clinician urgently, the same day if possible, for a painful red eye with light sensitivity and blurred vision.
