# Ache From Looking Down at Devices

A tired neck and upper back after hours of looking down at a tablet is a posture strain. People also describe a heavy head and a headache at the base of the skull.

Raise the device, take breaks, and tuck the chin gently. Walking resets the posture better than staying still.

See a doctor if the ache lasts or you have arm tingling. Seek emergency care for neck pain after a crash.
