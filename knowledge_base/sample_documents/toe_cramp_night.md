# Toe Cramps at Night

Toes that claw painfully in bed are a common night cramp. Tired feet, pointed toes under a heavy duvet, and dehydration can play a part.

Pull the toes up toward you, walk, and loosen bedding. Stretch the calves before bed.

See a doctor if cramps happen most nights. Seek urgent care for a swollen hot calf, which is not a simple cramp.
