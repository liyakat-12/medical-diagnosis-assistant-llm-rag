# Common Reasons for a Headache

A tight band can be a tension headache. A pulsing one-sided headache with nausea can be migraine. Pain over the sinuses with a blocked nose can follow a cold. Caffeine withdrawal and missed meals also cause headaches.

A simple diary of sleep, screens, food, and neck stiffness helps. Most everyday headaches are not caused by a brain tumour.

See a doctor for a new pattern of headaches. Seek emergency care for a sudden thunderclap headache or weakness on one side.
