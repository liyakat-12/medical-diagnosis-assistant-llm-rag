# Sore Knuckles in the Morning

Knuckles that ache and feel puffy first thing can be early arthritis or a reaction after extra gripping. Stiffness lasting more than half an hour needs a review.

Warm the hands under water and ease a tight morning grip. Keep the fingers moving gently.

See a doctor if several knuckles stay swollen. Seek urgent care for a single hot knuckle with fever.
