# Arch Pain When Walking

A tired or burning arch after walking can come from plantar fascia strain, flat or very high arches, or unsupportive shoes. The ache is under the foot, not on top.

Supportive shoes, calf stretches, and shorter walks on softer ground help. Avoid walking barefoot on hard tiles for long periods.

See a doctor if arch pain lasts or you have morning stabbing heel pain that is not settling. Seek urgent care for an open sore if you have diabetes.
