# Jaw Pain When Chewing

A tired or clicking jaw when chewing a steak or an apple can be jaw-joint strain or clenching. Pain may sit in front of the ear and can cause a temple headache.

Eat softer food for a few days, avoid chewing gum, and rest the teeth slightly apart. A warm cloth on the jaw eases tightness.

See a dentist or doctor if the jaw locks or you cannot open fully. Seek urgent care for sudden facial weakness or a jaw that will not close after a blow.
