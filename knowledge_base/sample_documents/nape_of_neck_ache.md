# Ache at the Nape of the Neck

The nape often aches after looking down at a phone, sleeping on a sofa, or sitting under a cold air vent. The pain can travel to the back of the head.

Raise the screen, take chin-tuck breaks, and use a supportive pillow. Gentle heat helps a stiff nape.

See a doctor if the nape stays stiff for two weeks or the arm tingles. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever, or after a crash.
