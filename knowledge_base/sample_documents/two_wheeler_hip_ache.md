# Hip Ache After a Long Scooter Ride

A long sit on a scooter or bike can stiffen the hips and the low back. The first steps after you park often feel tight.

Stop and walk every 30 to 45 minutes, and sit with the knees a little lower if you can.

See a doctor if hip pain lasts into the week. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot walk.
