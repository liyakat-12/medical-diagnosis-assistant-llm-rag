# Stiff Neck After a Haircut

Tipping the head back in a basin or sitting with the neck twisted can leave a stiff nape. It usually eases in a day or two.

Ask for a smaller tip-back, and use heat that evening. Keep the neck moving gently.

See a doctor if you cannot turn the head after a week. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever.
