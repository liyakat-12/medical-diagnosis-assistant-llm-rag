# Diabetic Retinopathy

Long-term high blood sugar damages the small blood vessels at the back of the eye. Early on there are no symptoms at all; later there are floaters, blurred or patchy vision, and difficulty seeing at night.

Good blood sugar and blood pressure control slows it. Laser treatment or eye injections can protect sight if it is caught early.

Everyone with diabetes should have a yearly retinal screening. Seek urgent eye care for sudden floaters, a dark curtain, or sudden vision loss.
