# Postpartum Haemorrhage

This is heavy bleeding after giving birth. Warning signs are soaking a maternity pad within an hour, passing large clots, dizziness, a racing heart, pale clammy skin, and feeling faint.

It can happen in the first day or up to twelve weeks after delivery. It requires immediate hospital treatment to stop the bleeding and replace fluids.

Call emergency services immediately for heavy bleeding after birth. Do not wait for the next midwife visit.
