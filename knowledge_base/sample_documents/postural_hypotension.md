# Postural Hypotension

Postural hypotension is a drop in blood pressure when standing up. It causes a few seconds of dizziness, blurred vision, or lightheadedness after rising from sitting or lying.

Stand up slowly, sit on the bed edge first, drink enough water, avoid very hot baths, and do not skip meals. Review blood pressure tablets with a clinician if it happens often.

See a doctor if you actually faint, fall, or feel it many times a day, as medicines or dehydration may need adjusting.
