# RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus)

RSV causes a cold in older children and adults, but can cause wheeze and breathing trouble in infants and older adults.

Care is supportive: fluids, nasal saline, and watching breathing. Some high-risk infants may receive prevention medicine.

See a doctor if a baby feeds poorly or breathes fast. Seek emergency care for pauses in breathing or blue lips.
