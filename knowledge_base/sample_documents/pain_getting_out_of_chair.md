# Pain Getting Out of a Chair

The knees, hips, or low back can hurt when you stand from a low chair. This is common with stiffness after a long sit or early joint wear.

Sit a little higher, scoot forward, and use the armrests. Stand and walk every half hour.

See a doctor if rising from a chair stays hard for weeks. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight after a fall.
