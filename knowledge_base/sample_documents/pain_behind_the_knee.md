# Pain Behind the Knee

A tight ache behind the knee after sitting can be a strained tendon or a Baker's cyst. A swollen painful calf with this ache can be a clot.

Avoid deep knee bends for a few days and ice after activity if the back of the knee is sore.

See a doctor for a lasting lump behind the knee. Seek emergency care for a swollen painful calf, especially after travel.
