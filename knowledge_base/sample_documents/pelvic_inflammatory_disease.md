# Pelvic Inflammatory Disease

PID is infection of the uterus or tubes, often after an untreated STI. Lower abdominal pain, fever, and unusual discharge can occur.

It needs prompt antibiotics to protect fertility. Sex partners may also need treatment.

See a doctor the same day for pelvic pain with fever. Seek emergency care if pain is sudden and severe or you faint.
