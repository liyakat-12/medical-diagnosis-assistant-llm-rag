# Back Ache After Standing in the Kitchen

Cooking in one spot aches the low back and the feet. A very low counter makes you slump.

Use a small footstool to rest one foot, and walk between tasks. Supportive shoes in the kitchen help.

See a doctor if kitchen pain is daily. Seek emergency care for back pain with new bladder trouble.
