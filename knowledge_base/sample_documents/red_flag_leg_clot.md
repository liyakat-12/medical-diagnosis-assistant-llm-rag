# Painful Swollen Calf Warning

A calf that is swollen, tight, and painful, especially after a flight, surgery, or being still, can be a blood clot. The skin may look redder or warmer. A cramp that lasts seconds is different.

Do not massage the calf. Sit and seek urgent assessment. Chest pain or breathlessness after a swollen calf can mean a clot in the lung.

Seek urgent or emergency care for a swollen painful calf, and call emergency services for chest pain or breathlessness with it.
