# Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

OCD involves unwanted thoughts and repeated rituals (checking, washing, repeating) that take a lot of time and cause distress.

Talking therapy (ERP) and, when needed, prescribed medicine help. It is not just liking things tidy.

See a mental health professional if rituals control your day. Seek emergency or crisis help for thoughts of self-harm.
