# Tinea Versicolor

Tinea versicolor causes pale or darker patches on the chest and back, often in warm months. The patches may be mildly scaly and do not tan evenly.

Antifungal shampoo or cream from a pharmacy, used as directed, often clears the yeast. Colour can take weeks to even out.

See a doctor if patches spread despite treatment or you are not sure of the cause.
