# What High Blood Sugar Can Feel Like

High blood sugar can make you thirsty, send you to the toilet often, blur vision, and tire you. Only a blood test confirms diabetes. These signs can build slowly.

Drink water and see a clinician for a test if this pattern lasts. Do not start or stop diabetes medicine on a guess.

See a doctor for lasting thirst and tiredness. Seek emergency care for confusion, deep sighing breaths, or vomiting with very high thirst.
