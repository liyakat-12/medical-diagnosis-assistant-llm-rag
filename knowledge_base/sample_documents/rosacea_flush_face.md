# Easy Facial Flushing and Redness

Easy flushing, lasting redness on the cheeks and nose, and visible small vessels can be rosacea. Spicy food, alcohol, and heat can trigger it. Acne spots look different.

Use gentle face wash, avoid known triggers, and see a clinician for treatment options. Do not pick.

See a doctor if redness lasts or the eyes feel gritty with rosacea. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye.
