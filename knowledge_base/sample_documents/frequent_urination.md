# Passing Urine Very Often

Needing to urinate many times in the day or night can follow a urine infection, drinking more fluid, caffeine, pregnancy, a large prostate, or higher blood sugar. Frequency alone does not name one disease.

Note how much you drink, whether nights are interrupted, and if there is burning or blood. Reduce evening caffeine as a first step if there is no pain.

See a doctor if frequency lasts more than a few days, you are thirsty all the time, or you have fever. Seek urgent care if you suddenly cannot pass urine at all.
