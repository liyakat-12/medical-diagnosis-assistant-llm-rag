# Neck Pain From Using a Laptop on the Bed

A laptop on the lap or a pillow makes you crane the neck down. The nape and the shoulders then ache.

Put the laptop on a table, raise the screen, and take breaks. Sit with the back supported.

See a doctor if the arms tingle. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever or after a crash.
