# Cardiomyopathy

Cardiomyopathy is disease of the heart muscle itself, making it thickened, stretched, or stiff. Symptoms include breathlessness on effort or lying flat, swollen ankles, tiredness, palpitations, and fainting.

A heart scan confirms the type. Treatment includes medicines, limiting salt and alcohol, and sometimes a device to protect the heart rhythm.

See a doctor for breathlessness on mild effort or ankle swelling. Seek emergency care for fainting, chest pain, or severe breathlessness at rest.
