# Elbow Pain When Gripping

Pain on the outer or inner elbow when gripping a cup, typing, or turning a door handle is often tendon overload, sometimes called tennis or golfer elbow even if you do not play sport.

Ease the gripping task, use a wider handle if possible, ice after activity, and keep the elbow moving gently. A forearm strap can reduce strain for some people.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if gripping pain lasts more than six to eight weeks. Seek urgent care if the elbow is hot, swollen, and you have a fever.
