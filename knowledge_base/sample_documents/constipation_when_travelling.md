# Constipation When Travelling

Holding on, less fluid, airport food, and time-zone change commonly slow the bowel. A few days without a usual stool on a trip is common.

Drink extra water, walk, and eat fruit. Do not ignore the urge. A pharmacist can advise on a short-term laxative if needed.

See a doctor if constipation lasts more than three weeks after you return or you have blood. Seek urgent care for no stool and no wind with a swollen tummy.
