# Lower Back Pain After Lifting

A twinge in the lower back after lifting a box is often a muscle or ligament strain. Pain may spread to the buttock. Most of these strains improve over days to weeks if you keep gently moving.

Avoid heavy lifting for a few days, use your legs next time, and walk rather than staying in bed. Heat can ease spasm.

See a doctor if pain shoots below the knee with numbness. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or new bladder trouble after a lift.
