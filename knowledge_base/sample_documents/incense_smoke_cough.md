# Cough From Incense or Smoke at Home

Smoke from incense, agarbatti, or a kitchen fire can tickle the throat and start a cough, especially if you have asthma.

Air the room, sit away from the smoke, and use a prescribed inhaler only if you already have one.

See a doctor for a cough that lasts or you wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or blue lips.
