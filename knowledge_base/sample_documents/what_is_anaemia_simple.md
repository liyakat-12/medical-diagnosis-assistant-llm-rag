# What Anaemia Can Feel Like

Anaemia means the blood is carrying less oxygen than it should. You may feel tired, look pale, and get breathless on stairs. A blood test, not a guess, confirms it.

Eat iron-rich food if you eat it, but do not start high-dose iron unless a clinician advises it.

See a doctor for lasting tiredness and pallor. Seek urgent care for fainting, chest pain, or soaking through pads every hour.
