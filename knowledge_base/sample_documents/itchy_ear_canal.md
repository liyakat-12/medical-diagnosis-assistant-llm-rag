# Itchy Ear Canal

An itchy ear canal can be dry skin, eczema, or early infection after water or hearing-aid use. Scratching with a bud makes it worse and can break the skin.

Keep the ear dry and stop using cotton buds. Pharmacy ear drops may help if a pharmacist agrees they suit you.

See a doctor for an itchy ear that becomes painful or blocked. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
