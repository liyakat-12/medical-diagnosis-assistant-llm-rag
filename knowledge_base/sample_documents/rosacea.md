# Rosacea

Rosacea causes facial redness, flushing, visible vessels, and sometimes acne-like bumps, often on the cheeks and nose.

Triggers include heat, alcohol, and spicy food. A dermatologist may prescribe gels or tablets. Gentle skincare helps.

See a doctor for persistent facial redness. Eye irritation with rosacea also needs review.
