# Fever and Body Aches

Fever with aching muscles is common in flu and other viral illnesses. You may also have a headache, dry cough, and feel washed out. Most otherwise healthy adults improve with rest.

Rest, fluids, and simple fever reducers if they are safe for you can ease symptoms. Stay home while feverish to reduce spread. Antibiotics do not treat flu viruses.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days or you have chest pain or confusion. Seek emergency care for difficulty breathing, blue lips, or a non-blanching rash with fever.
