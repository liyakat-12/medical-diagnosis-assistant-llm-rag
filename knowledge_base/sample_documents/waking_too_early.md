# Waking Too Early and Cannot Get Back to Sleep

Waking at 4 a.m. and staring at the ceiling can follow stress, alcohol, or low mood. A few nights happen to many people. Lasting early waking with low mood needs a conversation with a clinician.

Keep the room dark, avoid checking the clock, and get up at your usual time even after a short night. Cut evening alcohol.

See a doctor if early waking lasts more than a few weeks or mood is low. Seek urgent help for thoughts of harming yourself.
