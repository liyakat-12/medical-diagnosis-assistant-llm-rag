# Pain on the Top of the Head

A sore spot on the crown after a tight bun, a heavy helmet, or stress is usually muscular. A sudden explosion of pain is not ordinary tension.

Loosen hair or the helmet strap, drink water, and rest the neck. Heat on the upper neck can ease a crown ache.

See a doctor if the top-of-head pain is new and daily. Seek emergency care for sudden severe pain, confusion, or weakness.
