# Neck-Related Headache

Some headaches start at the base of the skull and wrap to the temples after a stiff neck or long time at a computer. This pattern is often called a cervicogenic or tension-type headache linked to neck muscles, not a brain problem.

Gentle neck stretches, better screen height, and breaks from looking down at a phone often help. Heat on the upper shoulders can loosen the area that feeds the headache.

See a doctor for headaches that are new, worsening, or wake you from sleep. Seek emergency care for the worst headache of your life, a weak face or arm, or a sudden stiff neck with fever.
