# Mild Viral Illness

A few days of tiredness, a scratchy throat, and a low fever is often a mild virus. Many different viruses cause the same picture. Antibiotics do not shorten a typical viral illness.

Rest, fluids, and time are the main treatments. You can usually return to usual activity when fever has gone and you feel able.

See a doctor if you are not improving after a week or you have chest pain. Seek emergency care for a non-blanching rash with fever or severe breathlessness.
