# Swollen Ankle After a Mild Sprain

Puffiness around the ankle bones after a twist is expected. Bruising can appear later. Swelling often looks worse the next morning.

Ice, elevation, and a light bandage reduce fluid. Start gentle movement after the first day or two so the ankle does not stiffen.

See a doctor if you cannot take four steps or swelling is very large. Seek urgent care if the ankle looks crooked.
