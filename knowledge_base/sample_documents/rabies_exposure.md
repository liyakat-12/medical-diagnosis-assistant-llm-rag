# Possible Rabies Exposure

Rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms start. Any bite or scratch from a bat or a mammal that might have rabies is an emergency even if the wound is small.

Wash the wound with soap and water for 15 minutes and go to emergency care for vaccines and, if needed, immunoglobulin. Do not wait for symptoms.

Seek care the same day after a suspect bite, including a bat found in a room with a sleeping person.
