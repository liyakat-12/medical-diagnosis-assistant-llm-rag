# Stomach Cancer Warning Signs

Warning signs include indigestion that does not respond to usual treatment, feeling full after small meals, unexplained weight loss, difficulty swallowing, persistent nausea, and tiredness from anaemia.

Long-standing H. pylori infection, smoking, and a diet high in salted or smoked food raise risk. Diagnosis is made by endoscopy.

See a doctor for new indigestion after age fifty-five or any indigestion with weight loss. Seek emergency care for vomiting blood or black tarry stools.
