# Map-Like Patches on the Tongue

Smooth red patches with a white wavy edge on the tongue that change place over days can be geographic tongue. It can sting with spicy food. It is usually harmless.

Avoid very spicy or acidic food when it stings, and keep the mouth clean. No cream is needed for a typical case.

See a doctor if patches are painful and last, or you have other mouth sores. Seek urgent care for a rapidly swelling tongue.
