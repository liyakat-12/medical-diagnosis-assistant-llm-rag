# Headache From a Tight Helmet

A tight helmet or a heavy one can cause a band headache and a sore crown. The pain often eases after you take it off.

Loosen the strap, check the size, and take short breaks on a long ride.

See a doctor if helmet headaches become daily. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache after a crash.
