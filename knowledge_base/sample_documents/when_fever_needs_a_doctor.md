# When a Fever Needs a Doctor

A short fever with a cold can be watched at home if you can drink and stay alert. A fever lasting more than three days, a very high fever, or fever in a baby, a pregnant person, or someone with a weak immune system needs advice.

Rest and fluids. Write the readings. Do not wrap a child in extra blankets to 'sweat it out'.

See a doctor if fever lasts or you are at higher risk. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck, a non-blanching rash, confusion, or breathlessness.
