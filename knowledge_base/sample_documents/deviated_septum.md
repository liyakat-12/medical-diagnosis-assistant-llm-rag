# Deviated Nasal Septum

A deviated septum means the wall between the nostrils is off-centre, so one side of the nose stays blocked. It can also cause noisy breathing in sleep and repeated nosebleeds.

Mild cases are managed with saline rinses and decongestant or steroid sprays. A corrective operation called septoplasty is offered when symptoms are persistent.

See a doctor if one nostril is always blocked, you cannot sleep well, or nosebleeds keep returning.
