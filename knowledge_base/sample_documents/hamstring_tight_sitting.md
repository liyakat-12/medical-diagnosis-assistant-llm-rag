# Tight Hamstrings After Sitting

The back of the thighs can feel tight after a long sit, then protest when you stand or bend. This is often shortened muscles plus a stiff lower back, not always a tear.

Stand and walk often, stretch the hamstrings gently without bouncing, and avoid sitting on a thick wallet.

See a doctor if tightness followed a pop during sport or you have back pain shooting below the knee. Seek emergency care for saddle numbness.
