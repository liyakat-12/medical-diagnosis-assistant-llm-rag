# Tailbone Pain When Sitting

Pain at the very bottom of the spine when sitting, especially on a hard chair, can follow a slip onto the buttocks or long sitting. The tailbone area is tender to sit on.

Use a doughnut cushion or sit leaning slightly forward. Stand often. Ice after a recent fall can help.

See a doctor if tailbone pain lasts more than a few weeks or you have bowel or bladder change. Seek emergency care for saddle numbness.
