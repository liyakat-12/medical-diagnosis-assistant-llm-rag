# Binge Eating Disorder

Binge eating disorder means recurring episodes of eating much more than usual, quickly and often secretly, with a feeling of being unable to stop, followed by guilt and distress. There is no purging afterwards.

It is the most common eating disorder and is linked to obesity, diabetes, and depression. Structured psychological therapy works well.

See a doctor or mental health professional if eating feels out of control and causes distress, rather than starting another restrictive diet.
