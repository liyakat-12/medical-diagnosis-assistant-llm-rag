# Sticky Eyes in the Morning

Lids stuck with yellow crust can be conjunctivitis or blepharitis. The eye may look pink. Vision should not be badly blurred once the crust is cleaned.

Boil and cool water, wipe lids from the nose outward with clean cotton, and wash hands. Do not share towels.

See a clinician if the eye is painful, light hurts, or vision drops. Seek urgent care after a chemical splash or for a newborn with sticky eyes.
