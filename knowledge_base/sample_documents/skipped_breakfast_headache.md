# Headache After Skipping Breakfast

A dull headache late morning after no food is often low blood sugar, missed caffeine, or dehydration. It usually eases after a meal and water.

Eat something with carbohydrate and protein, and drink water. Keep a more regular breakfast if this keeps happening.

See a doctor if meal-skip headaches are frequent. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache unlike your usual ones.
