# Dry Cough That Lingers After a Cold

A tickly cough can last three weeks after the other cold symptoms have gone. The airway stays sensitive. This leftover cough is common and usually viral, not a new pneumonia by itself.

Honey if you are over one year old, warm drinks, and sitting upright at night help. Avoid smoke. Antibiotics do not shorten a typical post-viral cough.

See a doctor if a cough lasts more than three to four weeks, you cough blood, or you feel breathless. Seek emergency care if you cannot catch your breath.
