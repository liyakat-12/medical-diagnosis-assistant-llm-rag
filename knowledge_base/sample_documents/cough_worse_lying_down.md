# Cough Worse When Lying Down

A cough that starts when you lie flat can be mucus dripping from the nose, reflux, or a leftover viral cough. People notice it as they get into bed.

Sleep more upright, finish meals three hours before bed if reflux is likely, and use saline for a blocked nose. Honey can soothe a tickle if you are over one.

See a doctor if a night cough lasts more than a few weeks or you wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or coughing blood.
