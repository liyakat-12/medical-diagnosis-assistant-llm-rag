# Tennis Elbow

Tennis elbow is pain on the outer side of the elbow from overuse of the forearm tendons. It hurts when gripping, lifting a cup, shaking hands, or twisting a door handle, and the bony point is tender.

Rest from the aggravating activity, ice, a forearm strap, and specific strengthening exercises usually work. Recovery commonly takes six months, so persistence matters.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if there is no improvement after six to eight weeks, or if the elbow locks, swells, or follows an injury.
