# Pain on the Inner Side of the Knee

Aching on the inner knee can follow a twist, extra walking, or strain of the inner ligament or cartilage. Some people feel it after sitting cross-legged. This pattern is common and does not name one injury by itself.

Rest from twisting sport, ice after activity, and keep the knee moving gently. Avoid forcing a full squat.

See a doctor if the inner knee locks, swells, or feels unstable. Seek urgent care if you cannot walk after an injury.
