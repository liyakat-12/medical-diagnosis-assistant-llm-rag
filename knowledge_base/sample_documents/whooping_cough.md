# Whooping Cough (Pertussis)

Pertussis starts like a cold, then becomes long bursts of coughing that may end in a whoop or vomiting. Infants can stop breathing.

A clinician may test and give antibiotics to reduce spread. Vaccination protects babies and close contacts.

See a doctor for a cough lasting weeks or any cough in a young infant. Seek emergency care if a baby turns blue.
