# Cholera

Cholera is a bacterial infection from contaminated water that causes sudden painless watery diarrhoea described as looking like rice water, often with vomiting but little fever.

Fluid loss can be extremely fast, leading to dangerous dehydration within hours. Oral rehydration solution should be started immediately, and severe cases need intravenous fluids.

Seek emergency care straight away for heavy watery diarrhoea with sunken eyes, no urine, cold hands, or collapse, especially during an outbreak.
