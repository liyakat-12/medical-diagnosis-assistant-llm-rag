# Teeth That Hurt With Sweets

A zing with sweet food can mean a cavity or a leaking filling. Sensitivity to cold alone can be exposed dentine. Lingering pain after the sweet is gone needs a dentist soon.

Cut very sugary snacks, clean carefully around the sore tooth, and book a dental visit. Do not put aspirin on the gum.

See a dentist soon. Seek urgent care for facial swelling or fever with a dental swelling.
