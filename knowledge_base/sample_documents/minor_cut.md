# Minor Cut

A small cut that stops bleeding with pressure and is not deep can usually be cared for at home.

Wash with clean water, press until bleeding stops, apply a plaster, and keep it dry for a day. Watch for redness or pus.

Seek urgent care if bleeding will not stop, the wound is deep or dirty, a tendon may be cut, or tetanus shots are not up to date.
