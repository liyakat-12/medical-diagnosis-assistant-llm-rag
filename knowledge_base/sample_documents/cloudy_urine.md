# Cloudy Urine

Urine that looks cloudy can be concentrated waste, phosphate crystals after a meal, or infection if it also burns and smells strong. Cloudiness alone is not proof of a UTI.

Drink more water and note burning, fever, or blood. Do not start leftover antibiotics.

See a doctor if cloudy urine comes with burning, pain, or fever. Seek urgent care for back pain, vomiting, and fever.
