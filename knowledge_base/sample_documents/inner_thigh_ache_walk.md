# Inner Thigh Pain When Walking

The inner thigh can strain after a sudden split, a slip, or a sport that needs a side step. A bruise and pain when you bring the knees together is typical.

Rest the split, ice, and walk within comfort. Do not force a full stretch in the first days.

See a doctor if you cannot walk after a few days. Seek urgent care for a groin bulge that will not reduce, or severe swelling.
