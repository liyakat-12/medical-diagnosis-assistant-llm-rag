# Pain When Putting On a Coat

Reaching back into a sleeve can catch a sore shoulder tendon. The first movement is often the worst.

Put the sore arm in the sleeve first, and avoid yanking the coat on. Ice after a flare.

See a doctor if dressing pain lasts. Seek emergency care if coat-reaching pain comes with chest pressure.
