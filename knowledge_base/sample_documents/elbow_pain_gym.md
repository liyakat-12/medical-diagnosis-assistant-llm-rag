# Elbow Pain After the Gym

An ache on the outer or inner elbow after extra weights, pull-ups, or racquet sport is often tendon overload. Pain may shoot into the forearm when you grip.

Ease gripping and wrist-heavy lifts, ice after sessions, and keep the elbow moving. Return with lighter loads and a wider grip.

See a doctor if gym elbow pain lasts more than six to eight weeks. Seek urgent care for a hot swollen elbow with fever.
