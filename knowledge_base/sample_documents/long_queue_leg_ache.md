# Aching Legs After Standing in a Queue

Standing still in a queue or kitchen can ache the feet, calves, and low back. Veins can also feel heavy by the end of the day.

Shift your weight, walk on the spot, and raise the feet later. Supportive shoes help more than very flat ones.

See a doctor if one leg swells or the pain is only in one calf. Seek emergency care for a swollen painful calf or swelling with breathlessness.
