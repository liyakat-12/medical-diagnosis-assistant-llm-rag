# Addison Disease

Addison disease means the adrenal glands do not make enough cortisol. Symptoms build slowly and include extreme tiredness, weight loss, poor appetite, dizziness on standing, salt craving, and darkening of the skin and skin creases.

Blood tests confirm it, and treatment is lifelong replacement steroid tablets with extra doses during illness, which must never be missed.

See a doctor for this combination of symptoms. Call emergency services for severe vomiting, confusion, or collapse, which is an adrenal crisis.
