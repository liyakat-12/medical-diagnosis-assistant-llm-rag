# Cradle Cap on a Baby's Scalp

Greasy yellow scales on a baby's scalp are common and usually harmless. The baby is otherwise well and not usually very itchy.

Wash with a mild baby shampoo and loosen scales with baby oil if a health visitor agrees. Do not pick the scales off hard.

See a doctor if the scalp looks infected or the rash spreads widely. Seek urgent care for a feverish unhappy baby.
