# Ear Pain During a Flight

Ears can hurt or pop when a plane descends because the pressure tube cannot equalise, especially if you have a cold. Hearing may feel muffled for a while after landing.

Swallow, yawn, or sip a drink on descent. A decongestant is sometimes used in adults if a pharmacist agrees; it is not for everyone. Do not fly with a severe ear infection if a clinician has advised against it.

See a doctor if ear pain lasts more than a few days after a flight or hearing does not return. Seek urgent care for severe dizziness, discharge, or a facial weakness.
