# Shoulder Pain After Carrying a Bag

A heavy bag on one shoulder can strain the upper trapezius and the rotator cuff. People notice an ache on the same side of the neck and outer shoulder after commuting or shopping.

Switch sides, use a backpack with two straps, and lighten the load. Heat and gentle shoulder rolls ease a tired muscle.

See a doctor if carrying pain lasts more than a few weeks or the arm feels weak. Seek emergency care for shoulder pain with chest tightness.
