# Back or Shoulder Pain After Lifting a Water Can

A full water can is an everyday heavy lift. A sudden pull in the back or shoulder is a common strain.

Lift with the knees, keep the can close, and split the load if you can. Ice after a flare.

See a doctor if you cannot stand straight after a few days. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks.
