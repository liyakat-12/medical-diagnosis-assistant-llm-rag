# Tummy Ache Before an Exam

Nerves can cramp the tummy, loosen the stool, or kill appetite before a test. The ache often eases after the exam starts.

Eat a simple familiar meal, sip water, and use a toilet before you sit. Slow breathing can ease a cramp.

See a doctor if tummy pain is daily even in holidays. Seek urgent care for severe pain, vomiting, or blood in the stool.
