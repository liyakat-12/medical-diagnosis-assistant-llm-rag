# Ache Under the Left Ribs

A stitch or gas under the left ribs after a fizzy drink or a big meal is common. Pain that is crushing, spreads to the arm, or comes with breathlessness is not gas.

Sit up, loosen a tight belt, and take smaller meals. Note whether food, wind, or exercise starts it.

See a doctor for left-rib pain that keeps returning. Seek emergency care for crushing pain, sweating, or faintness.
