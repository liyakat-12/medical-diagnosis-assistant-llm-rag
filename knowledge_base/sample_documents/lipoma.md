# Lipoma

A lipoma is a soft fatty lump under the skin that moves easily when pressed, grows very slowly, and is usually painless. They are common on the back, shoulders, neck, and arms.

Lipomas are harmless and usually need no treatment. One can be removed with a small operation if it is painful, pressing on something, or unsightly.

See a doctor to confirm the lump, and promptly if it becomes hard, fixed, painful, or grows quickly, which suggests a different diagnosis.
