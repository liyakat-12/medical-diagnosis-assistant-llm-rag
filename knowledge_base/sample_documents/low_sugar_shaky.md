# Shaky and Sweaty If You Have Diabetes

Shakiness, sweating, hunger, and confusion in someone who takes insulin or certain diabetes tablets can be low blood sugar. People without diabetes can feel shaky after a long gap without food, which is usually milder.

If you treat diabetes and have a plan, take the fast sugar your clinician has advised, then a snack. Do not drive.

Seek emergency care if the person cannot swallow, has a seizure, or does not recover quickly. Friends should not force drinks into an unconscious person.
