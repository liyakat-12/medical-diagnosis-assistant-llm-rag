# Tight Shoulders From Stress

Worry can show up as tight shoulders, a clenched jaw, a tension headache, or an upset stomach. The body stays in a ready state. These physical feelings are real even when tests are normal.

Short walks, slower breathing, regular sleep, and talking to someone you trust can lower the tightness. Heat on the shoulders helps some people.

See a doctor or mental-health service if stress stops you working or sleeping for weeks. Seek urgent help if you have chest pain with breathlessness, or thoughts of harming yourself.
