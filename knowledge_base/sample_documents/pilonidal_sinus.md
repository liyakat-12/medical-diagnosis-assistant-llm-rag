# Pilonidal Sinus

A pilonidal sinus is a small tunnel in the skin at the top of the buttock crease, often containing hair. When it gets infected it becomes a painful red swelling that may leak pus or blood.

Keeping the area clean and hair-free helps prevent flare-ups. An infected sinus needs drainage, and recurrent ones are treated with surgery.

See a doctor for a painful swelling or discharge at the top of the buttock crease, and urgently if there is fever.
