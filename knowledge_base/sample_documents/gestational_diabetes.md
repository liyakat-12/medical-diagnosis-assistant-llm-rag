# Gestational Diabetes

Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar first found in pregnancy. Many people feel well. A glucose drink test finds it.

Food planning, activity, and sometimes insulin protect parent and baby. Sugar usually falls after birth, but later type 2 risk is higher.

Attend pregnancy screening. Seek urgent care for very high thirst, vomiting, or reduced baby movements.
