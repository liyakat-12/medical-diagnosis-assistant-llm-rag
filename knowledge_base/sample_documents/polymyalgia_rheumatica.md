# Polymyalgia Rheumatica

Polymyalgia rheumatica causes sudden severe aching and stiffness in the shoulders, neck, and hips in people over fifty. Getting out of bed or raising the arms in the morning becomes very difficult.

Blood tests show high inflammation. It responds dramatically to a low dose of steroid tablets, usually within days, and the dose is tapered slowly over a year or more.

See a doctor for new shoulder and hip stiffness over fifty. Seek emergency care if a new headache, jaw pain on chewing, or vision change develops.
