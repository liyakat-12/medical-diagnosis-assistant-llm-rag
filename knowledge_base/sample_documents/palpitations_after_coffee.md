# Heart Flutter After Coffee

A brief flutter or thud after extra coffee, energy drinks, or missed sleep is common. You may feel the heart skip. Lasting fast beats with chest pain or faintness are different.

Cut caffeine for a few days and note whether the flutters settle. Sit and breathe slowly during a brief episode.

See a doctor if palpitations keep happening or last more than a few minutes. Seek emergency care for chest pain, severe breathlessness, or fainting.
