# Stomach Cramps After Food

Cramping in the upper tummy after meals can be indigestion, a rich meal, or a sensitive gut. Pain that eases after passing wind or stool is often gut-related. Pain that wakes you at night needs a review.

Smaller meals, less alcohol, and sitting up after eating help many people. Keep a simple food and symptom note for a week.

See a doctor for cramps lasting more than a few weeks, black stools, or vomiting blood. Seek emergency care for sudden severe abdominal pain.
