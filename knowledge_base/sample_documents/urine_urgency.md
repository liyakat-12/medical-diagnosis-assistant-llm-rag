# Sudden Urge to Urinate

A sudden need to rush to the toilet, passing only a little, often goes with a urine infection or an irritated bladder. Coffee and a cold can also increase urgency. Burning supports infection as a possibility.

Drink water, avoid holding on, and see a clinician rather than guessing antibiotics. Reduce evening caffeine if there is no pain and this is a long-term pattern.

See a doctor or clinic the same day if urgency comes with burning, blood, or fever. Seek urgent care if you cannot pass urine at all.
