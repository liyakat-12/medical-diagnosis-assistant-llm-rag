# Pain on the Outer Thigh

A tight band on the outer thigh after walking or lying on one side can be the iliotibial band or a sore hip tendon. Numbness in a patch of skin can be a trapped nerve.

Shorten the stride, avoid long side-lying on the sore side, and stretch gently after a warm-up.

See a doctor if outer-thigh pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold pale leg.
