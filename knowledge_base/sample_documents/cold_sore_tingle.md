# Tingling Lip Before a Cold Sore

A tingle or itch on the lip before a blister is a typical cold-sore warning. Sun, a cold, or stress can trigger it. The sores are contagious while they are open.

A pharmacist can advise on an antiviral cream if started early. Avoid kissing and sharing cups while blisters are present.

See a doctor if sores are very frequent, near the eye, or you have a weakened immune system. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye with a cold sore.
