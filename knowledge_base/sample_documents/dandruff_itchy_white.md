# White Flakes on Dark Clothes

Loose white flakes on the shoulders with an itchy scalp are typical dandruff. Thick silvery plaques stuck to the scalp can be psoriasis and need a different look.

Use an anti-dandruff shampoo regularly if a pharmacist agrees, and rinse well. Do not scratch until the scalp bleeds.

See a doctor if flakes are thick and the scalp is very red. Seek urgent care for a painful swollen scalp with fever.
