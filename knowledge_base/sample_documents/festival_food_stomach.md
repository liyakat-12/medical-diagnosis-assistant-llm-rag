# Upset Stomach After Festival Food

Rich sweets, fried snacks, and odd meal times can cramp the tummy. A sudden bug from shared food can add vomiting or diarrhoea.

Sip water or oral rehydration, eat bland food, and rest. Avoid more rich food until it settles.

See a doctor if vomiting lasts more than a day or you cannot keep fluids down. Seek urgent care for blood in vomit or signs of dehydration.
