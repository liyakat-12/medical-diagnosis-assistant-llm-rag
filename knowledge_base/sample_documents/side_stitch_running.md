# A Stitch in the Side When Running

A sharp catch under the ribs while running is a common stitch. It often happens if you start too fast or run soon after a meal.

Slow down, raise the arm on the sore side, and breathe out as the foot on that side lands. Wait after a big meal before you run.

See a doctor if the same pain happens at rest. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or breathlessness at rest.
