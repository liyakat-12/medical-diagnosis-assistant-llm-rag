# Stiff Fingers in the Morning

Fingers that feel puffy and slow first thing can be early osteoarthritis, especially if the end joints enlarge. Stiffness lasting more than 30 to 60 minutes in many joints can be inflammatory arthritis.

Warm water, gentle fist-and-open exercises, and avoiding a tight grip first thing help mechanical stiffness.

See a doctor if morning finger stiffness lasts more than half an hour most days or joints swell. Seek urgent care for a single hot finger joint with fever.
