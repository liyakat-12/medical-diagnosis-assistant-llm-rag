# Pain When Combing Hair

Pain when the arm goes up to comb or wash hair is a common rotator-cuff or frozen-shoulder pattern. A stiff turn of the shoulder is a clue.

Lower the elbow and lean to the side instead of forcing the arm straight up. Gentle pendulum swings can help.

See a doctor if you cannot reach your head after two weeks. Seek urgent care if the arm is numb or the shoulder looks out of place.
