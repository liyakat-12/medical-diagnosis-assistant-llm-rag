# Pain on the Inner Elbow

Inner elbow pain when carrying bags or doing a golf-style swing is often golfer's elbow, a tendon overload. You do not need to play golf to get it. The funny-bone nerve can also tingle into the ring and little fingers.

Reduce repeated wrist bending, use two hands to lift, and ice after activity. Stretch the forearm gently.

See a doctor if inner elbow pain lasts or you have numbness in the little finger. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold or very weak hand.
