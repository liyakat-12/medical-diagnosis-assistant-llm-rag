# Teeth Grinding (Bruxism)

Bruxism is clenching or grinding the teeth, usually during sleep and often linked to stress. It causes jaw ache, morning headaches, worn flat teeth, and a partner noticing the grinding sound.

Reducing stress, cutting caffeine and alcohol in the evening, and a dentist-made night guard protect the teeth and ease the ache.

See a dentist if teeth are visibly worn or chipped, or if jaw pain and headaches happen most mornings.
