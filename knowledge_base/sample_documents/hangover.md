# Hangover

A hangover follows heavy alcohol use. Headache, thirst, nausea, tiredness, and sensitivity to light are common the next morning.

Water, rest, bland food, and time are the main care. Do not take more alcohol to 'cure' it. Pain relief may help if it is suitable for you.

See a doctor if vomiting will not stop, you cannot keep fluids down, or you have chest pain, confusion, or a very fast heartbeat.
