# Cannot Turn the Head Fully

A neck that will not turn to check a blind spot is usually muscle spasm or stiff joints after sitting or sleeping oddly. Pain is local and there is no fever.

Move within a comfortable range several times a day. Heat and posture breaks help. Do not have someone yank the neck.

See a doctor if turning stays limited for more than two weeks or an arm becomes weak. Seek emergency care for inability to bend the neck with fever.
