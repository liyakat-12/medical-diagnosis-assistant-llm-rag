# Septic Arthritis

Septic arthritis is infection inside a joint. One joint becomes suddenly hot, swollen, and so painful you will not move it. Fever is common.

This needs emergency hospital drainage and antibiotics. Delay can destroy the joint.

Go to emergency care for a single hot joint you cannot move, especially after a joint injection or if you have a weak immune system.
