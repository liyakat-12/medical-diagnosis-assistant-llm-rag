# Face Pain When Bending Forward

Pressure in the cheeks or forehead that worsens when you bend to tie shoes is a common sinus picture after a cold. Most cases are viral.

Steam, saline, fluids, and simple pain relief help. Most improve within two to three weeks without antibiotics.

See a doctor if facial pain lasts more than three weeks or you have a high fever. Seek emergency care for a swollen red eyelid with vision change.
