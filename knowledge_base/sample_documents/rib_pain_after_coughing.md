# Rib Pain After a Long Cough

A sharp catch in the chest wall after days of coughing is often a strained chest muscle or a sore rib joint. It usually hurts more when you twist or press the spot.

Support the chest with a pillow when you cough, use ice or heat, and ease extra strain. This is not the same as crushing central chest pain.

See a doctor if rib pain lasts after the cough settles. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, breathlessness, or pain after a blow to the chest.
