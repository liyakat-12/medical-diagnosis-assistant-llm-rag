# A Gland That Stays Up on One Side

A single neck gland can stay a little large after an ear or throat infection. A hard, painless gland that keeps growing, or glands with night sweats and weight loss, needs a prompt review.

Give a typical tender cold-related gland a couple of weeks to settle. Do not squeeze it.

See a doctor if a gland lasts more than two weeks or is hard and painless. Seek urgent care for a rapidly growing swelling with breathing trouble.
