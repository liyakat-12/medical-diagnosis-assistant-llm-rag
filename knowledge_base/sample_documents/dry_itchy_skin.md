# Dry Itchy Skin

Dry, tight, itchy skin is common in cold weather, after hot showers, or with soap that strips oil. Eczema and ageing skin also itch. Widespread itching without a rash can have other causes and needs a check if it lasts.

Use lukewarm water, pat dry, and put on a plain moisturiser while the skin is still damp. Avoid scratching until the skin bleeds. Fragrance-free products are kinder.

See a doctor if itching keeps you awake, a rash spreads, or you have yellow skin or unexplained weight loss. Seek urgent care for a sudden widespread rash with lip swelling.
