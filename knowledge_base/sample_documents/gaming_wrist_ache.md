# Wrist Ache After Gaming

A bent gaming wrist for hours can ache the tendons and the carpal tunnel area. Night tingling can start.

Keep the wrist straighter, take half-hour breaks, and shake the hands out.

See a doctor if the fingers stay numb. Seek urgent care after a fall if the wrist looks deformed.
