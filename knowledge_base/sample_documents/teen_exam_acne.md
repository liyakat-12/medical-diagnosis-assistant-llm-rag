# Acne Flare in Exam Season

Stress, missed sleep, and extra touching of the face can flare teen acne around exams. This is common and not a character fault.

Wash gently, do not pick, and keep a regular sleep if you can. A pharmacist can advise a simple cream if suitable.

See a doctor for painful cysts or acne that scars. Seek urgent care for a very swollen red face with fever.
