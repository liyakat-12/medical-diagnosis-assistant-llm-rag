# Premenstrual Syndrome

PMS is a set of symptoms in the one to two weeks before a period, including bloating, breast tenderness, headaches, food cravings, irritability, and low mood, which settle once bleeding starts.

Regular exercise, reducing salt, caffeine, and alcohol, good sleep, and tracking symptoms in a diary help most people. Simple pain relief eases cramps and headaches.

See a doctor if symptoms stop you working or damage relationships, which may be the more severe form called PMDD.
