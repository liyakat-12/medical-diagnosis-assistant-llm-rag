# Bruising More Easily Than Before

A few bruises on the shins after knocks are common, especially as skin thins with age. New crops of bruises without knocks, or bleeding gums with bruises, need a medical review.

Protect thin skin on the arms, and tell a clinician about blood-thinning medicines. Do not stop prescribed medicines on your own.

See a doctor for bruises without injury or very large painful lumps. Seek urgent care after a head bump with vomiting or confusion.
