# Indigestion After a Large Meal

Fullness, belching, and upper tummy discomfort after a feast is ordinary indigestion. Tight clothes and lying down make it worse.

Walk gently, loosen your waistband, and wait before lying down. Smaller future meals prevent many attacks.

See a doctor if indigestion is frequent or you lose weight. Seek emergency care for chest pain, sweating, or pain into the arm.
