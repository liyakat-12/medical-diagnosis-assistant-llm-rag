# Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder

PMDD is a severe form of PMS. In the two weeks before each period there is intense low mood, anger, anxiety, hopelessness, or feeling out of control, which disappears within days of the period starting.

Tracking symptoms over two cycles confirms the pattern. Treatment includes certain antidepressants taken either continuously or only in the luteal phase, and hormonal options.

See a doctor or mental health service. Seek emergency help immediately for any thoughts of self-harm.
