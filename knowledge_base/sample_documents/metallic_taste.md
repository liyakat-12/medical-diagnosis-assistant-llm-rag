# Metallic Taste in the Mouth

A metal taste can follow a cold, some medicines, pregnancy, reflux, or poor oral hygiene. It is usually not a sign of heavy-metal poisoning in everyday life. Lasting change needs a check.

Clean the teeth and tongue, stay hydrated, and tell a clinician about new medicines. Do not stop prescribed tablets on your own.

See a doctor if a metallic taste lasts more than a couple of weeks. Seek urgent care for a sudden swollen tongue or trouble breathing.
