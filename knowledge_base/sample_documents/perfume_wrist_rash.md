# Rash Where Perfume Touches

Perfume, deodorant, or aftershave can inflame a patch of skin. The rash sits where the spray or splash went.

Stop that product, wash the skin, and moisturise. Do not add more fragrance on the sore patch.

See a doctor if the rash blisters or spreads. Seek emergency care for trouble breathing or facial swelling.
