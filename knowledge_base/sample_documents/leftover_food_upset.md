# Upset After Eating Leftovers

Food left out too long can cause cramps, nausea, or diarrhoea. Rice and meat leftovers need careful cooling and reheating.

Reheat leftovers until steaming hot, and throw away food that sat at room temperature for hours.

See a doctor if you cannot keep fluids down. Seek urgent care for bloody diarrhoea or high fever.
