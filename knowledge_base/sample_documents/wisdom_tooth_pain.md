# Wisdom Tooth Pain

A wisdom tooth that cannot come through properly causes aching at the very back of the jaw, red swollen gum over the tooth, bad taste, and difficulty opening the mouth wide.

Warm salt-water rinses and simple pain relief help short term. A dentist decides whether cleaning, antibiotics, or removal is needed.

See a dentist for repeated flare-ups. Seek urgent care for facial swelling, fever, or trouble swallowing.
