# Throat Pain When Yawning

A wide yawn can stretch a raw throat after a cold, dry air, or shouting. Pain that makes you drool or cannot swallow saliva is more serious.

Sip cool drinks and rest the voice. A humidifier can ease a dry room.

See a doctor if yawning pain lasts more than a week. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
