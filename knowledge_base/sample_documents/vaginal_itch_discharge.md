# Vaginal Itch or Discharge Change

Itch with a thick white discharge can be thrush. A thin grey discharge with a fishy smell can be bacterial vaginosis. Green or yellow discharge with pelvic pain needs a sexual-health check. These are possibilities, not a self-diagnosis.

Avoid scented soaps and tight synthetic underwear. Do not douche. A pharmacist or clinic can advise on suitable treatment after questions about your symptoms.

See a clinic or doctor for a first episode, pregnancy, pelvic pain, fever, or symptoms after a new partner. Seek urgent care for severe pelvic pain and fainting.
