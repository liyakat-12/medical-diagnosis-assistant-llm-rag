# Infant Colic

Colic is intense crying in a healthy baby for more than three hours a day, several days a week, often in the late afternoon or evening. The baby clenches the fists, goes red, and draws the knees up.

Try holding the baby during crying, gentle rocking, a warm bath, winding during and after feeds, and white noise. It usually peaks at six weeks and settles by four months.

See a doctor if the baby has fever, vomiting, poor feeding, blood in stool, or is not gaining weight, as that is not simple colic.
