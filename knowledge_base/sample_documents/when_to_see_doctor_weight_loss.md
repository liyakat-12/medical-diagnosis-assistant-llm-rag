# Unplanned Weight Loss

Clothes becoming loose without trying can follow a leftover infection, low mood, an overactive thyroid, or other illness. Unplanned weight loss is a reason to see a doctor rather than a diagnosis by itself.

Note appetite, thirst, night sweats, and bowel change to tell a clinician. Do not start extreme diets while this is being checked.

See a doctor for unplanned weight loss. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood, black stools, or fainting.
