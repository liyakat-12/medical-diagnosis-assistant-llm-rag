# Cannot Sleep After a Late Nap

A long nap after mid-afternoon can steal sleep pressure from the night. You then lie awake. A short early nap is less likely to do this.

Keep naps under 20 to 30 minutes and before mid-afternoon if you nap at all. Get up at a regular time the next day.

See a doctor if you cannot stay awake in the day or poor sleep lasts weeks. Seek urgent care for chest pain that wakes you.
