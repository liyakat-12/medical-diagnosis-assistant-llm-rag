# Thirst and Dark Urine on a Hot Day

Thirst, a dry mouth, headache, and darker urine after heat, exercise, or a busy day without drinks are early dehydration signs. Older adults may feel less thirst, so they can dry out faster.

Drink water or an oral rehydration drink in small sips. Rest in a cool place. Water-rich foods such as fruit also help.

Seek urgent care if you have no urine for eight hours, feel confused, or cannot keep fluids down, and for any unwell baby or frail older person.
