# Jaw That Clicks When You Eat

A click in front of the ear when you chew is common. It may be the jaw disc moving. Pain, locking, or a jaw that will not open needs a check. Painless long-standing clicks often need no treatment.

Avoid chewing gum and very chewy food for a while. Rest the teeth slightly apart.

See a dentist or doctor if the jaw locks or you cannot open fully. Seek urgent care after a blow if the jaw will not close.
