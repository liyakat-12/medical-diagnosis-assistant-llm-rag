# Hand Cramp After Long Writing

The hand and forearm can cramp after long exam writing or extra pen work. The grip feels tired rather than numb.

Shake the hand out, use a thicker pen, and take page breaks. Stretch the fingers the other way.

See a doctor if the hand stays weak or numb. Seek urgent care after an injury if the hand is cold and pale.
