# Stiff Neck After Sleeping on the Sofa

A sofa arm or a deep cushion can twist the neck for hours. You may wake unable to turn to one side.

Use heat, keep the neck moving in a small pain-free range, and sleep on a bed with a supportive pillow.

See a doctor if you cannot turn the neck after a week. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever or after a crash.
