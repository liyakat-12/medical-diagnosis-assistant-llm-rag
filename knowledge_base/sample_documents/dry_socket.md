# Dry Socket

Dry socket is intense throbbing pain that begins two to four days after a tooth is pulled, when the protective blood clot is lost. There is often a bad taste and visible bare bone in the socket.

The dentist cleans the socket and places a medicated dressing, which relieves the pain quickly. Smoking and rinsing too hard after extraction raise the risk.

See the dentist who performed the extraction. Do not rely on painkillers alone, as the socket needs dressing.
