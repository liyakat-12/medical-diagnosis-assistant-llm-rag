# Back Ache After a Long Meeting

Sitting still in a meeting chair stiffens the low back and the hips. The first stand often hurts most.

Shift your weight, uncross the legs, and walk at the break. A small lumbar cushion can help.

See a doctor if meeting pain lasts into the weekend. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks.
