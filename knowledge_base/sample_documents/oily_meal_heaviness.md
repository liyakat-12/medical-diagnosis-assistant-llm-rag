# Heavy Feeling After an Oily Meal

A large fried meal can sit heavy, with burps and a tight upper tummy. Repeated attacks under the right ribs can be a gallbladder pattern.

Eat a smaller portion next time, walk slowly, and avoid lying flat. Do not pile on more oil to 'settle' it.

See a doctor for repeated heavy attacks. Seek emergency care for severe pain with fever or yellow eyes.
