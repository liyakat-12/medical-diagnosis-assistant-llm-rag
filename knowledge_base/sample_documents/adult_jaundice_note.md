# Jaundice in Adults

In adults, jaundice can follow hepatitis, gallstones, or other liver and bile-duct problems. Yellow eyes, dark urine, pale stools, and itching are the usual clues.

Stop alcohol and do not take extra paracetamol or herbal pills until a clinician advises. This cannot be cleared with a home cleanse.

See a doctor the same day. Seek emergency care for confusion, vomiting blood, or severe belly pain.
