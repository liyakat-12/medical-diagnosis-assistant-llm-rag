# Gum Boil Near a Tooth

A pimple on the gum next to a tooth can be a dental abscess draining. The tooth may ache or feel high. Antibiotics alone do not remove the cause.

Rinse with warm salt water and see a dentist as soon as you can. Simple pain relief can help if it is safe for you.

See a dentist urgently. Seek emergency care for a swollen face or eye, difficulty swallowing, or a high fever.
