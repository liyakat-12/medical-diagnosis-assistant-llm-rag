# Raynaud Phenomenon

In Raynaud phenomenon the small blood vessels of the fingers and toes narrow in cold or stress. The digits turn white, then blue, then red and tingle painfully as blood returns.

Keep hands and the whole body warm, wear gloves, stop smoking, and reduce caffeine. Attacks usually last minutes and settle with gentle rewarming.

See a doctor if attacks are frequent, affect only one hand, or if you develop skin ulcers or sores on the fingertips, which need prompt treatment.
