# Sore Mouth Corners and Tiredness

Cracked mouth corners with a sore tongue and tiredness can happen when B12, folate, or iron is low. Many other causes exist, so this is a reason for a check, not a self-diagnosis.

Eat a varied diet and see a clinician for blood tests rather than stacking supplements.

See a doctor for lasting cracked corners with tiredness. Seek urgent care for sudden weakness or confusion.
