# What Low Blood Sugar Can Feel Like

Low blood sugar can shake you, sweat you, confuse you, and make you hungry or irritable. It is more likely if you take diabetes medicine.

If you are treated for diabetes and this happens, take fast sugar the way your clinician taught you, then a snack. People without diabetes should still get checked if this keeps happening.

See a doctor after a low-sugar episode. Seek emergency care for a seizure, collapse, or a person with diabetes who cannot swallow.
