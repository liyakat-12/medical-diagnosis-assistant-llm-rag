# Heat Exhaustion

Heat exhaustion causes heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, headache, and a fast pulse after heat or hard work. The person is usually still aware.

Move to shade, loosen clothes, sip water, and cool the skin. Rest until recovered.

Seek emergency care if confusion, fainting, or very hot dry skin develops, which can be heat stroke.
