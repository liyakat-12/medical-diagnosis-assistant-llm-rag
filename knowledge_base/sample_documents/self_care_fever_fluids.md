# Fluids and Rest During a Fever

When you have a fever from a typical virus, rest and extra drinks are the main home measures. You do not need to bring every mild fever down with tablets if you are otherwise comfortable.

Sip water or oral rehydration drinks. Use simple fever reducers only if they are safe for you and you feel miserable. Do not give aspirin to children or teenagers with flu-like illness.

See a doctor if you cannot drink or fever lasts more than three days. Seek emergency care for confusion or severe breathlessness.
