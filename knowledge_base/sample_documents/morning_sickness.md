# Morning Sickness

Nausea and vomiting are very common in early pregnancy, usually starting around week six and settling by week fourteen. Despite the name it can happen at any time of day.

Small frequent dry snacks, eating something before getting out of bed, ginger, plenty of small sips of fluid, and avoiding strong smells all help.

See a doctor if you cannot keep fluids down for a day, lose weight, pass very little dark urine, or feel dizzy, which may be the severe form.
