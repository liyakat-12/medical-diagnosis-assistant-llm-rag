# Knee Giving Way

A knee that buckles or dives inward when you turn can mean a ligament is loose, the kneecap is unstable, or pain is making the thigh muscles switch off. Giving way after a sports twist needs a clinical check.

Avoid pivoting sport until assessed. A supportive brace and thigh strengthening can help some people after a clinician has examined the knee.

See a doctor for repeated giving way. Seek urgent care if the knee swells quickly after a pop and you cannot continue walking.
