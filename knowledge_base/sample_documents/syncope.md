# Fainting (Syncope)

Fainting is a brief loss of consciousness from reduced blood flow to the brain. Common harmless triggers are standing a long time, heat, pain, or the sight of blood, usually with warning nausea and tunnel vision first.

Lie the person flat and raise the legs. Recovery within a minute is typical. Afterwards they should drink water and stand up slowly.

See a doctor after any faint. Seek emergency care if fainting happened during exercise, without warning, with chest pain or palpitations, or if recovery took more than a minute.
