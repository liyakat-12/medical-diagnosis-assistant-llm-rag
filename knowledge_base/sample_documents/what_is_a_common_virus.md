# What a Simple Virus Does

Many everyday illnesses are viruses: a cold, a short fever, or a stomach bug. Antibiotics do not treat viruses. Symptoms usually peak then fade in days to a week or two.

Rest, fluids, and time. Stay home if you are feverish to protect others.

See a doctor if you are not improving as expected or you are at higher risk. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, confusion, or a non-blanching rash.
