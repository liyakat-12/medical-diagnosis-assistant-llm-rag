# Labyrinthitis

Labyrinthitis is inflammation of the inner ear, usually after a viral infection. It causes strong spinning dizziness, nausea, unsteadiness, and sometimes reduced hearing or ringing.

Most cases settle over one to three weeks. Resting in a quiet room, drinking fluids, and moving gradually rather than suddenly all help.

See a doctor to confirm the cause. Seek emergency care for dizziness with slurred speech, face or limb weakness, severe headache, or sudden total hearing loss.
