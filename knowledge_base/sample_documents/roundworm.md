# Intestinal Roundworm (Ascariasis)

Ascariasis is infection with large intestinal roundworms from contaminated soil or food. Light infections cause no symptoms; heavier ones cause vague abdominal pain, poor appetite, and a worm passed in stool or vomit.

A single prescribed deworming course usually clears it. Handwashing, washing vegetables, and safe sanitation prevent reinfection.

See a doctor if a worm is seen or the child is losing weight. Seek emergency care for severe cramping pain with vomiting, as worms can block the bowel.
