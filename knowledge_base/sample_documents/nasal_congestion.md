# Blocked Nose

A stuffy nose makes it hard to breathe through one or both sides. Colds, allergies, and swollen turbinates from long use of decongestant sprays are common causes. Sleep is often worse.

Saline rinses, steam, and sleeping slightly upright can ease a blocked nose. Do not use decongestant sprays for more than a few days, as they can worsen rebound blockage.

See a doctor for blockage on one side only that lasts, or a blocked nose with a bad smell or bleeding. Seek urgent care after a head injury if fluid leaks from the nose.
