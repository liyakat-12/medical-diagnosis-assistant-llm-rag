# Nappy Rash

Nappy rash is red sore skin in the nappy area caused by prolonged contact with urine and stool. The skin may look blotchy, feel warm, and be uncomfortable during changes.

Change nappies frequently, clean with water or fragrance-free wipes, dry gently, leave nappy-free time, and apply a thin barrier cream at each change.

See a doctor if the rash spreads into the skin folds with small red spots at the edge, which suggests thrush, or if there are blisters, pus, or fever.
