# Sore Nipples From a Bra

Sore or chafed nipples after a run or a poorly fitting bra are common. The skin can crack. A new inversion or a scaly nipple that does not heal needs a medical check.

A better-fitting sports bra, a barrier cream if a pharmacist agrees, and cotton next to the skin help.

See a doctor for a nipple change that lasts, discharge, or a lump. Seek urgent care for a red hot breast with fever.
