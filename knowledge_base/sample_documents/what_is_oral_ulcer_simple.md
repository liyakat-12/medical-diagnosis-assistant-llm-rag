# What a Mouth Ulcer Is

A mouth ulcer is a sore white or yellow patch with a red edge inside the mouth. Biting the cheek, stress, and some toothpastes can start one. Most heal in one to two weeks.

Avoid very spicy or salty food, and use a soft brush. A pharmacist can advise a suitable gel.

See a doctor or dentist for an ulcer lasting more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for a rapidly swelling tongue.
