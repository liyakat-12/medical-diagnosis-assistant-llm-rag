# Kawasaki Disease

Kawasaki disease affects young children and causes fever lasting five days or more with red eyes, cracked red lips and strawberry tongue, a rash, swollen hands and feet, and swollen neck glands.

It can damage the heart arteries if untreated. Hospital treatment with immunoglobulin and aspirin within ten days greatly reduces that risk.

Seek emergency care for a young child with fever lasting five days plus any of these features. Do not wait for the fever to break on its own.
