# Itchy Weals After a Food

Raised itchy weals after a food can be an allergic hive rash. They move around and fade within hours. Lip swelling or wheeze with hives is an emergency.

Stop the suspected food and use an antihistamine if a clinician or pharmacist agrees it is suitable. Photograph the rash.

Seek emergency care for breathing difficulty or lip or tongue swelling. See a doctor for repeated hives without a clear trigger.
