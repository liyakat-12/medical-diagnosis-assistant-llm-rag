# Heart Failure

Heart failure means the heart cannot pump well enough. Ankles swell, breathlessness comes on walking or lying flat, and weight can rise quickly.

A cardiologist uses scans and blood tests. Tablets, salt reduction, and daily weight checks are common parts of care.

See a doctor for new swelling and breathlessness. Seek emergency care if you wake gasping or cannot speak in sentences.
