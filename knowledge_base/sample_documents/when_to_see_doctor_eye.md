# When a Red Eye Needs Urgent Care

A mildly pink, gritty eye with normal vision can often wait for advice the same or next day. Pain, light hurting a lot, reduced vision, or a chemical splash cannot wait.

Do not wear contact lenses until the eye is better. Do not share towels if infection is possible.

See a clinician the same day for a painful red eye or reduced vision. Seek urgent care after a chemical splash or for sudden vision loss.
