# Hepatitis B

Hepatitis B is a viral liver infection spread by blood or sexual contact. Some people have tiredness, jaundice, and dark urine; others have no early symptoms.

A blood test confirms it. There is a vaccine. Chronic infection needs specialist follow-up to protect the liver.

See a doctor after possible exposure or jaundice. Seek urgent care for confusion or vomiting blood.
