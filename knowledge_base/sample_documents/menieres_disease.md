# Meniere Disease

Meniere disease causes attacks of severe spinning dizziness lasting twenty minutes to several hours, together with a feeling of fullness in one ear, fluctuating hearing loss, and ringing.

A clinician may suggest a lower-salt diet, medicines for the attacks, and balance therapy. Hearing should be checked regularly because it can decline over time.

See an ear specialist for repeated spinning attacks with hearing change. Seek urgent care if hearing drops suddenly in one ear.
