# Meniscus Tear

A meniscus tear is damage to the cartilage cushion in the knee, often from twisting on a planted foot. There is pain along the joint line, swelling over a day, clicking, and sometimes the knee locking or giving way.

Many tears settle with rest, ice, physiotherapy, and strengthening. Surgery is considered mainly when the knee truly locks or symptoms persist.

See a doctor if the knee locks, will not straighten, or swells immediately after an injury.
