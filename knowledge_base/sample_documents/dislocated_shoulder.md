# Dislocated Shoulder

A dislocated shoulder happens when the upper arm bone comes out of its socket, usually after a fall or sporting impact. The shoulder looks square rather than round, is extremely painful, and cannot be moved.

Do not try to push it back yourself, as this can damage nerves and blood vessels. Support the arm against the body and keep it still.

Go to an emergency department immediately, and especially urgently if the arm is numb, cold, pale, or pulseless.
