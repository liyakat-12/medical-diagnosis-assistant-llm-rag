# What Wear-and-Tear Joint Pain Is

Osteoarthritis is wear of the joint surface. The joint aches after use, can stiffen after a sit, and may creak. It is common in knees, hips, hands, and the spine.

Keep moving, strengthen the muscles around the joint, and use supportive shoes. Extra weight on the knees makes them work harder.

See a doctor if a joint swells or limits daily life. Seek urgent care for a single hot joint with fever.
