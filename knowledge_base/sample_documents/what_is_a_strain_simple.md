# What a Muscle Strain Is

A strain is an overstretched muscle. It hurts to use that muscle, and you may feel a pull during sport or a lift.

Rest the move that hurt, ice, and ease back into activity. Gentle movement is better than complete stillness after the first day.

See a doctor if you cannot walk or lift at all. Seek urgent care after a snap in the heel or a gap in the muscle.
