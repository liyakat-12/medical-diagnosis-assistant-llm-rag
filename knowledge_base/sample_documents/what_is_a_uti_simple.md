# What a Urine Infection Can Feel Like

A bladder infection often burns when you wee, makes you go often, and can ache low in the tummy. Cloudy or strong-smelling urine is common. Fever and back pain can mean the kidney.

Drink water and see a clinician. Antibiotics are used when a clinician confirms an infection. Cranberry is not a treatment for a clear infection.

See a doctor promptly for burning urine. Seek urgent care for fever, shaking, or back pain with this.
