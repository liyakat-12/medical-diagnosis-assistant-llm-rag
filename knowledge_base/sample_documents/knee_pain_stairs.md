# Knee Pain When Climbing Stairs

A common complaint is my knee hurts when I climb stairs or go downstairs. Pain under or around the kneecap often relates to how the kneecap tracks, weak thigh muscles, or early cartilage wear. Going down stairs is often worse than going up.

Try a handrail, shorter steps, strengthening the front of the thigh, and avoiding deep squats for a while. Ice after stairs and activity that does not force a full bend can help.

See a doctor if stair pain is getting worse, the knee swells after every flight, or you are over 50 with night pain. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot straighten the knee.
