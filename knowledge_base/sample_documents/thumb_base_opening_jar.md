# Pain at the Base of the Thumb Opening a Jar

Pain at the thumb base when you open a jar or pinch is a common tendon or joint pattern. It often starts after extra phone or tool use.

Use a jar opener, rest the pinch, and ice after a flare. A soft thumb splint can help some people.

See a doctor if you drop cups or the thumb base stays swollen. Seek urgent care after a fall if the thumb looks out of place.
