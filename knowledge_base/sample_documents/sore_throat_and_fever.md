# Sore Throat and Fever

A sore throat with fever is common with viral infections. People say I have a fever and sore throat. Swallowing hurts, the neck may feel tender, and you may also have a runny nose or cough. Strep throat is more likely without a cough and with tender neck glands, but only a clinician can decide if a test is needed.

Rest, cool drinks, and simple pain relief if safe for you usually help. Most sore throats improve within a week. Antibiotics are not needed for most viral throats.

See a doctor if you cannot swallow saliva, the fever lasts more than three days, or a rash appears. Seek emergency care for drooling, noisy breathing, or a very swollen neck.
