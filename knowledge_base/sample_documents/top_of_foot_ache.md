# Pain on the Top of the Foot

The top of the foot can ache from tight laces, new shoes, or extra walking. A stress on the long foot bones can start the same way after a sudden mileage jump.

Loosen laces, use a wider pair, and rest from impact for a few days.

See a doctor if the top of the foot stays swollen or hurts at rest. Seek urgent care after a crush injury or if the foot is cold and numb.
