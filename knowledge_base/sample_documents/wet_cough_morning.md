# Chesty Cough First Thing

Bringing up mucus first thing can happen after a cold, with post-nasal drip, or in people who smoke. A daily morning cough for months needs a medical review.

Fluids, sitting upright, and not smoking help. Steam can loosen mucus. Green mucus alone does not prove you need antibiotics.

See a doctor for a morning cough that lasts more than three weeks or you cough blood. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness.
