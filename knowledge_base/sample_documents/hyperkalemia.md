# High Potassium (Hyperkalaemia)

High blood potassium often causes no symptoms until it is dangerous. When present, symptoms are muscle weakness, tiredness, numbness, and palpitations from an irregular heartbeat.

It is most common in kidney disease and with certain blood pressure medicines. It can stop the heart, so it is treated urgently in hospital.

Seek emergency care for palpitations with weakness in anyone with kidney disease. Have blood tests as advised when taking medicines that raise potassium.
