# Neck Pain From Looking Down at a Phone

Holding a phone in the lap pulls the head forward and loads the neck joints and muscles. People feel an ache at the base of the skull or across the shoulders after scrolling.

Raise the phone, take breaks, and tuck the chin gently rather than poking the head forward. Stretching the chest and walking helps.

See a doctor if phone-related neck pain lasts or you have arm tingling. Seek emergency care for neck pain after a crash or with fever and a severe headache.
