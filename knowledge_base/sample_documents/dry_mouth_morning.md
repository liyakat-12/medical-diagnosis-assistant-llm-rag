# Very Dry Mouth on Waking

A sticky dry mouth first thing often follows mouth breathing from a blocked nose, snoring, or some medicines. Dry mouth raises the risk of tooth decay.

Sip water, treat a blocked nose, and tell a dentist if it lasts. Sugar-free gum can help saliva if your dentist agrees.

See a doctor if dry mouth lasts or you have unusual thirst and frequent urine. Seek urgent care for a dry mouth with confusion and no urine.
