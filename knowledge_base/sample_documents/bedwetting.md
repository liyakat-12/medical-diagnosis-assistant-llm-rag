# Bedwetting (Nocturnal Enuresis)

Bedwetting is involuntary urination during sleep. It is normal up to about age five and often runs in families. The child is not being lazy and should never be punished for it.

Helpful steps are regular daytime drinks, no large drinks or caffeine in the evening, emptying the bladder before bed, and a reward chart. A bedwetting alarm works well from about age seven.

See a doctor if a previously dry child starts wetting again, or if there is daytime wetting, pain, excessive thirst, or constipation.
