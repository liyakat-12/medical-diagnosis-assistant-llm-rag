# Food Repeating Into the Throat

A mouthful of food or sour fluid coming back up without much effort can be reflux or a hiatus hernia. True vomiting is more forceful. Food sticking on the way down needs a prompt check.

Eat smaller meals, stay upright, and raise the head of the bed. Avoid tight belts after food.

See a doctor if food comes back often or sticks. Seek emergency care for chest pain or vomiting blood.
