# Lower Tummy Ache

A dull or cramping ache below the navel can be wind, constipation, a urine infection, period pain, or a gut upset. The location and extra symptoms matter more than the word ache alone.

Note whether the pain relates to meals, stool, urine, or periods. Heat and rest help some cramps. Do not take leftover strong pain tablets.

See a doctor for pain lasting more than a few days. Seek emergency care for sudden severe pain, fainting, a rigid tummy, or pain with vomiting and fever.
