# Hidradenitis Suppurativa

This condition causes painful deep lumps and boil-like swellings in the armpits, groin, and under the breasts. They can burst, leak, and heal into tunnels and scars, and they keep coming back.

It is not caused by poor hygiene. Treatment includes stopping smoking, weight management, antiseptic washes, long-term antibiotics, and specialist medicines or surgery.

See a dermatologist for recurring painful lumps in these areas rather than repeatedly treating them as simple boils.
