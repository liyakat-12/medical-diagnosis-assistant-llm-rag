# Bladder Stones

Bladder stones cause lower abdominal pain, a burning feeling when passing urine, a stream that starts and stops suddenly, cloudy or bloody urine, and needing to pass urine often.

They usually form when the bladder does not empty fully, such as with an enlarged prostate. Treatment is to break up or remove the stone and fix the underlying cause.

See a doctor for an interrupted urine stream with pain. Seek emergency care if you cannot pass urine at all or develop fever with chills.
