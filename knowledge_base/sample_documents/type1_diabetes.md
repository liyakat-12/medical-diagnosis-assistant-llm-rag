# Type 1 Diabetes

Type 1 diabetes happens when the pancreas stops making insulin. Thirst, lots of urine, weight loss, and tiredness can appear over days or weeks.

Lifelong insulin, prescribed and adjusted by a diabetes team, is required. It is not caused by eating sugar.

See a doctor promptly for these symptoms, especially in a child. Seek emergency care for vomiting, deep breathing, or confusion.
