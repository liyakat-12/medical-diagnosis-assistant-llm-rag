# Chronic Pancreatitis

Chronic pancreatitis is long-term inflammation of the pancreas, most often from heavy alcohol use. It causes repeated upper abdominal pain going through to the back, greasy floating stools, weight loss, and later diabetes.

Care includes stopping alcohol and smoking completely, a low-fat diet, enzyme supplements with meals, and a clinician-led pain plan.

See a doctor for repeated episodes of upper abdominal pain. Seek emergency care for severe constant pain with vomiting and fever.
