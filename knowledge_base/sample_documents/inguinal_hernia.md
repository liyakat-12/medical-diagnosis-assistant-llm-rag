# Inguinal Hernia

An inguinal hernia is a bulge in the groin that may appear when you cough or stand. It can ache after lifting.

A clinician confirms it. Surgery is often advised if the bulge grows or hurts. Do not try to push a painful stuck bulge repeatedly.

See a doctor for a groin bulge. Seek emergency care if the bulge becomes red, very painful, and will not reduce.
