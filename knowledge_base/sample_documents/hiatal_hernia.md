# Hiatal Hernia

A hiatal hernia is when part of the stomach slides up through the diaphragm. Many people have no symptoms; others get heartburn, regurgitation, burping, and chest discomfort after meals or when lying flat.

Smaller meals, not lying down for three hours after eating, raising the head of the bed, weight loss, and acid-reducing medicine control most cases. Surgery is reserved for severe cases.

See a doctor for frequent heartburn. Seek emergency care for sudden severe chest or upper abdominal pain with vomiting and inability to burp.
