# Reduced Smell During a Cold

A blocked nose from a cold commonly dulls smell and taste. Smell usually returns as the swelling settles over days to a couple of weeks.

Saline rinses and time help. Stay aware of smoke and gas alarms while smell is reduced.

See a doctor if smell has not returned after a few weeks or the loss was sudden without a cold. Seek urgent care for a head injury with clear fluid from the nose.
