# Shigellosis (Bacillary Dysentery)

Shigella is a highly contagious gut infection that causes fever, cramping pain, and diarrhoea that often contains blood or mucus. It spreads easily between children and within families.

Rehydration is the main care. A clinician may prescribe antibiotics for severe cases, young children, or people with weak immunity. Anti-diarrhoeal tablets should be avoided.

See a doctor for bloody diarrhoea with fever. Seek emergency care for severe dehydration, seizures in a child, or confusion.
