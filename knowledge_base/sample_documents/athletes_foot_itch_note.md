# Itchy Peeling Skin Between the Toes

Itchy, peeling skin between the toes after sweaty shoes is often athlete's foot, a fungal infection. The skin can split and sting.

Dry carefully, use an antifungal cream or powder a pharmacist agrees is suitable, and rotate shoes.

See a doctor if you have diabetes or the rash spreads to the foot. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
