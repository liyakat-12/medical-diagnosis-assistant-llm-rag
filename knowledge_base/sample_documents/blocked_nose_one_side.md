# Blocked Nose on One Side Only

A cold can block one side more than the other for a few days. A blockage that is only ever one side, lasts weeks, or comes with bloody mucus needs a check for other causes.

Saline and time help a typical cold. Do not keep using decongestant spray for weeks.

See a doctor for one-sided blockage lasting more than three weeks or bloody discharge. Seek urgent care after a head injury if fluid leaks from the nose.
