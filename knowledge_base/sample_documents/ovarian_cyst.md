# Ovarian Cyst

Many ovarian cysts are fluid sacs that come and go with the cycle. Some cause one-sided pelvic ache or bloating.

Ultrasound checks the type. Simple cysts are often watched. Hormone treatment or surgery is used if they persist.

See a doctor for pelvic pain that lasts. Seek emergency care for sudden severe one-sided pain with fainting.
