# When to See a Doctor for a Cough

A cough after a cold can last three weeks and still be ordinary. You should still get checked if a cough lasts longer than three to four weeks, you cough blood, lose weight, or feel breathless at rest.

This note is about timing, not naming a disease. Keep a simple diary of when the cough is worse, what comes up, and whether you smoke.

See a doctor for a cough lasting more than three to four weeks. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness, chest pain, or coughing large amounts of blood.
