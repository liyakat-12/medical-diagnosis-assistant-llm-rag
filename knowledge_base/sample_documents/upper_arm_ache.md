# Ache in the Upper Arm

The upper arm often aches from a sore shoulder tendon, a vaccine, or extra lifting. Pain that spreads from the chest into the arm with sweat is not a simple strain.

Rest overhead work, ice after activity, and keep the elbow moving so the arm does not stiffen.

See a doctor if the arm ache lasts more than two weeks. Seek emergency care for arm pain with chest pressure, breathlessness, or faintness.
