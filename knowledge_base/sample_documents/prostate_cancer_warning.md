# Prostate Cancer Warning Signs

Early prostate cancer usually has no symptoms. Later signs are difficulty starting urine, a weak stream, getting up often at night, blood in urine or semen, and new persistent back or hip pain.

These symptoms are far more often caused by a benign enlarged prostate, but they should be checked. Assessment includes an examination and a PSA blood test.

See a doctor for new urinary symptoms over age fifty, or earlier with a family history. Seek urgent care for inability to pass urine or new leg weakness with back pain.
