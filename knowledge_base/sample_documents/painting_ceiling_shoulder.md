# Shoulder Pain After Painting a Ceiling

Long spells with the arms up can inflame the shoulder tendons. Night pain is common the next day.

Use a longer handle, take breaks, and ice after overhead work. Sleep with a pillow under the arm.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm after two weeks. Seek urgent care if the shoulder looks out of place.
