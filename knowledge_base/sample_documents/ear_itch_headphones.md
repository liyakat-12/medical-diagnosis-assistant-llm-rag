# Sore Ears From Headphones

The ear canal or the fold of the ear can get sore and itchy from long headphone or earbud use. Sweat and rubbing inflame the skin. Early swimmer's ear feels similar.

Take headphone breaks, keep the ears dry, and stop using buds if the canal hurts. Do not scratch with a cotton bud.

See a doctor if the canal becomes very painful or swollen. Seek urgent care for fever and a very tender bone behind the ear.
