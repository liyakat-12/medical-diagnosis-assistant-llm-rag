# Sore Neck After a Hotel Bed

An unfamiliar mattress and pillow can leave the neck and back stiff after travel. It usually eases in a day or two at home.

Walk, use heat, and pack a familiar small pillow if you travel often.

See a doctor if travel stiffness lasts more than a week. Seek emergency care after a crash or with fever and a stiff neck.
