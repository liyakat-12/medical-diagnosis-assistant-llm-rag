# Gnawing Hunger Pain

A hollow gnawing in the upper tummy when you skip a meal is often just hunger. An ulcer can also gnaw and may ease after food, so lasting night pain needs a review.

Eat regular meals and avoid long gaps. Notice if pain wakes you at night.

See a doctor for lasting night pain, black stools, or vomiting blood. Seek emergency care for sudden severe abdominal pain.
