# Perianal Abscess

A perianal abscess is a pocket of pus near the back passage. It causes constant throbbing pain that is worse on sitting, a tender hot swelling, and often fever.

Antibiotics alone rarely clear it. A surgeon needs to drain the pus, usually as a small procedure, after which pain settles quickly.

See a doctor the same day. Seek emergency care for high fever, spreading redness, or feeling very unwell, especially with diabetes.
