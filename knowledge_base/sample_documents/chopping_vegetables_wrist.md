# Wrist Pain After Chopping Food

A long chop can flare the thumb tendons or the wrist. The ache is often on the thumb side.

Use a sharper knife, a heavier blade that does the work, and take breaks. Ice after cooking.

See a doctor if the thumb side stays swollen. Seek urgent care if you cannot hold a cup.
