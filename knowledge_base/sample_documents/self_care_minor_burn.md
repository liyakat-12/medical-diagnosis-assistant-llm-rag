# Looking After a Small Burn

A small red burn smaller than the palm, with no blister across a large area, can often be cooled at home. Chemical and electrical burns need urgent care.

Hold the area under cool running water for 20 minutes, remove tight rings, and cover loosely with cling film. Do not use ice, butter, or toothpaste.

Seek urgent care for burns on the face, hands, or groin, any blistered burn larger than the palm, or burns in a child or older adult.
