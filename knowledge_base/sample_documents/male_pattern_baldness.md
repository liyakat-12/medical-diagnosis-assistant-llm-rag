# Male Pattern Hair Loss

Male pattern hair loss is gradual thinning that starts at the temples and crown, driven by genetics and hormones. The scalp skin stays healthy and there is no itching or scaling.

Treatments such as topical minoxidil can slow loss and partly regrow hair but must be continued to keep the benefit. Hair transplant is an option for some.

See a doctor if hair falls out in patches, comes out in handfuls, or comes with scalp scaling, redness, or scarring, as that is a different problem.
