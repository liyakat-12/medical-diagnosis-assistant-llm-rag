# Headache When You Have Not Drunk Enough

A dull all-over headache with a dry mouth and darker urine often follows missed drinks, heat, or exercise. Many everyday headaches ease after water and food.

Sip water steadily, rest in a cool place, and eat a small meal. This does not prove every headache is dehydration.

See a doctor if headaches keep returning. Seek emergency care for a thunderclap headache, confusion, or no urine for many hours.
