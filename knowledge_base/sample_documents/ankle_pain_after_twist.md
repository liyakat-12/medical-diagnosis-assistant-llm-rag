# Ankle Pain After a Twist

Rolling the ankle on a kerb stretches the outer ligaments. Swelling and bruising can appear the next day. You can often still take some steps after a mild sprain.

Rest from running, ice, a light bandage, and elevation for the first two days. Then start gentle circles so the ankle does not stiffen.

See a doctor if you cannot take four steps or there is a large swelling. Seek urgent care if the ankle looks out of place.
