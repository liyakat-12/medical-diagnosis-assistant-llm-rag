# Lower Back Pain After Standing

Aching in the lower back after standing at a counter or queue is common. The back muscles fatigue, and a sway-back posture can pinch the joints. Pain often eases when you sit or walk.

Shift weight from foot to foot, use a small footstool to rest one foot, and walk short loops. Supportive shoes help more than standing still on a hard floor.

See a doctor if standing pain is constant or wakes you. Seek emergency care for back pain with weak legs or loss of bladder control.
