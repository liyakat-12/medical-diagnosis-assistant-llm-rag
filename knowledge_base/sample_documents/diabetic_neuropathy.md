# Diabetic Neuropathy

Long-term high blood sugar damages nerves, most often in the feet. There is numbness, tingling, burning pain at night, and loss of the ability to feel injuries, which allows unnoticed ulcers to form.

Keeping blood sugar in range slows the damage. Daily foot checks, well-fitting shoes, and prescribed nerve-pain medicine are the main care.

See a doctor for new foot numbness with diabetes. Seek urgent care for any foot ulcer, black skin, or a hot swollen foot.
