# Giardiasis

Giardiasis is a gut parasite infection spread through contaminated water or food. It causes greasy foul-smelling diarrhoea, bloating, excess wind, stomach cramps, and tiredness lasting more than a week.

Unlike simple food poisoning it rarely clears quickly on its own. A stool test confirms it and a short prescribed antiparasitic course treats it.

See a doctor for diarrhoea lasting more than seven days, especially after travel or drinking untreated water. Seek urgent care for signs of dehydration.
