# Prickly Heat on the Chest

Tiny itchy bumps on the chest and neck in hot weather are often blocked sweat ducts. They sting when you sweat more.

Cool the room, wear loose cotton, and rinse sweat off. Do not use thick oily cream on the rash.

See a doctor if the rash looks infected. Seek emergency care for heat confusion or fainting.
