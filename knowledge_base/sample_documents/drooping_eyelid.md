# A Drooping Eyelid

A lid that sits lower than the other can be long-standing or new. A new droop with double vision, a weak face, or a severe headache needs urgent care. A tired twitchy lid is different.

Photograph the face in good light to show a clinician how sudden it was. Do not ignore a new droop.

See a doctor for a new drooping lid. Seek emergency care for a droop with face, arm, or speech changes, or a sudden severe headache.
