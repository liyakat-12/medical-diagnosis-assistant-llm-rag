# Sore or Bleeding Gums

Gums that bleed when you brush and feel swollen are often gingivitis from plaque. They can look red and tender. Bleeding is a sign to clean more carefully, not to stop brushing.

Brush twice a day along the gum line, clean between teeth, and see a dentist or hygienist. Smoking makes gum disease worse.

See a dentist for lasting gum pain or loose teeth. Seek urgent care for rapidly spreading facial swelling.
