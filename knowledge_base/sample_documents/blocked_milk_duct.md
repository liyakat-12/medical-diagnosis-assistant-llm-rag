# A Tender Lump While Breastfeeding

A sore lump in a feeding breast can be a blocked duct. If the area becomes red and you feel flu-like, it may be mastitis.

Feed often, start on the sore side if you can, and use a warm cloth before a feed. Do not stop feeding suddenly unless a clinician says to.

See a doctor promptly for a red painful breast or fever. Seek urgent care if you feel very unwell or the redness spreads fast.
