# Stye on the Eyelid

A tender red lump on the eyelid edge is often a stye, a blocked gland. It can look like a pimple and make the lid feel heavy.

A clean warm compress several times a day helps many styes drain. Do not squeeze. Leave contact lenses out.

See a clinician if the stye does not improve in a few days, vision is affected, or the whole lid is very swollen. Seek urgent care for a spreading red eyelid with fever.
