# Mind That Will Not Switch Off at Night

Replaying the day in bed is common anxiety. The body stays alert and sleep will not come. A few nights happen to most people. Lasting nightly worry needs support.

Write worries earlier in the evening, keep the room for sleep, and get up at a regular time. Cut late news scrolling if it winds you up.

See a doctor or talking-therapy service if worry is daily. Seek urgent help for thoughts of harming yourself.
