# Waking Often at Night

Waking several times can follow noise, a full bladder, reflux, pain, or racing thoughts. A few nights after stress are common. Lasting broken sleep needs a look at both habits and health.

Keep the room dark and cool, reduce evening fluid if you are up to urinate, and avoid checking the clock. Get up at the same time even after a bad night.

See a doctor if you snore, choke in sleep, or broken sleep lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care for chest pain that wakes you.
