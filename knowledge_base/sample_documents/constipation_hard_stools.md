# Hard Stools and Straining

Hard pebble stools, straining, and going less often than usual is constipation. Low fluid, little fibre, travel, and some pain tablets make it more likely. A few days of change is common.

Drink more water, add fruit, vegetables, and oats, and walk daily. Do not ignore the urge to go. Pharmacy fibre or stool softeners can help if a pharmacist agrees.

See a doctor if constipation lasts more than three weeks, you have blood, or you lose weight. Seek urgent care for no stool and no wind with a swollen painful tummy.
