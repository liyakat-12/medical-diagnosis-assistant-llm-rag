# Common Reasons for Everyday Tiredness

Poor sleep, a leftover virus, low iron, thyroid problems, low mood, and simply doing too much can all cause tiredness. Tiredness alone does not name one disease.

A regular sleep window, meals, daylight, and a note of other symptoms help a clinician.

See a doctor if tiredness lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care for chest pain or fainting with severe paleness.
