# Cramps With a First Period

The first periods can bring a low tummy and back ache. This is common. Soaking through a pad every hour is not ordinary.

A warm cloth, rest, and age-appropriate pain relief a pharmacist can advise if suitable. Gentle walking helps some people.

See a doctor for pain that keeps a young person off school every month. Seek urgent care for fainting or soaking through every hour.
