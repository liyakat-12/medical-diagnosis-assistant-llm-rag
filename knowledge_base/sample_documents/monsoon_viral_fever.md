# Viral Fever in the Monsoon

A fever with body aches after rain and crowding is often a simple virus. In some areas the same pattern can be dengue or malaria, so lasting fever needs a test, not a guess.

Rest, drink fluids, and use paracetamol for fever if it is safe for you. Avoid aspirin or ibuprofen if dengue is possible until a clinician advises.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than two days in the monsoon. Seek emergency care for bleeding, severe belly pain, fainting, or confusion.
