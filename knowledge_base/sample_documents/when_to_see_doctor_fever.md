# When a Fever Needs a Doctor

A short fever with a cold can be cared for at home in many healthy adults. You should still get checked if fever lasts more than three days, returns after seeming better, or you are pregnant, older, or have a long-term illness.

This note is about timing, not naming an infection. Keep fluids up and note any rash, neck stiffness, or breathlessness.

See a doctor for a fever lasting more than three days or if you are at higher risk. Seek emergency care for a non-blanching rash, confusion, or severe breathlessness.
