# Room-Spinning Dizzy When Turning in Bed

A brief spin of the room when you roll over or look up is a common inner-ear crystal problem called BPPV. It lasts seconds and can make you feel sick. It is different from feeling faint.

Sit up slowly. A clinician can teach a repositioning manoeuvre. Do not drive while you are spinning.

See a doctor for repeated spinning attacks. Seek emergency care for dizziness with a weak face or arm, double vision, or a sudden severe headache.
