# Loss of Appetite

Not wanting food for a day or two during a cold or stress is common. Lasting loss of appetite with weight loss needs a medical look, because infection, low mood, medicines, and other illnesses can all reduce hunger.

Offer small regular meals, fluids, and foods you still enjoy. Do not force large plates. Note any fever, pain, or swallowing trouble.

See a doctor if appetite stays low for more than two weeks or clothes become loose. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood or black stools.
