# Feeling Full After a Few Bites

Feeling full after a few bites can follow a virus, anxiety, reflux, or simply eating too fast. Lasting early fullness with weight loss needs a medical look.

Eat smaller, more frequent meals and chew well. Do not force large plates.

See a doctor if early fullness lasts more than two weeks or clothes become loose. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood or black stools.
