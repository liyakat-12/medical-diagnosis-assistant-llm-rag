# Sore Throat After Shouting

A raw throat after a sports match or concert is voice-box and throat strain. You may be hoarse. Fever is usually absent, unlike a viral sore throat.

Rest the voice, drink fluids, and avoid whispering. Steam can soothe.

See a doctor if the throat is still very sore after a week or you cannot swallow. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
