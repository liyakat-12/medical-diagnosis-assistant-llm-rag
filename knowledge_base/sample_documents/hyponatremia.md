# Low Sodium (Hyponatraemia)

Hyponatraemia means the blood sodium level is too low. Mild cases cause nausea, headache, and tiredness; as it worsens there is confusion, muscle cramps, drowsiness, and seizures.

Causes include certain medicines such as diuretics, heart or liver disease, and drinking very large amounts of water. Correction must be done slowly in hospital.

Seek emergency care for confusion, drowsiness, or seizures. Do not try to correct it by drinking more water, which makes it worse.
