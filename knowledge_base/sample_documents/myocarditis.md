# Myocarditis

Myocarditis is inflammation of the heart muscle, usually viral. It causes chest pain, unusual breathlessness, a racing or irregular heartbeat, severe tiredness, and swollen ankles, often days after a viral illness.

It can weaken the heart quickly. Hospital assessment with an ECG, blood tests, and a heart scan is needed, and strenuous exercise must be stopped.

Seek emergency care for chest pain or breathlessness after a viral illness, and immediately for fainting or a very fast irregular pulse.
