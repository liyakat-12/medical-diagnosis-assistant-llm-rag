# Wrist Pain After a Fall on the Hand

Falling onto an outstretched hand can sprain ligaments or break a bone, including a scaphoid fracture that does not always show on a first X-ray. Swelling in the hollow at the base of the thumb is a warning.

Ice, a supportive splint, and elevation reduce swelling while you get checked. Do not keep using a very painful wrist as if it were only a bruise.

See a clinician the same day after a fall if the wrist is swollen or you cannot grip. Seek urgent care if the wrist looks bent.
