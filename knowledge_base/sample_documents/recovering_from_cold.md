# Still Blocked After the Worst of a Cold

A stuffy nose and leftover cough can linger after the fever and aches have gone. This recovery phase is still a cold, not automatically a sinus infection needing antibiotics.

Saline, rest, and fluids help the last few days. Return to usual activity when you feel able and fever has gone.

See a doctor if you worsen after seeming better, or symptoms last more than ten days and are getting worse. Seek emergency care for breathlessness.
