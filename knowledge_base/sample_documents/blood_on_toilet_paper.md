# Bright Blood on Toilet Paper

Bright red blood on the paper after a hard stool is often piles or a small tear. Dark blood mixed through the stool, or blood with weight loss, needs a prompt check. Never assume bleeding is only piles.

Drink more fluid, add fibre, and avoid straining. Soften the stool so the tear or pile can settle.

See a doctor for any new rectal bleeding so a serious cause is not missed. Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding, fainting, or black tarry stools.
