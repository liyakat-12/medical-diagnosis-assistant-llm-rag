# Food Stuck Between Teeth

Meat or fibre lodging between two teeth can make the gum sore. A gap from gum disease or a broken filling makes this more likely. The gum can swell until the food is removed.

Use floss or interdental brushes gently. Do not use a sharp pin. See a dentist if food always packs in the same spot.

See a dentist for a lasting sore gum or a broken tooth. Seek urgent care for spreading facial swelling.
