# Shin Splints

Shin splints cause aching pain along the inner edge of the shin bone, usually in runners who increase distance or intensity too quickly or train on hard surfaces.

Reduce running volume, switch to low-impact exercise, ice the area, use supportive shoes, and strengthen the calf and hip muscles before returning gradually.

See a doctor for a sharp pain in one specific spot that hurts even at rest or at night, which may be a stress fracture rather than shin splints.
