# Essential Tremor

Essential tremor is a rhythmic shaking that appears when using the hands, such as holding a cup or writing, and settles at rest. It often runs in families and may affect the head or voice.

It is different from Parkinson disease, where the tremor is worst at rest. Medicines can reduce it, and caffeine reduction helps some people.

See a doctor if the tremor affects eating, writing, or work, or if it starts suddenly or affects only one side.
