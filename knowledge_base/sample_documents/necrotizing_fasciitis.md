# Necrotizing Fasciitis

This is a rapidly spreading deep infection. Pain is out of proportion to the skin change. The area may look dusky, and the person becomes very unwell.

Emergency surgery and antibiotics are life-saving. It is not ordinary cellulitis.

Call emergency services for severe pain after a small wound with rapid darkening of the skin and fever.
