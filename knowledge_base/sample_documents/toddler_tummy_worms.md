# Itchy Bottom and Tummy in a Toddler

Threadworms can itch the bottom, especially at night, and can make a child restless. The tummy may ache. You may see tiny white threads.

Wash hands, keep nails short, and wash bedding. Treatment is usually a pharmacy or prescribed medicine for the household.

See a pharmacist or doctor for suspected worms. Seek urgent care for a rigid tummy, green vomit, or a child who is unusually drowsy.
