# Stiff Neck After Sleeping With Wet Hair

A damp cold pillow can tighten the neck muscles overnight. Wet hair does not cause an infection by itself, but the stiff nape is real.

Dry the hair before bed, use heat in the morning, and keep the neck moving gently.

See a doctor if you cannot turn the head after a week. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck with fever.
