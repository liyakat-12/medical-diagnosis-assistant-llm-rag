# Cluster Headache

Cluster headache is a severe one-sided pain around the eye, with a watery eye and a blocked nostril on that side. Attacks come in bouts.

This is not an ordinary tension headache. A clinician can offer oxygen or prescribed attack medicine. Do not drive during an attack.

See a doctor for a first severe one-sided eye pain. Seek emergency care if it is the worst headache of your life with fever or a stiff neck.
