# Bad Breath (Halitosis)

Persistent bad breath is usually caused by bacteria on the tongue and between the teeth, dry mouth, or strong foods. Gum disease and smoking make it worse.

Brush twice daily, clean between the teeth, scrape the tongue, drink water regularly, and limit smoking. Most cases improve within a couple of weeks.

See a dentist or doctor if bad breath continues despite good cleaning, as it can point to gum disease, sinus infection, reflux, or uncontrolled diabetes.
