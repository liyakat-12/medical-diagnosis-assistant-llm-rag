# Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS)

ME/CFS is severe tiredness lasting more than six months that is not relieved by rest. The key feature is a crash of symptoms a day or two after even mild activity, along with unrefreshing sleep, brain fog, and dizziness on standing.

There is no quick cure. Care focuses on pacing activity to stay inside your energy limit, treating sleep and pain, and avoiding push-and-crash cycles.

See a doctor so that treatable causes such as anaemia, thyroid problems, and sleep apnoea are excluded first.
