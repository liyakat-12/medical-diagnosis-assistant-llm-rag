# Bitter Taste at the Back of the Throat

A bitter or sour splash at the back of the throat is typical reflux. It can happen without much burning. Hoarseness and a morning cough can go with it.

Smaller meals, not lying down after food, and raising the head of the bed help. Pharmacy antacids may help if suitable.

See a doctor for a bitter taste most days or food sticking. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain.
