# Tooth Decay (Dental Caries)

Tooth decay is damage to the tooth surface caused by acid from bacteria feeding on sugar. Early decay causes no pain; later there is sensitivity to sweet or cold, a visible hole, or a dull toothache.

A dentist removes the decay and places a filling. Brushing twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and cutting sugary drinks prevents new cavities.

See a dentist for any hole, persistent sensitivity, or toothache. Seek urgent care for facial swelling or fever, which suggests the infection has spread.
