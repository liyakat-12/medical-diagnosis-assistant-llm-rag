# Sore Arm After a Vaccination

A sore, heavy upper arm for a day or two after a jab is common. Mild fever and tiredness can happen. Trouble breathing or a widespread rash soon after is not ordinary soreness.

Move the arm gently, use a cool cloth, and rest. Age-appropriate paracetamol can help if a clinician or the vaccine leaflet says it is suitable.

See a doctor if the arm is still very red and hot after a few days. Seek emergency care for trouble breathing, facial swelling, or collapse after a vaccine.
