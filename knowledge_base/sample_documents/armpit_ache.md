# Pain in the Armpit

A tender armpit can be a strained muscle, a blocked sweat gland, or a swollen gland after a cold. A new hard lump that does not settle needs a clinical check.

Avoid deodorant on broken skin and do not squeeze a boil. A warm cloth can ease a sore gland.

See a doctor for an armpit lump that lasts more than two weeks. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading red hot area with fever.
