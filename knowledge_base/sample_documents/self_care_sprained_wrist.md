# Looking After a Mildly Sprained Wrist

A wrist that is sore and a little puffy after a twist, but still looks straight, can often be rested at home after you are sure it is not broken. You can usually still move the fingers.

Ice, a supportive splint for a few days, and elevation help. Then start gentle movement so the wrist does not stiffen.

See a clinician after a fall if swelling is large or you cannot grip. Seek urgent care if the wrist looks bent.
