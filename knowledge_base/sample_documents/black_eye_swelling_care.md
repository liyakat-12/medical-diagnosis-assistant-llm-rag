# Swelling After a Knock Near the Eye

Puffiness and colour around the eye after a knock can worsen for a day. You should still be able to see clearly. Double vision or blood in front of the iris needs urgent care.

A cold pack in a cloth and sleeping slightly upright reduce swelling. Do not press the eyeball.

See a clinician the same day for any vision change. Seek emergency care for vomiting after a head bump or a pupil that looks odd.
