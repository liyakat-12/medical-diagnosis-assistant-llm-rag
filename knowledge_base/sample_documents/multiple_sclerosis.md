# Multiple Sclerosis

MS can cause episodes of numbness, vision blur in one eye, weakness, or unsteadiness that last days. Symptoms may ease then return.

A neurologist uses MRI and clinical signs. Disease-modifying treatment started early can reduce relapses.

See a doctor for a first episode of one-sided vision loss or unexplained weakness. Seek emergency care for sudden inability to walk.
