# Tired Eyes After Reading

Sore, heavy eyes and a dull forehead after reading or close work are eye strain. You may rub the eyes and find the words blur. Uncorrected glasses needs can add to this.

Look into the distance every 20 minutes, improve lighting, and blink. An eye test is useful if this keeps happening.

See an optometrist if reading strain is new or vision is changing. Seek urgent care for sudden vision loss or a painful red eye with light hurting.
