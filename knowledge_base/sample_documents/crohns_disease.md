# Crohn's Disease

Crohn's can inflame any part of the gut. Pain, diarrhoea, weight loss, and tiredness are common. Mouth ulcers or joint pain may occur.

Specialist drugs and diet support keep many people well. Smoking worsens Crohn's.

See a doctor for lasting gut symptoms and weight loss. Seek emergency care for a rigid abdomen or high fever.
