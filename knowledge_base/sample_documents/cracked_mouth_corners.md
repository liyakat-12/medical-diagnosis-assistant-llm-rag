# Cracks at the Corners of the Mouth

Sore splits at the corners of the mouth can be saliva collecting, ill-fitting dentures, or a yeast or bacterial infection of the folds. Licking the corners makes them worse.

Keep the corners dry, use a plain barrier ointment if a pharmacist agrees, and see a dentist if dentures do not fit.

See a doctor if cracks last more than two weeks or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for rapidly spreading redness.
