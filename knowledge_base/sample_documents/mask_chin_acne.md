# Spots Where a Mask Rubs

Sweat and rub under a mask can flare spots on the chin and the bridge of the nose. This is a common everyday flare, not a new disease.

Use a clean mask, wash the face gently, and do not pick. Moisturise if the skin is dry.

See a doctor if spots become painful cysts. Seek urgent care for a very swollen red face with fever.
