# Jet Lag

Jet lag is a sleep-timing problem after flying across several time zones. People feel sleepy by day, wake at night, and may have a dull headache.

Get daylight in the new morning, keep the first day active, and shift sleep toward local time. Short naps can help. Avoid heavy caffeine late in the day.

See a doctor if sleep problems last more than two weeks after travel or you cannot stay safe while driving.
