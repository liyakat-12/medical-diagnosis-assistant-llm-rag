# Frostbite

Frostbite is freezing of skin and deeper tissue, usually on fingers, toes, ears, nose, and cheeks. The skin becomes white or greyish-yellow, feels hard or waxy, and goes numb.

Move indoors, remove wet clothing, and rewarm in warm, not hot, water. Do not rub the area, use direct heat, or let it refreeze after thawing.

Call emergency services for numb hard pale skin, blisters, or black tissue. Frostbite and hypothermia often occur together.
