# Traveler's Diarrhea

Traveler's diarrhea is sudden loose stools, cramps, and urgency after food or water abroad. Fever is usually low.

Oral rehydration, bottled water, and bland food are first steps. Handwashing protects others. Most cases last a few days.

See a doctor for blood in stool, high fever, or diarrhoea lasting more than three days, especially in children or older adults.
