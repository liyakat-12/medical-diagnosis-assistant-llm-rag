# Sore Hangnail

A torn bit of skin beside the nail can get red and sore if bacteria enter. Early on it is a small tender area. Pus and spreading redness mean infection.

Soak in warm salty water, keep it clean, and do not bite the skin. A plaster protects it.

See a clinician if pus collects or redness spreads. Seek urgent care for a red streak up the finger or fever.
