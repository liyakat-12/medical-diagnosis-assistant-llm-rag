# Back Pain When You Sneeze

A sudden stab in the back when you sneeze or cough is often a muscle strain. Pain shooting down the leg can be a nerve irritation.

Brace the tummy and hold a pillow to the chest when you sneeze. Ease lifting for a few days.

See a doctor if sneeze pain keeps the leg weak. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or loss of bladder control.
