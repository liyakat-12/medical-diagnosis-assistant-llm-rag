# Burning Eye After Chilli or Spice

Chilli oil on a finger that then rubs the eye burns fiercely. Vision is usually OK once you rinse. A scratch from rubbing can keep the eye painful.

Rinse with plenty of clean water or saline for several minutes. Do not put oil or butter in the eye.

See a doctor if pain or blurred vision lasts after rinsing. Seek urgent care for a chemical other than food spice, or a contact-lens wearer with a painful red eye.
