# Aching Veins After Standing

Heavy, aching legs with visible twisted veins after standing can be varicose veins. Ankle swelling by evening is common. A hot, hard, painful vein can be inflammation and needs a check.

Walk, raise the legs, and ask a clinician about compression if suitable. Avoid long still standing when you can.

See a doctor for aching veins or skin changes at the ankle. Seek urgent care for a swollen painful calf, which may be a clot.
