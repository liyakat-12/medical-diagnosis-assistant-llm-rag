# What Vertigo Feels Like

Vertigo is a spinning feeling, as if the room is moving. It is different from a faint light head. A common cause is a crystal problem in the inner ear when you turn in bed.

Sit still until the spin fades, and avoid driving while it is happening. A clinician can teach a repositioning move for some types.

See a doctor for new spinning. Seek emergency care for vertigo with a new weak face, slurred speech, or fainting.
