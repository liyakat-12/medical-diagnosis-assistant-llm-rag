# Looking After Flu-Like Symptoms at Home

If you have sudden fever, aches, and a dry cough, home care is rest, fluids, and staying away from others. You may feel weak for more than a week even after the fever falls.

Use simple fever reducers only if they are safe for you. Do not give aspirin to children or teenagers with flu-like illness because of Reye syndrome risk.

See a doctor if you are pregnant, older, or have lung or heart disease. Seek emergency care for trouble breathing or a fever that returns after seeming better.
