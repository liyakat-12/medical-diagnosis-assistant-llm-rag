# No Appetite When Stressed

Worry can switch off hunger for a day or two. You may feel a tight upper tummy. Lasting loss of appetite with weight loss still needs a medical look.

Offer small regular snacks and fluids. Eat something plain even if you are not hungry.

See a doctor if appetite stays low for more than two weeks. Seek urgent care for vomiting blood or black stools.
