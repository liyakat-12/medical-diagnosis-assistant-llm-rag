# Shoulder Pain After Hanging Clothes

Reaching up to a line or a high rail loads the same tendons as a gym press. One sore arc when you lift the arm is typical.

Use a stool so the rail is not overhead, and ice after a long hang.

See a doctor if the arm will not lift. Seek emergency care for shoulder pain with chest pressure.
