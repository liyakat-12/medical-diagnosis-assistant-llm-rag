# Ulcer After Biting the Cheek

A sore spot after accidentally biting the cheek is a traumatic ulcer. It usually heals in one to two weeks. Sharp teeth or a broken filling can keep injuring the same place.

Avoid spicy food, use a soft brush, and see a dentist if a tooth keeps catching the cheek.

See a dentist or doctor if an ulcer lasts more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for a rapidly swelling face.
