# Shoulder Pain After Exercise

A sore shoulder the day after extra press-ups, swimming, or throwing is often tendon overload. A sudden pop during a lift is different and needs a prompt check.

Rest from the painful movement, ice after activity, and return with lighter sets. Cross-training that does not go overhead can keep you active.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm or there is a new dent in the muscle. Seek urgent care after a pop with severe swelling.
