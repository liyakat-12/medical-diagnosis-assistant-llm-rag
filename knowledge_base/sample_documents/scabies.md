# Scabies

Scabies is a mite infestation. Intense itch, worse at night, appears in finger webs, wrists, and waist. Close contacts often itch too.

A clinician prescribes a cream for the whole body and treats household members at the same time. Wash bedding on a hot cycle.

See a doctor rather than sharing leftover cream. Seek care if the skin becomes infected with pus.
