# Glomerulonephritis

Glomerulonephritis is inflammation of the kidney filters. Signs are cola or tea-coloured urine, reduced urine output, puffy face and ankles, and high blood pressure, sometimes a week or two after a throat or skin infection.

Treatment depends on the cause and may include blood pressure medicine, salt restriction, and immune-suppressing treatment under a kidney specialist.

See a doctor quickly for dark or bloody urine with swelling. Seek emergency care for very little urine, severe breathlessness, or confusion.
