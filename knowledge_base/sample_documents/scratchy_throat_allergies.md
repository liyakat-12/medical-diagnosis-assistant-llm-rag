# Scratchy Throat From Allergies

An itchy, scratchy throat with sneezing and itchy eyes, without fever, is often allergy rather than a cold. Pollen, dust, or pets can trigger it.

Reduce the trigger if you can, use saline, and consider a pharmacy antihistamine if it is suitable for you. Showering after being outdoors can rinse pollen.

See a doctor if allergy symptoms wreck sleep or you wheeze. Seek emergency care for tongue swelling or trouble breathing.
