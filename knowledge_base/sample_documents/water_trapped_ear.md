# Water Trapped in the Ear

A blocked, soggy ear after swimming is often trapped water or early swimmer's ear. Itching and mild pain can follow if the canal skin gets inflamed.

Tilt the head and gently pull the earlobe to let water out. Dry the outer ear. Do not poke buds into the canal.

See a doctor if the ear becomes very painful, swollen, or drains. Seek urgent care for a high fever or a very tender bone behind the ear.
