# Tender Neck Glands With a Cold

Soft, tender lumps under the jaw or in the neck often swell while you fight a sore throat or cold. They usually shrink over one to two weeks after the infection.

Rest, fluids, and time. Do not squeeze the lumps. Simple pain relief can ease tenderness if it is safe for you.

See a doctor if glands last more than two weeks, are hard and painless, or you have night sweats and weight loss. Seek urgent care for a rapidly growing painful swelling with trouble breathing.
