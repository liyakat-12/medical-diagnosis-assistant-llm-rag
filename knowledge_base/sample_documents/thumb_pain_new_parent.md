# Thumb Pain After Lifting a Baby

Pain on the thumb side of the wrist after repeatedly lifting a baby is often tendon irritation. A sting when you lift the thumb up or make a fist with the thumb inside is typical.

Lift with the whole arm, not a bent wrist, and rest the thumb when you can. A short period in a thumb splint can help.

See a doctor if thumb-side pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care after a fall if the thumb looks crooked.
