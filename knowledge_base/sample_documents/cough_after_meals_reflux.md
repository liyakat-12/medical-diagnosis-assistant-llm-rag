# Cough After Meals From Reflux

A cough or throat clear after eating, especially a large or late meal, can be reflux irritating the throat. You may not always feel classic heartburn.

Smaller meals, staying upright after food, and less coffee or alcohol can reduce this cough. Do not assume every cough is reflux.

See a doctor if a meal-related cough lasts more than a few weeks or you have food sticking. Seek emergency care for chest pain with sweating.
