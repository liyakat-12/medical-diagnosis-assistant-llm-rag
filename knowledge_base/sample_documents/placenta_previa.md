# Placenta Praevia

Placenta praevia means the placenta lies low and covers the cervix. The classic sign is painless bright red vaginal bleeding in the second half of pregnancy, which can be sudden and heavy.

It is usually picked up on a routine scan. Care involves avoiding sex and heavy lifting, monitoring, and planning a caesarean delivery.

Call emergency services for any bleeding in later pregnancy. Do not insert anything into the vagina or have a vaginal examination at home.
