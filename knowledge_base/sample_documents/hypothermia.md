# Hypothermia

Hypothermia is a dangerously low body temperature after cold exposure. Shivering, slurred speech, clumsiness, and sleepiness are warning signs.

Call emergency services. Move the person to shelter, remove wet clothes, and warm the trunk with blankets. Do not give alcohol.

This is an emergency even if the person wants to 'rest'. Infants and older adults are at higher risk.
