# Chlamydia

Chlamydia is a common sexually transmitted infection. Many people have no symptoms. Others notice discharge, burning urine, or pelvic pain.

A urine or swab test confirms it. Antibiotics prescribed by a clinician clear it. Partners also need treatment.

See a sexual health clinic for testing. Seek care for severe pelvic pain or fever, which can mean a complication.
