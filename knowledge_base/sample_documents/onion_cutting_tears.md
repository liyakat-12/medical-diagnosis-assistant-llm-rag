# Streaming Eyes While Cutting Onions

Onions release a vapour that stings the eyes and makes them water. This is ordinary and settles when you stop cutting.

Cut near a fan or under a vent, and wash your hands before you touch your face. Cool water on the lids helps.

See a doctor if the eye stays red and painful after you finish. Seek urgent care for a chemical splash that is not food.
