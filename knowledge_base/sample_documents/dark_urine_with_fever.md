# Dark Urine With a Fever

Dark urine with fever can be dehydration, a urine infection, or, with yellow eyes, a liver problem. One dark wee after a hot day with no fever is often just low fluids.

Drink water and note burning, back pain, or yellow eyes. Do not ignore fever with loin pain.

See a doctor for dark urine with fever. Seek emergency care for confusion, no urine, or yellow skin with severe pain.
