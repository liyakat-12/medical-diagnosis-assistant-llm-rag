# Pain on the Sides of the Heel

The sides of the heel can rub in a stiff shoe counter. First-step pain under the heel is more often plantar fascia.

Soften the heel counter, use a cushioned pair, and ease a sudden jump in walking.

See a doctor if side-heel pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight after a snap.
