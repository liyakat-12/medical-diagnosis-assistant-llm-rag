# Common Reasons a Knee Can Hurt

Front knee pain after hills can be kneecap irritation. Inner pain after a twist can be a meniscus or ligament strain. Pain with morning stiffness in an older adult can be osteoarthritis. These are common patterns, not a diagnosis.

Think about whether there was an injury, swelling, locking, or giving way. That story helps a clinician more than the word knee pain alone.

See a doctor if the knee locks, swells after every walk, or you cannot take weight. Seek urgent care for a deformed knee after injury.
