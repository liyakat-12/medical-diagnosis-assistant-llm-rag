# Forearm Ache From Typing

The forearm can ache from a mouse, a low desk, or extra gripping. Pain on the outer elbow is a tennis-elbow pattern; the inner side is a golfer-elbow pattern.

Raise the wrists to a straight line, take typing breaks, and ease a tight grip.

See a doctor if the forearm stays weak. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot turn the palm.
