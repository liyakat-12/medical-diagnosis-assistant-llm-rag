# After a Fall in an Older Adult

A fall in later life can cause a hidden hip fracture even if the person tries to walk. New confusion after a fall can be a head injury or infection. Blood-thinning medicines raise the stakes.

Do not leave an older person who has fallen without a check if they cannot take weight, hit their head, or seem confused.

See a clinician the same day after a fall if walking is painful or there was a head bump. Seek emergency care for being unable to take weight, vomiting, or confusion.
