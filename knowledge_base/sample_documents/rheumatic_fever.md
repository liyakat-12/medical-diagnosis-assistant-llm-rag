# Rheumatic Fever

Rheumatic fever can follow an untreated strep throat, usually in children. It causes fever, painful swollen joints that move from one joint to another, a rash, jerky movements, and sometimes heart inflammation.

It is treated with antibiotics and anti-inflammatory medicine, followed by long-term preventive penicillin to protect the heart valves.

See a doctor urgently for fever with migrating joint pain after a sore throat. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, chest pain, or swollen ankles.
