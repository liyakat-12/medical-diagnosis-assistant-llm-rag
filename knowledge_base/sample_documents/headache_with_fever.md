# Headache With a Fever

A headache with a fever is common in flu and other viruses. You feel achy and prefer a dark room. A headache with fever and a stiff neck or a rash that does not fade is an emergency.

Rest, fluids, and simple fever care if safe. This everyday pairing is not automatically meningitis.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days. Seek emergency care for a headache with a stiff neck, confusion, or a non-blanching rash.
