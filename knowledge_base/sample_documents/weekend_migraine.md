# Headache That Starts on Days Off

A headache on Saturday morning can follow sleeping in, delayed coffee, or a let-down after a stressful week. Some people with migraine notice this pattern. It is not a brain tumour pattern by itself.

Keep a regular get-up time and caffeine time even on days off. Rest in a quiet room if an attack starts.

See a doctor for a new weekend headache pattern. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache.
