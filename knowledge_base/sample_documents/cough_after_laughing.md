# Cough After Laughing or Talking

A cough that starts when you laugh, talk a lot, or go into cold air can be a sensitive airway after a virus, reflux, or asthma. A little cough after a long chat is common while you recover from a cold.

Sip water, rest the voice, and note whether wheeze is also present. Honey can soothe if you are over one year old.

See a doctor if this cough lasts more than three to four weeks or you wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness.
