# Corneal Abrasion (Scratched Eye)

A corneal abrasion is a scratch on the clear front of the eye, often from a fingernail, dust, or a contact lens. It causes sharp pain, watering, light sensitivity, and a feeling that something is stuck.

Do not rub the eye or keep wearing the lens. Most surface scratches heal within two days, and a clinician may give antibiotic drops to prevent infection.

See an eye clinician the same day, and seek emergency care if the injury involved metal, chemicals, or high speed, or if vision drops.
