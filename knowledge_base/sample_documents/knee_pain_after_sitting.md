# Knee Stiffness After Sitting

Some people notice a stiff, sore knee after sitting a long time, such as at a desk or in a car. This can happen with early osteoarthritis, tight thigh muscles, or simple inactivity. The first few steps often feel awkward, then loosen.

Stand and stretch every 30 to 45 minutes, gently straighten and bend the knee, and keep walking short distances through the day. Heat can ease morning stiffness.

See a doctor if stiffness lasts more than 30 minutes every morning, several joints swell, or you also have unexplained fever or weight loss.
