# Presbyopia (Age-Related Near Vision Loss)

Presbyopia is the normal stiffening of the eye lens after about age forty. Small print has to be held further away, and reading in dim light becomes tiring.

Reading glasses, bifocals, or varifocals fix it easily. Brighter light and larger phone text also help a lot.

See an eye clinician for a proper prescription, and sooner if vision blurs suddenly, you see flashes or floaters, or one eye becomes painful.
