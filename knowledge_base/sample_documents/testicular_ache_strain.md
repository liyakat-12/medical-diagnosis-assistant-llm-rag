# Aching Testicle After Strain

A dull ache after lifting or a long walk can be a strain or a varicocele. Sudden severe pain in one testicle is an emergency because the cord can twist. A new lump needs a prompt check even if it does not hurt much.

Supportive underwear and rest from heavy lifting can ease a mild ache. Examine the testicle for a new lump.

See a doctor for an ache lasting more than a few days or any new lump. Seek emergency care for sudden severe testicular pain.
