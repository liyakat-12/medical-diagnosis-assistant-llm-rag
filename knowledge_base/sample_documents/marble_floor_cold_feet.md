# Aching Cold Feet on Marble Floors

Bare feet on cold hard floors can ache the heels and the arches, and can stiffen the joints in the morning.

Wear indoor slippers, and take a short walk to warm the feet. A mat by the bed helps the first steps.

See a doctor if foot pain lasts or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a foot ulcer or a suddenly cold pale foot.
