# Furred Tongue During Illness

The tongue can look white or brown when you have a fever, a dry mouth, or have not been eating. This coat often clears as you recover and drink more.

Brush the tongue gently and sip water. See a clinician if a painful white coating lasts after you recover.

See a doctor if the coating is painful or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for a swelling tongue.
