# Ankle Pain When Walking

Ankle pain when walking often follows a twist, uneven ground, or worn shoes. A mild sprain, irritated tendons, or early arthritis after old injuries are common explanations. Swelling around the bone knobs is typical after a sprain.

Rest from running, ice, a supportive shoe or brace, and elevation reduce swelling. Start gentle circles when the first sharp pain eases. Do not hop on a very swollen ankle.

See a doctor if you cannot take four steps, there is bruising and a large swelling, or pain lasts more than two weeks. Seek urgent care if the ankle looks out of place.
