# Hoarse Voice

A croaky or whispered voice after shouting, a cold, or reflux irritation is usually laryngitis. The voice box is inflamed. Most cases settle with voice rest.

Rest the voice, drink fluids, and avoid whispering, which can strain the cords more. Steam can soothe. Do not smoke.

See a doctor if hoarseness lasts more than three weeks, especially if you smoke. Seek urgent care for noisy breathing or difficulty swallowing saliva.
