# Tender Chest Wall When You Press

A sharp chest-wall pain that is sore to press on the rib joints can be costochondritis. It often follows a cough or extra lifting. Crushing central pressure with sweat is not this pattern and is an emergency.

Rest from the movement that hurts, use heat or ice, and simple pain relief if safe. The pain can last weeks but is usually not dangerous once a clinician has considered the heart.

See a doctor for new chest pain so heart causes can be considered. Seek emergency care for crushing pain, breathlessness, or pain into the arm or jaw.
