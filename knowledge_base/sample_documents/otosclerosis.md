# Otosclerosis

Otosclerosis is abnormal bone growth in the middle ear that stops sound passing through. Hearing fades slowly, usually in young adults, often with ringing and mild unsteadiness.

Hearing aids help many people, and a small operation called stapedectomy can restore hearing in suitable cases.

See an ear specialist for gradual hearing loss, especially if close relatives also lost hearing young.
