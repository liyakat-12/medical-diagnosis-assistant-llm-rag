# Pale or Clay-Coloured Stools

Stools that look pale, clay-like, or putty-coloured, especially with dark urine or yellow eyes, can mean bile is not reaching the gut. A one-off pale stool after a light diet is less worrying.

Keep a photo note of stool and urine colour for the clinician. Keep drinking water.

See a doctor for pale stools with dark urine or yellow eyes. Seek emergency care for this with severe right-upper pain or fever.
