# Thumb Pain After Using a Phone

Aching at the base of the thumb after lots of texting or scrolling is tendon irritation. Pain can shoot along the thumb side of the wrist when you lift a phone or a mug.

Hold the phone with both hands, take typing breaks, and rest the thumb. A short period in a thumb splint can help some people.

See a doctor if thumb-base pain lasts more than a few weeks or the joint looks square and swollen. Seek urgent care after a fall onto the thumb if it looks crooked.
