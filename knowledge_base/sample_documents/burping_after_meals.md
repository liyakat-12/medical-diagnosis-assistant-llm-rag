# Burping a Lot After Meals

Extra burping after eating fast, drinking fizz, or chewing gum is swallowed air. Reflux and indigestion can also cause belching. Occasional burping is normal.

Eat more slowly, cut fizzy drinks, and sit up after meals. Notice if fatty meals or anxiety make it worse.

See a doctor if burping comes with weight loss, trouble swallowing, or black stools. Seek emergency care for sudden severe tummy pain.
