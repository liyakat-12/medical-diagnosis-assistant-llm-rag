# Itchy Rash After Henna

Natural henna is often well tolerated. Dark 'black henna' can contain extra chemicals that inflame the skin days later.

Wash the area gently and do not scratch. Avoid further black henna.

See a doctor for blistering or a spreading rash. Seek emergency care for facial swelling or trouble breathing.
