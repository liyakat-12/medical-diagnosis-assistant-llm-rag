# Wrist Pain After Yoga

Planks and downward dog load the wrists. A new practice or stiff shoulders can dump extra weight into the hands.

Make a fist on the floor or use wedges, and shorten holds. Ice after class.

See a doctor if the wrist swells or you cannot push up. Seek urgent care after a fall onto the hand if it looks deformed.
