# Puffy Eyes on Waking

Puffy lids first thing can follow salty food, crying, allergy, or lying flat. The extra fluid usually settles after you sit up. Sudden swelling of lips and eyes together can be allergy.

Sleep with an extra pillow, reduce extra salt if you notice a link, and use a cool cloth. Do not rub the lids hard.

See a doctor if puffiness lasts all day or you have swelling elsewhere. Seek emergency care for lip or tongue swelling or trouble breathing.
