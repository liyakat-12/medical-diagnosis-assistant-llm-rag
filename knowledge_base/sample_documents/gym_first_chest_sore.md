# Sore Chest Muscles After First Push-Ups

The chest and the front of the shoulders can feel bruised a day after the first push-up session. Pressing the muscle hurts; crushing pressure with sweat is different.

Ease the next session and keep the arms moving. The soreness should fade in a few days.

See a doctor if soreness is extreme. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the jaw.
