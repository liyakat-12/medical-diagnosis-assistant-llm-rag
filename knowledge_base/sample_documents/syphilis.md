# Syphilis

Syphilis starts as a painless sore, then may cause a rash on palms or soles, and later can damage the heart or brain if untreated.

A blood test diagnoses it. Penicillin given by a clinician is the usual cure. Follow-up blood tests check that treatment worked.

See a clinic for any unexplained genital sore or palm rash. This cannot be self-treated.
