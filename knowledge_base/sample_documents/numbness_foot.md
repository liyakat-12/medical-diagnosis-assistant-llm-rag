# Numbness in a Foot

A numb or burning foot can follow sitting on a nerve, tight shoes, or, when lasting, nerve irritation from the back or blood sugar problems. Both feet becoming numb over time needs a medical check.

Do not ignore foot numbness if you have diabetes. Check shoes for stones, and inspect the skin daily for unnoticed sores.

See a doctor for numbness that does not settle or affects walking. Seek urgent care for a suddenly weak leg, or a numb foot that is also cold and pale.
