# Stiff Shoulder That Will Not Turn Out

A frozen shoulder slowly becomes stiff and painful, especially when turning the arm outward or reaching up. It is more common in people with diabetes and can last many months. It is different from a sudden injury.

Keep using the arm for daily tasks within comfort, and a clinician can teach stretching. Pain relief that is safe for you can help sleep.

See a doctor if the shoulder is getting stiffer over weeks. Seek urgent care if stiffness started after a fall and you cannot lift the arm at all.
