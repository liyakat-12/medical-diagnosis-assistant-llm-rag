# Getting Up at Night to Urinate

Waking once to pass urine can be ordinary, especially if you drink in the evening. Waking many times can follow prostate enlargement, a urine infection, heart or kidney fluid shifts, or higher blood sugar.

Reduce evening caffeine and alcohol, and empty the bladder before bed. Keep a simple night diary for a clinician.

See a doctor if you wake more than twice most nights or you are thirsty all day. Seek urgent care if you suddenly cannot pass urine.
