# Bulimia Nervosa

Bulimia involves episodes of eating a large amount of food with a feeling of loss of control, followed by vomiting, laxatives, or excessive exercise. Weight may look normal, hiding the problem.

Repeated vomiting damages tooth enamel, causes sore throat and swollen cheeks, and upsets body salts. Treatment is psychological therapy with medical and dental support.

See a doctor or mental health service. Seek emergency care for chest pain, palpitations, fainting, or vomiting blood.
