# Constipation After Holding On

Repeatedly ignoring the urge at work or school makes stools drier and harder to pass. Children and busy adults both do this. A few days of holding on can start a cycle.

Go when you get the urge, drink water, and allow unhurried toilet time. Raise the feet on a small stool if that helps you pass stool more easily.

See a doctor if constipation lasts more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for no stool and no wind with a swollen tummy.
