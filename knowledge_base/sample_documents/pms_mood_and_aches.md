# PMS Mood Changes and Aches

Irritability, low mood, breast tenderness, and bloating in the week before a period are common PMS symptoms. They ease when the period starts. Severe monthly mood change that disrupts life needs a medical conversation.

Sleep, regular meals, and movement can take the edge off. A symptom diary over two cycles helps a clinician.

See a doctor if PMS stops you working or you have very low mood. Seek urgent help if you have thoughts of harming yourself.
