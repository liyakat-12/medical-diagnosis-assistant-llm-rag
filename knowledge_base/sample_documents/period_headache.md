# Headache With a Period

A headache or migraine in the days around a period is common and linked to hormone change. Nausea and light sensitivity can occur. A new severe headache unlike your usual period pattern still needs care.

Rest, water, and the pain plan a clinician has already agreed if you have one. A diary of cycle and headaches helps.

See a doctor for period headaches that disable you most months. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache.
