# Chafing on the Inner Thighs

Sore red skin where the thighs rub after a walk or in heat is chafing. Sweat makes it worse. A yeast infection can look similar in the folds.

Wear smoother shorts, dry the skin, and use a plain barrier ointment if a pharmacist agrees. Lose friction rather than using scented powder in broken skin.

See a doctor if the skin splits, weeps, or you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for spreading painful redness and fever.
