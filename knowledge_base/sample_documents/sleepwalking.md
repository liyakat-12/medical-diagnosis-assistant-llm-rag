# Sleepwalking

Sleepwalking means getting up and moving about while still asleep, usually in the first few hours of the night. The person has a blank expression, does not respond properly, and remembers nothing afterwards.

Make the home safe by locking doors and windows and clearing the floor. Keep a regular sleep routine, avoid alcohol, and gently guide the person back to bed rather than waking them abruptly.

See a doctor if sleepwalking is frequent in an adult, causes injury, or involves leaving the house, as it can be linked to sleep apnoea or medicines.
