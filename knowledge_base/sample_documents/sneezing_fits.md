# Bouts of Sneezing

Sudden runs of sneezing can be a cold, pollen, dust, or a bright light reflex. Itch in the nose without fever points more toward allergy.

Tissues, saline, and stepping away from the trigger help. Cover sneezes and wash hands if you might have a cold.

See a doctor if sneezing with a blocked nose lasts more than a few weeks. Seek emergency care for sneezing with lip swelling and wheeze.
