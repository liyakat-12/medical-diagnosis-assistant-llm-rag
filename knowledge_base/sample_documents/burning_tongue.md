# Burning Feeling on the Tongue

A scalded feeling on the tongue with a normal-looking mouth can follow a hot drink, dry mouth, low B12 or iron, or, less often, burning mouth syndrome. A white coating with soreness can be thrush.

Avoid very hot, spicy, or acidic food until reviewed. Sip water. Do not start high-dose supplements on a guess.

See a doctor for a burning tongue that lasts. Seek urgent care for tongue swelling or trouble breathing.
