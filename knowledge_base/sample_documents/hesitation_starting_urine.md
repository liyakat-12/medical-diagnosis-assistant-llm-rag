# Waiting a Long Time to Start Urinating

Standing a while before the stream starts is common with prostate enlargement in older men and can also follow a shy bladder, a cold medicine, or a urine infection. Women can have hesitancy after childbirth or with a prolapse.

Do not strain hard. Reduce evening caffeine. This symptom needs a check rather than prostate supplements from a shop.

See a doctor for a changing stream. Seek urgent care if you suddenly cannot pass urine and the bladder is painful.
