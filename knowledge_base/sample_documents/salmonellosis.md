# Salmonella Infection

Salmonella usually comes from undercooked eggs, poultry, or unpasteurised milk. It causes diarrhoea, stomach cramps, and fever starting six hours to six days after eating.

Most healthy people recover in four to seven days with fluids and rest. Antibiotics are reserved for severe illness, infants, older adults, and people with weak immunity.

See a doctor if diarrhoea lasts more than three days or there is blood. Seek emergency care for high fever with confusion or signs of severe dehydration.
