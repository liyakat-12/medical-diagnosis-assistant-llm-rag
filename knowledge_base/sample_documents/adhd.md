# Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

ADHD involves a lasting pattern of inattention, hyperactivity, or impulsiveness that is more than expected for the age and causes problems in at least two settings, such as home and school.

Diagnosis needs information from parents, teachers, and the person themselves over time. Treatment combines behavioural strategies, school support, and sometimes medicine.

See a doctor or school health service if concentration or restlessness is affecting learning, friendships, or safety.
