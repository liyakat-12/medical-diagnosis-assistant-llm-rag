# Stroke Warning Signs

A drooping face, arm weakness, or slurred speech needs emergency care even if it improves after minutes. A mini-stroke is still a warning. Sudden loss of vision or balance can also be a stroke.

Note the time symptoms started. Do not give food or drink. Stay with the person until help arrives.

Call emergency services immediately for face, arm, or speech changes, even if they come and go.
