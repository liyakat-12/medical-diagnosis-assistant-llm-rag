# Knee Pain After Praying on the Floor

Kneeling and sitting back on the heels can press the kneecap and the front of the knee. A thin mat makes this worse.

Use a thicker cushion, change position if allowed, and ice after a long prayer time.

See a doctor if the knee swells. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight.
