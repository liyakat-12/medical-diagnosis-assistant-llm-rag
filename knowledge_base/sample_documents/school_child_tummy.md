# Tummy Ache on School Mornings

A tummy ache that appears on school days and eases at weekends can be worry, a skipped breakfast, or constipation. Pain that wakes a child at night needs a check.

A calm breakfast, a toilet routine, and water help many children. Take the child seriously and look for a pattern.

See a doctor for lasting pain, weight loss, or blood in the stool. Seek urgent care for severe pain, green vomit, or a rigid tummy.
