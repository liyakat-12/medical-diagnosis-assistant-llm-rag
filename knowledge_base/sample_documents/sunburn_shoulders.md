# Red Painful Shoulders After Sun

Pink, hot, tender shoulders after sun are sunburn. Blisters mean a deeper burn. Sunburn increases later skin-cancer risk, so prevention matters next time.

Cool the skin with a damp cloth, drink water, and use a simple moisturiser. Do not pop blisters. Stay out of further sun until healed.

Seek urgent care for blistered burns over a large area, fever, confusion, or sunburn in a baby. See a doctor if you feel very unwell.
