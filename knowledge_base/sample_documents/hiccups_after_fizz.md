# Hiccups After Fizzy Drinks

A short run of hiccups after fizzy drinks or a big meal is common. The diaphragm spasms. Most bouts stop within minutes to a few hours.

Sip water slowly and avoid more fizz. Hold a gentle breath for a moment if it is comfortable. Do not scare someone hard on purpose.

See a doctor if hiccups last more than 48 hours. Seek urgent care for hiccups with chest pain or trouble swallowing.
