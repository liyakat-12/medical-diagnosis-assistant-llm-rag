# Hip Pain When Walking

Hip pain when walking may be felt in the groin, the side of the hip, or the buttock. Common possibilities include early arthritis in the joint, an irritated tendon on the outer hip, or referred pain from the lower back. Groin pain that limits putting on socks is more joint-related.

Shorter steps, a walking stick on the opposite side if needed, and avoiding deep sitting can reduce load. Gentle movement is better than complete rest.

See a doctor if hip pain lasts more than a few weeks or wakes you at night. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot take weight, especially if you are older.
