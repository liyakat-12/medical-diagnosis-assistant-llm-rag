# Stable Angina

Stable angina is chest tightness or pressure that appears predictably with exertion, cold weather, or stress and eases within a few minutes of rest. It may spread to the arm, neck, or jaw.

It signals narrowed heart arteries. A clinician confirms it with tests and prescribes medicines plus lifestyle changes to reduce risk.

See a doctor promptly for new exertional chest tightness. Call emergency services if chest pain comes at rest, lasts more than ten minutes, or comes with sweating and breathlessness.
