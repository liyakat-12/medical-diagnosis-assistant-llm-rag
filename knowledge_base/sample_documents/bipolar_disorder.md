# Bipolar Disorder

Bipolar disorder includes episodes of very high mood or energy (mania) and episodes of depression. Sleep may drop sharply in mania.

A psychiatrist diagnoses it. Mood-stabilising medicine and regular sleep protect against swings. Alcohol makes swings worse.

See a clinician for extreme mood cycles. Seek emergency care if there is no sleep for days with risky behaviour, or thoughts of suicide.
