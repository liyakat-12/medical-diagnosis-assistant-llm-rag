# Shoulder Pain When Reaching Across the Body

Pain on the top of the shoulder when you reach to the opposite pocket can involve the acromioclavicular joint. It is common after gym dips or a fall onto the point of the shoulder.

Avoid heavy dips and reaching across for a while. Ice after activity. Keep the shoulder gently moving.

See a doctor if cross-body pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care if the shoulder looks out of place after a fall.
