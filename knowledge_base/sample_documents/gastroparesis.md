# Gastroparesis (Slow Stomach Emptying)

In gastroparesis the stomach empties too slowly. People feel full after a few bites, bloated and nauseated, and may vomit undigested food hours after eating. Long-term diabetes is a common cause.

Small frequent low-fat low-fibre meals, chewing well, and walking after eating all help. A clinician may add medicine that speeds stomach emptying.

See a doctor for repeated vomiting of old food or unexplained weight loss. Seek urgent care if you cannot keep fluids down.
