# Foot Pain After Standing

Aching feet after standing at work can come from tired small muscles, flat or very high arches, or unsupportive shoes. The ball of the foot or the arch usually aches and eases when you sit.

Change shoes for ones with cushioning, sit for short breaks, and stretch the calves. Compression socks help some people who also have ankle swelling by evening.

See a doctor for foot pain with numbness, an open sore if you have diabetes, or pain that does not ease overnight. Seek urgent care for a suddenly cold, pale, or pulseless foot.
