# Oral Thrush

Oral thrush is a yeast coating on the tongue or inner cheeks. White patches may wipe off and leave a sore red surface. It is more common after antibiotics or with dentures.

Rinse the mouth after inhalers, clean dentures, and see a pharmacist about antifungal gel if it is mild.

See a doctor if patches spread, swallowing hurts, or you have a weak immune system.
