# What a Fever Means

A fever is a raised body temperature, often from an infection. You may feel hot, shivery, and achy. Fever is a sign, not a disease name.

Rest, drink fluids, and use paracetamol if it is safe for you. Write the temperature down.

See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days or you are at higher risk. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck, a non-blanching rash, confusion, or breathlessness.
