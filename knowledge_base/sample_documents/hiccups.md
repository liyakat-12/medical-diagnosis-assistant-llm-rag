# Hiccups

Hiccups are sudden diaphragm spasms that make a 'hic' sound. Most spells last minutes and are harmless. Eating fast, fizzy drinks, or a big meal can start them.

Sip cold water, pause and breathe slowly, or swallow a teaspoon of sugar. Most bouts stop on their own.

See a doctor if hiccups last more than 48 hours, stop you from eating or sleeping, or follow a head or chest injury.
