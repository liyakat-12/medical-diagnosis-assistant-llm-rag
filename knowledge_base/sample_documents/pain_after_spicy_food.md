# Burning Tummy After Spicy Food

A burning upper tummy or chest after chilli or curry is often indigestion or reflux. Occasional burning after a very spicy meal is common.

Eat a smaller portion next time, avoid lying down, and use an antacid if a pharmacist agrees. Water and a plain snack can settle mild burning.

See a doctor for burning most weeks or trouble swallowing. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain.
