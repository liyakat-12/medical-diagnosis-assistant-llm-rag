# Sneezing Fits While Dusting

House dust can start sneezes, itchy eyes, and a blocked nose while you clean. There is usually no fever.

Dust with a damp cloth, wear a simple mask if you wish, and wash your face after. A pharmacy antihistamine may help if suitable.

See a doctor if dust always makes you wheeze. Seek emergency care for lip swelling or trouble breathing.
