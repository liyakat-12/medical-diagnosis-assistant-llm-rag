# Shin Pain After Running

A dull ache along the inner shin after extra running on hard ground is often shin splints. Pain usually eases with rest. A pinpoint sharp pain in one spot that worsens can be a stress fracture and needs a check.

Cut impact, ice the shin, and check shoe wear. Return gradually on softer surfaces.

See a doctor if shin pain is pinpoint, worse at night, or does not ease with rest. Seek urgent care for a shin that is hot, red, and swollen after a kick.
