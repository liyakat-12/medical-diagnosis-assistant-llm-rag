# Burning When You Urinate

A burning sting when you pass urine, with a need to go often and only a little urine, is a common urinary tract infection pattern. People say it burns when I urinate. Other causes include irritation after sex, some soaps, and, in men, prostate or urethra inflammation.

Drink extra water, do not hold urine, and see a clinician rather than guessing antibiotics. Pain relief and a hot water bottle on the lower tummy can ease discomfort while you wait.

See a doctor or clinic the same day for burning urine, especially if you are pregnant. Seek urgent care for fever, shaking, back pain, or vomiting, which can mean a kidney infection.
