# Golfer Elbow

Golfer elbow is pain on the inner side of the elbow where the tendons attach. It worsens with gripping, bending the wrist, or lifting, and there may be stiffness and weak grip.

Treatment mirrors tennis elbow: relative rest, ice, gradual loading exercises for the wrist flexors, and avoiding the painful movement for a time.

See a physiotherapist if symptoms persist beyond two months, and a doctor if there is numbness or tingling in the little finger.
