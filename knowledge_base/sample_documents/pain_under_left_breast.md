# Pain Under the Left Breast

A tender spot you can press under the left breast is often a chest-wall joint. Crushing pressure, sweat, or pain into the arm is a different emergency pattern.

Ease reaching and coughing strain, and try a warm cloth on a tender spot if it is clearly sore to touch.

See a doctor if the sore spot lasts. Seek emergency care for crushing pain, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the jaw or arm.
