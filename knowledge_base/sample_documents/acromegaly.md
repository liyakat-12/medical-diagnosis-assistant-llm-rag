# Acromegaly

Acromegaly is caused by too much growth hormone in adults, usually from a small pituitary tumour. Hands, feet, jaw, and facial features slowly enlarge, rings and shoes stop fitting, and there is sweating, joint pain, and headache.

Because changes are gradual, it is often noticed by comparing old photographs. Treatment is surgery to remove the tumour, sometimes with medicine or radiotherapy.

See a doctor if rings and shoes have become tight over a few years alongside headaches or vision changes.
