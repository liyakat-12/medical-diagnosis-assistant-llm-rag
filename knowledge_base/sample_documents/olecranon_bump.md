# Soft Lump on the Tip of the Elbow

A squashy lump on the point of the elbow after leaning or a knock can be olecranon bursitis. The elbow usually still moves. A red hot lump with fever can be infected.

Pad the elbow, avoid leaning on it, and ice. Do not puncture the lump.

See a doctor for a lasting or red lump. Seek urgent care for fever with a hot elbow lump.
