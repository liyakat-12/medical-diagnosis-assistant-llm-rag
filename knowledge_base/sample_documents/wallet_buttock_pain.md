# Buttock Pain From a Thick Wallet

Sitting on a thick wallet can press the deep buttock and irritate a nerve, causing ache down the leg. The pain often eases when you take the wallet out and walk.

Keep the wallet out of the back pocket, stand often, and walk. This is a simple mechanical cause, not a diagnosis of disc disease.

See a doctor if leg pain lasts or the foot is weak. Seek emergency care for saddle numbness or bladder change.
