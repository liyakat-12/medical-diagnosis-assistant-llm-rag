# Swollen Knee After Activity

A knee that puffs up after sport or extra walking may have irritated lining, a small cartilage injury, or fluid after overload. Rapid swelling within a few hours of a twist is more concerning than mild puffiness the next day.

Rest, ice, a light compression bandage, and elevation reduce fluid. Avoid running until swelling settles. Do not drain a knee at home.

See a doctor for swelling that returns after every activity. Seek urgent care for sudden swelling after injury, a hot red knee with fever, or inability to walk.
