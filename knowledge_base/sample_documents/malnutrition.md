# Malnutrition (Undernutrition)

Malnutrition means not getting enough energy or nutrients. Signs are unintended weight loss, clothes becoming loose, muscle wasting, constant tiredness, slow wound healing, and frequent infections. In children, growth slows.

Treatment means finding the cause, which may be illness, poverty, swallowing difficulty, or mental health problems, and building intake gradually with nutrient-rich food.

See a doctor for unintentional weight loss of more than five percent in six months, or for a child not gaining weight.
