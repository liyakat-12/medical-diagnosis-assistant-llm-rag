# Gingivitis (Gum Inflammation)

Gingivitis makes gums red, swollen, and easy to bleed when you brush. Plaque left on the teeth is the usual cause.

Twice-daily brushing, daily floss, and a dental clean reverse most early gum disease.

See a dentist for bleeding gums that last. Loose teeth or gum abscesses need prompt dental care.
