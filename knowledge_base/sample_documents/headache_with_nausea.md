# Headache With Nausea

A throbbing headache with nausea, and sometimes sensitivity to light or sound, is a common migraine pattern. People ask what can cause a headache with nausea. Tension headaches can also make you feel sick if they are severe, but migraine is more often one-sided and pulsing.

Rest in a quiet dark room, sip water, and use the pain relief your clinician has advised if you already have a plan. Keep a note of foods, sleep, and screens that seem to trigger attacks.

See a doctor for new headaches with nausea that keep returning. Seek emergency care for a sudden thunderclap headache, a weak face or arm, confusion, or a headache with fever and a stiff neck.
