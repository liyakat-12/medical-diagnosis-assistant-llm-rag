# Fever in a Child: When to Get Help

Many children get a short fever with a virus and recover. Babies under three months with a fever need urgent assessment. A rash that does not fade, breathing difficulty, or a child who is unusually drowsy also needs urgent care.

Offer drinks, light clothing, and check on the child often. Use fever medicine only if a clinician or pharmacist has advised it is suitable for that child.

Seek urgent or emergency care for a baby under three months with fever, a non-blanching rash, or a child who is very drowsy or struggling to breathe.
