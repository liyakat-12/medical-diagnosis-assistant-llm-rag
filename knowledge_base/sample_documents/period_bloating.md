# Bloating Around a Period

A swollen tummy, tender breasts, and tighter clothes in the days before a period are common PMS changes. Fluid and hormone shifts play a part. The bloating usually eases when bleeding starts.

Regular meals, less extra salt if you notice puffiness, gentle exercise, and heat for cramps can help. This is not a disease by itself.

See a doctor if bloating is constant through the month or you have very heavy bleeding. Seek urgent care for sudden severe pelvic pain and fainting.
