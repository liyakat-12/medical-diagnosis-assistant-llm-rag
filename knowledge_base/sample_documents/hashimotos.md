# Hashimoto Thyroiditis

Hashimoto disease is an immune attack on the thyroid and the commonest cause of an underactive thyroid. Symptoms build over months with tiredness, cold intolerance, weight gain, dry skin, constipation, and low mood.

Blood tests show thyroid antibodies and a high TSH. Treatment is a daily thyroid hormone tablet with periodic blood tests to adjust the dose.

See a doctor for persistent tiredness with weight gain and cold intolerance, especially with a family history of thyroid disease.
