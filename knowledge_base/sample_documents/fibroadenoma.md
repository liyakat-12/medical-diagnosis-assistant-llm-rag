# Fibroadenoma

A fibroadenoma is a smooth firm breast lump that moves easily under the fingers, often called a breast mouse. It is common in women under thirty and is not cancer.

Once confirmed by scan, small fibroadenomas are usually just monitored, as many shrink on their own. Larger or growing ones can be removed.

See a doctor to confirm any breast lump rather than assuming it is harmless, especially if it grows or becomes hard.
