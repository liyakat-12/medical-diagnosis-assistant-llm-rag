# Heavy Menstrual Bleeding

Heavy periods mean soaking a pad or tampon every hour or two, passing clots larger than a coin, bleeding more than seven days, or needing double protection. Tiredness and breathlessness from low iron are common.

Causes include fibroids, hormone imbalance, and bleeding disorders. Treatments include tranexamic acid, hormonal options such as a coil, and iron replacement.

See a doctor if periods interfere with work or sleep. Seek emergency care for soaking through protection every hour for several hours, fainting, or chest pain.
