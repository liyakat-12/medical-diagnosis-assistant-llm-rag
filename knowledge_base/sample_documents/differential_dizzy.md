# Common Reasons You Might Feel Dizzy

A brief wooze when you stand can be a blood-pressure drop. A spin of the room when you turn in bed can be an inner-ear crystal problem. Feeling faint in a hot room is different from true spinning. These patterns guide questions; they do not confirm a label.

Sit or lie down when dizzy. Note whether the room spins or you feel you might faint.

See a doctor for repeated dizziness. Seek emergency care for dizziness with a weak face or arm, chest pain, or a sudden severe headache.
