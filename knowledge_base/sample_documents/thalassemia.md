# Thalassaemia

Thalassaemia is an inherited condition where the body makes abnormal haemoglobin, causing anaemia. Signs are pallor, tiredness, poor growth in children, yellowish skin, and an enlarged abdomen from a big spleen.

Mild forms need only monitoring and should not be treated with iron unless a doctor confirms iron deficiency. Severe forms need regular transfusions plus treatment to remove excess iron.

See a doctor for persistent anaemia that does not improve with iron. Genetic counselling is advised before pregnancy if thalassaemia runs in the family.
