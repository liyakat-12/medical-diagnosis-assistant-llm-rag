# Graves Disease

Graves disease is an immune condition that makes the thyroid overactive. It causes weight loss despite a good appetite, a fast heartbeat, heat intolerance, shaky hands, anxiety, and sometimes bulging eyes.

Blood tests confirm it. Treatment options are anti-thyroid tablets, radioactive iodine, or surgery, with beta blockers used early to settle the heart rate.

See a doctor for unexplained weight loss with a racing pulse. Seek emergency care for fever, confusion, and a very fast heartbeat, which is a thyroid storm.
