# Pain on the Little-Finger Side of the Wrist

The little-finger side of the wrist can hurt after a twist, a fall, or leaning on the heel of the hand. Pain after a fall can be a missed fracture.

Rest, ice, and avoid pushing up from a chair on that hand for a few days.

See a doctor if the wrist stays weak after a fall. Seek urgent care if the wrist looks deformed or the fingers are numb.
