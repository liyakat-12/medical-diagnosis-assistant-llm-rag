# Colon Polyps

Colon polyps are small growths on the inner lining of the large bowel. Most cause no symptoms; some cause blood in the stool, a change in bowel habit, or iron deficiency.

They are found and removed during a colonoscopy. Removing them matters because some types can slowly turn into bowel cancer over years.

See a doctor for blood in the stool or a bowel habit change lasting more than three weeks, especially over age forty-five or with a family history.
