# Lower Back Pain After Sitting

People often say my lower back hurts after sitting or I have pain on one side of the lower back after a long desk day. Prolonged sitting, a soft sofa, or a wallet in a back pocket can strain the lumbar muscles and joints. Pain is usually aching and eases when you walk.

Stand and walk for a few minutes each hour, sit with both feet on the floor, and try a small cushion in the lower back. Gentle knee-to-chest stretches and staying active are better than long bed rest.

See a doctor if back pain lasts more than a few weeks or keeps you from daily tasks. Seek emergency care for back pain with numbness between the legs, new bladder or bowel trouble, or weak legs.
