# Sweating at Night With a Cold

Sweating through the sheets while a fever is breaking is common with a cold or flu. You may feel better afterward. Night sweats that last for weeks without an infection need a review.

Use layers you can remove, keep fluids up, and change damp clothes. This short-term sweating with a cold is different from weeks of drenching sweats.

See a doctor for night sweats lasting more than a couple of weeks, weight loss, or swollen glands. Seek emergency care for confusion with a high fever.
