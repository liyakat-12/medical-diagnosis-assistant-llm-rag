# Bruise After a Bump

A bruise is blood under the skin after a knock. It changes from red-blue to green-yellow as it heals over one to two weeks. A single bruise after a remembered bump is common.

Ice wrapped in a cloth and rest ease the ache. Raise the limb if it is a limb bruise. Do not puncture a bruise.

See a doctor for bruises that appear without knocks, or very large painful swelling. Seek urgent care after a head bump with vomiting, confusion, or being hard to wake.
