# Jitters After Too Much Coffee

Extra tea or coffee can race the heart, shake the hands, and disturb sleep. A panic-like wave can follow a very strong cup.

Stop further caffeine that day, drink water, and eat something. The shaky feeling usually fades in a few hours.

See a doctor if palpitations keep happening with little caffeine. Seek emergency care for chest pain, fainting, or severe breathlessness.
