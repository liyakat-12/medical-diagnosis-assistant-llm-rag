# Itchy Watery Eyes Outdoors

Itchy watery eyes after being outdoors in pollen season are allergic. The eyes itch more than they hurt. Vision stays clear.

Sunglasses, not rubbing, and pharmacy allergy eye drops or antihistamines if suitable can help.

See a doctor if you also wheeze or drops fail. Seek urgent care for pain, light sensitivity, or reduced vision.
