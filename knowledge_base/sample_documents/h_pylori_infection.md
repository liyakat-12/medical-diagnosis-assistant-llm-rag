# Helicobacter pylori Infection

H. pylori is a stomach bacterium that can cause gastritis and ulcers. Symptoms include a burning upper abdominal pain that may ease or worsen with food, bloating, burping, and nausea.

A breath, stool, or endoscopy test confirms it. Treatment is a two-week combination of antibiotics with an acid-reducing tablet, and the full course must be completed.

See a doctor for recurring upper abdominal burning. Seek emergency care for vomiting blood, black stools, or sudden severe pain.
