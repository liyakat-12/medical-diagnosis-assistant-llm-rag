# Cough After Exercise

A dry cough, tight chest, or wheeze during or after a run can be exercise-induced airway narrowing, especially in cold air. A cough after hard effort can also be ordinary airway dryness.

Warm up, cover the mouth in cold air, and note whether a reliever inhaler you already have helps. Do not start inhalers without advice.

See a doctor for a cough that comes with most exercise. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness, blue lips, or being unable to speak.
