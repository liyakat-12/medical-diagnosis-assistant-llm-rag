# Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia involves hearing voices, strong beliefs others do not share, confused speech or thinking, withdrawal from people, and reduced motivation and emotional expression, usually starting in late teens or twenties.

It is a treatable medical condition. Antipsychotic medicine, talking therapy, family support, and help with work and housing greatly improve outcomes, especially when started early.

See a doctor or mental health service early. Seek emergency help for thoughts of harming oneself or others, or for refusing all food and drink.
