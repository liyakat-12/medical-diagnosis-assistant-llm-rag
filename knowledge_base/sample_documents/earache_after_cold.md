# Earache After a Cold

An earache after a blocked nose is often pressure from the tube that joins the ear to the throat, or a middle-ear infection. Pain can be sharp and worse when lying down. Children get this more often than adults.

Simple pain relief, sitting more upright, and treating the blocked nose with saline can help. Most earaches improve in a few days.

See a doctor if ear pain lasts more than three days, fluid drains, or hearing drops suddenly. Seek urgent care for a swollen, very tender bone behind the ear, or a high fever with a stiff neck.
