# Breast Cancer Warning Signs

Warning signs include a hard painless lump, a change in breast size or shape, skin dimpling like orange peel, a nipple pulling inwards, bloody nipple discharge, scaly nipple skin, or a lump in the armpit.

Breast cancer found early is very treatable, which is why screening and prompt reporting of changes matter so much. Pain alone is rarely the first sign.

See a doctor without delay for any of these changes, and attend screening appointments even when you feel completely well.
