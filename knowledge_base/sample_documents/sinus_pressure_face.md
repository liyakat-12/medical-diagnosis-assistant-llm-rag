# Sinus Pressure in the Face

Pressure around the cheeks, eyes, or forehead that is worse when you bend forward often follows a cold. The sinuses can fill with mucus. Most cases are viral and settle without antibiotics.

Fluids, steam, saline rinses, and simple pain relief help. Sleep with an extra pillow. Most sinus discomfort eases within two to three weeks.

See a doctor if facial pain lasts more than three weeks, you have a high fever, or swelling around one eye. Seek emergency care for a swollen red eyelid with vision change or a severe headache.
