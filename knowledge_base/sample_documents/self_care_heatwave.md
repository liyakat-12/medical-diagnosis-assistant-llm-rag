# Looking After Yourself in a Heatwave

Hot days raise the risk of dehydration and heat exhaustion. Older adults, young children, and people on some medicines are more at risk. Feeling faint, sick, and headachy in the heat is a warning.

Drink water, stay in the coolest room, wear loose clothes, and avoid heavy exercise at midday. Check on older neighbours if you can.

Seek emergency care if someone becomes confused, has very hot dry skin, or faints and does not recover quickly.
