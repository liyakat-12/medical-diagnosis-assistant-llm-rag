# Nausea in Early Pregnancy

Nausea, and sometimes vomiting, is common in early pregnancy and can happen at any time of day. Small snacks and fluids help many people. Very frequent vomiting that stops you keeping drinks down needs prompt care.

Eat little and often, sip water, and talk to a midwife or doctor before taking any tablet. Do not use leftover anti-sickness medicine without advice.

See a midwife or doctor if you cannot keep fluids down. Seek urgent care for dark urine, dizziness, or vomiting blood.
