# Back Ache From a Heavy School Bag

A heavy bag on one shoulder can ache a child's neck and low back. Two straps and a lighter load reduce this.

Use both straps, pack only what is needed, and keep the bag close to the back.

See a doctor if a child limps or the pain lasts into the weekend. Seek urgent care after a fall or for pain with fever.
