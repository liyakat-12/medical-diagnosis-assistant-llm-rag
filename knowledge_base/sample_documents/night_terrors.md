# Night Terrors

Night terrors mostly affect children aged three to eight. The child suddenly screams, looks terrified, sweats, and is hard to comfort, yet is still asleep and will not remember it in the morning.

Do not try to wake the child. Stay nearby to keep them safe, keep bedtime regular, and avoid overtiredness, which is the main trigger. Most children grow out of it.

See a doctor if episodes happen several times a night, continue into the teenage years, or involve jerking that could be a seizure.
