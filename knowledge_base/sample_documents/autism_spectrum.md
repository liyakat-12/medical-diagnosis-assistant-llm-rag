# Autism Spectrum Disorder

Autism involves differences in social communication and repetitive or focused behaviours. Early signs include limited eye contact, not responding to their name, delayed speech, repeated movements, and strong need for routine.

It is a lifelong difference, not an illness. Early assessment opens the door to speech therapy, occupational therapy, and educational support that make a large difference.

See a doctor or child development service if a child is not babbling by twelve months, not using single words by sixteen months, or loses skills they previously had.
