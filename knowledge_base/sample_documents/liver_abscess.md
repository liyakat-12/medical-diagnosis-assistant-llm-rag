# Liver Abscess

A liver abscess is a pocket of pus in the liver, often from a gut infection or amoebiasis. It causes high swinging fever with chills, pain in the upper right abdomen, sweating, and weight loss.

It does not clear with tablets alone. Hospital treatment with intravenous antibiotics and often drainage guided by ultrasound is required.

Seek emergency care for high fever with right-sided upper abdominal pain, especially with yellow eyes or feeling very unwell.
