# Acid Taste and Burning Chest

A sour taste with burning behind the breastbone after tomato, coffee, or a late meal is typical acid reflux. Lying flat soon after eating makes it worse. Occasional acidity is common.

Finish meals three hours before bed, raise the head of the bed, and eat smaller portions. Pharmacy antacids may help if a pharmacist agrees they are suitable.

See a doctor for swallowing difficulty, food sticking, or heartburn most days. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, sweating, or pain into the arm.
