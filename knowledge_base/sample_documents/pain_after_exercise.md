# Muscle Soreness After Exercise

A dull muscle ache that starts a day after a new or harder workout is delayed onset muscle soreness. It is common in the thighs, calves, or arms and eases within a few days. It is not the same as a sudden snap during sport.

Gentle walking, light stretching, sleep, and fluids help. You can train other body parts while the sore muscles recover. Sudden sharp pain during a lift is a strain, not ordinary soreness.

See a doctor if soreness lasts more than a week, the muscle swells a lot, or your urine turns dark after extreme exercise. Seek urgent care for a muscle that bunches up after a pop.
