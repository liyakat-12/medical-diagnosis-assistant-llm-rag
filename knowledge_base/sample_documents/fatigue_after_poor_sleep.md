# Washed Out After a Bad Night

Feeling foggy, clumsy, and short-tempered after little sleep is expected. One bad night does not mean a disease. Repeated poor sleep, though, can mimic depression and weaken immunity.

Keep the next day's get-up time, get daylight, and avoid a long late nap. Catch-up sleep the following night helps more than extra caffeine.

See a doctor if you are exhausted most days even after sleep, or you snore and stop breathing. Seek urgent care for chest pain or fainting with severe paleness.
