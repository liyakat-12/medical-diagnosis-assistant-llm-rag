# TMJ Jaw Pain

TMJ disorders cause pain in the jaw joint, clicking, and trouble opening the mouth wide. Chewing gum and night grinding make it worse.

Soft food, heat, a night guard if advised, and not resting the chin on a hand help many people.

See a dentist or doctor if the jaw locks, pain lasts weeks, or you cannot eat.
