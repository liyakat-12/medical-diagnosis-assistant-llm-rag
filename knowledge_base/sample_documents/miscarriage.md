# Miscarriage

A miscarriage is pregnancy loss before twenty weeks. Signs are vaginal bleeding with cramping lower abdominal or back pain, passing tissue, and a sudden reduction in pregnancy symptoms.

Light spotting in early pregnancy is common and does not always mean loss, but it should always be assessed with an examination and a scan.

Seek emergency care for heavy bleeding soaking a pad an hour, severe one-sided pain, shoulder-tip pain, fever, or feeling faint.
