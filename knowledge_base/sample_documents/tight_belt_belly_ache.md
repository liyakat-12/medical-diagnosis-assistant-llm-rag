# Tummy Ache From a Tight Belt

A tight belt after a meal can add to bloating and reflux. Loosening it often eases the ache within minutes.

Wear the belt looser after food, and sit up rather than slump.

See a doctor if belt-pain happens without a tight waistband. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or a rigid tummy.
