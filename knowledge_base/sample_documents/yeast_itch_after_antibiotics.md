# Itch After a Course of Antibiotics

Vaginal itch and a thick white discharge after antibiotics can be thrush, because helpful bacteria were reduced. Not every itch after antibiotics is thrush. A first episode still needs advice.

Avoid scented soaps. A pharmacist or clinic can advise on suitable treatment after questions about your symptoms.

See a clinic if this is a first episode, you are pregnant, or symptoms do not settle. Seek urgent care for severe pelvic pain and fever.
