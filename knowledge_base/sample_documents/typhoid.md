# Typhoid Fever

Typhoid is a bacterial infection from contaminated food or water. It causes stepwise fever, headache, stomach pain, and sometimes a faint rash.

A blood or stool test confirms it. Prescribed antibiotics and fluids are needed. Vaccination helps travellers to risk areas.

See a doctor for fever after travel to a typhoid region. Seek emergency care for confusion or a rigid abdomen.
