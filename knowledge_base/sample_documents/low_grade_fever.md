# Low Fever and Feeling Off

A temperature a little above normal with aches and low energy is common at the start of a viral illness. You may feel cold then hot. Most healthy adults can rest at home if they can drink and stay alert.

Rest, fluids, and light clothing. Use a fever reducer only if it is safe for you and you are uncomfortable. Check on children and older adults more often.

See a doctor if a fever lasts more than three days. Seek emergency care for a non-blanching rash, confusion, or severe breathlessness.
