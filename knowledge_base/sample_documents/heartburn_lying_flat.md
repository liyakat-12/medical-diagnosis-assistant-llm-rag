# Heartburn When You Lie Flat

A burning rise into the throat when you lie down is typical reflux. Gravity no longer keeps acid in the stomach. A sour taste on the pillow can occur.

Finish eating three hours before bed, raise the head of the bed, and avoid a very large late meal. Pharmacy antacids may help if suitable.

See a doctor for night heartburn most nights or food sticking. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain or pain into the arm.
