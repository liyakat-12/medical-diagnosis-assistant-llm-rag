# Overactive Bladder

Overactive bladder is a sudden strong urge to pass urine, often with frequent day and night trips. Leakage can happen if a toilet is not near.

Bladder training, timed drinks, and pelvic-floor exercises help. A clinician may add medicine after checking for infection.

See a doctor for new urgency, blood in urine, or pain, which need other causes ruled out.
