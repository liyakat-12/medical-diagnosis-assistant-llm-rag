# Painful Paper Cut

A tiny slice on a fingertip can sting out of proportion to its size because many nerve endings sit there. Bleeding is usually slight.

Wash, press to stop bleeding, and cover. Keep it dry when you clean or cook.

See a clinician if it will not stop bleeding or looks infected. Seek urgent care for numbness beyond the cut or a cut from a dirty tool if tetanus is not up to date and a clinician advises a check.
