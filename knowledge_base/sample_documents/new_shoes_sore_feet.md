# Sore Feet From New Shoes

New shoes can rub a blister or make the whole foot ache after extra walking. A red hot spreading patch is not a simple rub.

Wear them in for short spells, use a plaster on a hot spot, and keep the skin clean and dry.

See a doctor if a blister looks infected, especially if you have diabetes. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
