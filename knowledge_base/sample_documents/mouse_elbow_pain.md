# Outer Elbow Pain From a Mouse

Pain on the outer elbow after long mouse use is a common tennis-elbow pattern. Gripping and lifting a kettle can hurt even if you never play tennis.

Bring the mouse closer, take breaks, ice after work, and keep the wrist straighter. A forearm strap helps some people.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if pain lasts more than six to eight weeks. Seek urgent care for a hot swollen elbow with fever.
