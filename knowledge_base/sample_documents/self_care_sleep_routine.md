# A Simple Sleep Routine

A regular get-up time, a dim quieter hour before bed, and using the bed for sleep rather than scrolling help many everyday sleep problems. This is general advice, not a cure for sleep apnoea.

Get daylight in the morning and ease caffeine after mid-afternoon. A short early nap is better than a long late one.

See a doctor if poor sleep lasts more than a few weeks or you snore and stop breathing. Seek urgent care for chest pain that wakes you.
