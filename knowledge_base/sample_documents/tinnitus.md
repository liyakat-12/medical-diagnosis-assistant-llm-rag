# Tinnitus (Ringing in the Ears)

Tinnitus is hearing a ring, buzz, or hiss that others cannot hear. Noise exposure, wax, and some medicines are common links.

A hearing check helps. Sound therapy, sleep care, and treating wax or ear disease can reduce the burden. There is rarely a single pill cure.

See a doctor for new tinnitus, especially in one ear, with hearing loss or dizziness.
