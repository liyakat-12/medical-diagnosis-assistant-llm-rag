# Actinic Keratosis

Actinic keratoses are rough scaly patches on sun-exposed skin such as the face, scalp, ears, and backs of the hands. They often feel like sandpaper before they can be clearly seen.

They are pre-cancerous, so a small number can turn into skin cancer. Treatments include freezing, prescription creams, and strict daily sun protection.

See a dermatologist for rough persistent patches on sun-exposed skin, and urgently for a patch that becomes thick, tender, or bleeds.
