# Tickle in the Throat From Drip

A feeling of mucus dripping down the back of the nose can cause a constant need to clear the throat or a dry cough. Colds and allergies are common reasons.

Saline rinses, extra pillows, and treating the blocked nose help. Sip water instead of constant throat clearing, which can irritate more.

See a doctor if the drip and cough last more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for facial swelling and fever.
