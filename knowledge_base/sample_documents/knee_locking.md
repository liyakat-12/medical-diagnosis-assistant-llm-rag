# Knee That Locks or Gets Stuck

True locking means you cannot fully straighten the knee until something shifts. A catching feeling that eases when you wiggle the knee can be a loose cartilage flap or a tight kneecap. Locking is not ordinary stiffness.

Do not force the knee straight. Ice and rest reduce swelling while you arrange a review. Note whether it locks after a squat or a twist.

See a doctor promptly for a knee that will not straighten. Seek urgent care if locking follows an injury and you cannot take weight.
