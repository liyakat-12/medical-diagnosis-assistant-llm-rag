# Strong-Smelling Urine

Urine can smell stronger after asparagus, coffee, or when you have not drunk much. A new foul smell with burning and frequency can be a urine infection.

Drink more water and notice whether burning or fever is also present. Smell alone is not a diagnosis.

See a doctor if strong smell comes with burning, blood, or fever. Seek urgent care for fever and back pain.
