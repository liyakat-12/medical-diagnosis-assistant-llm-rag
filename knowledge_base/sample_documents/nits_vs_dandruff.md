# White Specks in the Hair: Nits or Dandruff

Dandruff flakes brush off easily. Nits are louse eggs stuck to the hair shaft and are harder to slide off. Itch behind the ears is common with lice.

Check close to the scalp with a fine comb. Treat lice with a method a pharmacist or school nurse recommends. Do not share combs.

See a pharmacist or doctor if you cannot clear the specks. Seek urgent care for a very swollen infected scratch with fever.
