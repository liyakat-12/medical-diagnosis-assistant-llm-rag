# Astigmatism

Astigmatism is an uneven curve of the eye surface, so vision is slightly blurred or stretched at all distances. Headaches and tired eyes after reading are common.

Glasses or special contact lenses correct it completely. It is very common and is not a disease.

Have an eye test for ongoing blur or eye strain, especially in a child who rubs the eyes or tilts the head to see.
