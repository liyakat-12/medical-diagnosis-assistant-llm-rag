# Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis

ALS is progressive loss of the nerves controlling muscle. Early signs are weakness in one hand or foot, tripping, slurred speech, muscle twitching, and cramps, with no loss of sensation.

There is no cure, but medicines can slow progression and a specialist team supports breathing, speech, nutrition, and mobility.

See a neurologist for progressive painless weakness with twitching. Seek urgent care for breathing difficulty or choking while eating.
