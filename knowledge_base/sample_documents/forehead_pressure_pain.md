# Pressure Across the Forehead

A heavy band across the forehead can be a tension headache, eye strain, or sinus pressure after a cold. Bending forward can make sinus pressure worse.

Steam, saline, water, and screen breaks help many people. Sleep with the head a little raised if the nose is blocked.

See a doctor if forehead pressure lasts more than 10 days or fever returns. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache.
