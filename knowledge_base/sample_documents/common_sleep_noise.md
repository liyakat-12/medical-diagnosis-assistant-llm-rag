# Partner Says You Snore Loudly

Loud snoring happens when the airway is partly narrowed in sleep. Alcohol, a blocked nose, sleeping on the back, and extra weight around the neck make it worse. Snoring plus pauses in breathing needs a sleep review.

Try sleeping on your side, easing evening alcohol, and treating a blocked nose. This may reduce simple snoring but does not treat sleep apnoea on its own.

See a doctor if you stop breathing in sleep, wake unrefreshed, or fall asleep in the day. Seek urgent care for chest pain at night with severe breathlessness.
