# Hip Stiffness After Sitting

A stiff hip after sitting that eases after a few steps is common with early wear in the joint or tight hip flexors from desk work. Some people also feel it in the groin when getting out of a car.

Stand regularly, stretch the front of the hip, and keep walking. A slightly higher chair can make standing up easier.

See a doctor if you limp most days or pain spreads down the thigh. Seek urgent care for a sudden inability to move the hip after injury.
