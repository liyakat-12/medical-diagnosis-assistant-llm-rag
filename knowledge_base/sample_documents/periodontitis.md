# Periodontitis (Gum Disease)

Periodontitis is advanced gum disease where the gum pulls away from the tooth and the supporting bone is lost. Signs are bleeding gums, bad breath, gum recession, and teeth that feel loose.

A dentist performs a deep clean below the gum line and may prescribe antibiotics. Daily brushing and cleaning between the teeth are essential to stop further bone loss.

See a dentist for bleeding gums that do not improve in two weeks, gum recession, or any loose adult tooth.
