# Painful Cracks on Winter Fingers

Cold dry air and extra washing split the fingertips. The cracks sting in lemon, spice, or sanitiser.

Moisturise after every wash, wear gloves outdoors, and use a thicker cream at night.

See a doctor if cracks look infected. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
