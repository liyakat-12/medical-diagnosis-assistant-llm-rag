# Aching Joints in Cold Weather

Some people with early arthritis or old injuries feel more joint ache in cold, damp weather. The joints are not necessarily damaged more on those days. Stiffness often eases once you warm up and move.

Layer warm clothing, keep moving indoors, and use heat packs. Stay as active as you can rather than resting all day.

See a doctor if several joints swell or morning stiffness lasts more than half an hour. Seek urgent care for a single hot joint with fever.
