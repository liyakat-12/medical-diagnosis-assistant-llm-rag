# Trichomoniasis

Trichomoniasis is a common sexually transmitted parasite. Women may notice frothy yellow-green discharge with a strong smell, itching, and soreness; men often have no symptoms or mild burning.

It is confirmed by a swab or urine test and cured with prescribed antibiotics. Sexual partners must be treated at the same time to avoid passing it back.

See a doctor or sexual health clinic for unusual discharge or itching, and avoid sex until treatment is complete.
