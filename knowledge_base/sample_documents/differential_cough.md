# Common Reasons for a Cough

A short dry cough after a cold is often leftover airway irritation. A wet cough with a runny nose is often a simple virus. Night cough can be drip or reflux. A cough lasting months, especially in a smoker, needs a different review.

Think about duration, mucus, fever, and breathlessness. Those details help a clinician more than the word cough alone.

See a doctor for a cough lasting more than three to four weeks. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or coughing large amounts of blood.
