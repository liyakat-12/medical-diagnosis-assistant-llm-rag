# Peritonsillar Abscess (Quinsy)

A quinsy is a pocket of pus beside a tonsil, usually after tonsillitis. The throat pain becomes severe and one-sided, the voice sounds muffled, and the jaw becomes hard to open.

This does not clear with home care. It needs urgent drainage plus antibiotics given by a hospital team.

Seek emergency care for severe one-sided throat pain with drooling, difficulty opening the mouth, or any trouble breathing or swallowing saliva.
