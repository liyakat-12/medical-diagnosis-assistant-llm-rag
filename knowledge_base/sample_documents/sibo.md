# Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth

SIBO means too many bacteria grow in the small intestine. It causes bloating that builds through the day, excess wind, loose stools, and sometimes weight loss or low vitamin B12.

A clinician may use a breath test and then prescribe a specific antibiotic course, alongside treating the underlying cause such as slow gut movement.

See a doctor for daily bloating with diarrhoea or weight loss rather than self-treating with long-term probiotics.
