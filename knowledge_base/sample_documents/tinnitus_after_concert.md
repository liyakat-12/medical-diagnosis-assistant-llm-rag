# Ringing Ears After a Loud Concert

A temporary ring or buzz after a loud gig is common. Hearing may feel muffled and usually improves over hours. Repeated loud noise can damage hearing over time.

Rest in a quiet place and protect your ears at the next loud event. Do not poke the ears.

See a doctor if ringing lasts more than a few days or hearing does not return. Seek urgent care for sudden hearing loss or ringing with facial weakness.
