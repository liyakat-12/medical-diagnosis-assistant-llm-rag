# Painful Corn on a Toe

A hard, painful spot on a toe where a shoe rubs is a corn. The skin thickens to protect itself and then hurts when pressed. Diabetes changes how you should treat foot skin.

Wear wider shoes and use a cushioned pad. Do not cut a corn with a blade at home.

See a podiatrist or doctor if you have diabetes or the corn is very painful. Seek urgent care for spreading redness or a foot ulcer.
