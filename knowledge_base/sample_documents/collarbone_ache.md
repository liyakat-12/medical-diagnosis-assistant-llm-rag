# Pain Around the Collarbone

A sore collarbone can follow a backpack strap, a bag on one shoulder, or a fall onto the side. A bump or a step in the bone after a fall can be a break.

Ease the strap load, ice after a knock, and rest the arm in a sling-like position if it is sore.

See a doctor if collarbone pain lasts after a fall. Seek urgent care if the bone looks deformed or the arm is numb.
