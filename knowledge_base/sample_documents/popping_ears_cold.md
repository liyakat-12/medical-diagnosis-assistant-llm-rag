# Popping Ears With a Cold

Ears that pop, crackle, or feel full during a cold happen when the pressure tube is swollen. Hearing can seem underwater. This often clears as the nose clears.

Saline for the nose, swallowing, and extra pillows at night help. Do not force a hard Valsalva if it hurts.

See a doctor if popping and muffled hearing last more than a week or two after the cold. Seek urgent care for sudden severe hearing loss.
