# Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm

An abdominal aortic aneurysm is a bulge in the main artery of the abdomen. It usually causes no symptoms until it leaks, when there is sudden severe abdominal or back pain, a pulsing feeling, and collapse.

Small aneurysms found on scans are monitored; larger ones are repaired before they burst. Smoking and high blood pressure are the main risk factors.

Call emergency services immediately for sudden severe abdominal or back pain with dizziness or fainting, especially in older smokers.
