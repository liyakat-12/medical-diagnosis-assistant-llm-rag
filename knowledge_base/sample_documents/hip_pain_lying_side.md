# Outer Hip Pain When Lying on That Side

A sore spot on the bony side of the hip that wakes you when you lie on it is often trochanteric pain from tendons or a bursa. Walking and climbing stairs can also hurt.

Sleep on the other side with a pillow between the knees. Avoid lying on the sore hip. Gentle side-lying stretches and not crossing the legs can reduce load.

See a doctor if outer hip pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot take weight on that hip.
