# Head Bump Warning Signs

After a knock to the head, worsening headache, repeated vomiting, confusion, being hard to wake, or a seizure needs emergency care. A brief daze that clears can still need advice, especially if you take blood thinners.

Stay with the person, keep them still if their neck may be injured, and do not give food or drink if they are drowsy.

Call emergency services for vomiting, confusion, a seizure, or being hard to wake after a head bump.
