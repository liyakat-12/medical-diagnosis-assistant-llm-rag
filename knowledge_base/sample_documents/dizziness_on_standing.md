# Light-Headedness When Standing Up

Feeling woozy or seeing spots when you stand quickly is often a brief drop in blood pressure. Dehydration, a hot room, some blood pressure tablets, and standing after a long sit make it more likely.

Stand up slowly, sit on the edge of the bed first, and drink extra fluid unless a doctor has limited your fluids. Compression stockings help some people.

See a doctor if fainting happens more than once or you are on new medicine. Seek emergency care if light-headedness comes with chest pain, breathlessness, or an irregular pounding heartbeat.
