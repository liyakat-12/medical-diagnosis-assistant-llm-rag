# Ringworm (Tinea Corporis)

Ringworm is a fungal rash, not a worm. It forms a ring-shaped, itchy, scaly patch that slowly grows outward.

Antifungal cream from a pharmacy for two to four weeks often works on the body. Keep the area dry. Do not share towels.

See a doctor if the scalp or nails are involved, or the ring does not fade after home cream.
