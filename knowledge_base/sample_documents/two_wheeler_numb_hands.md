# Numb Hands After a Scooter Ride

Vibration and locked elbows can numb the ring and little fingers or the whole hand. The feeling usually returns after you shake the hands out.

Change grip, soften the elbows, and stop to stretch on a long ride.

See a doctor if the hands stay numb at rest. Seek urgent care if a hand is cold, pale, and weak after an injury.
