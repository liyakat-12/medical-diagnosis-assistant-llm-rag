# Watery Eyes in Wind

Eyes that stream in cold wind are often a reflex because the surface is a little dry. Paradoxically, dry eyes can water. A blocked tear duct usually waters one eye all the time.

Wraparound glasses and lubricating drops if suitable help. Avoid a fan blowing on the face.

See an optometrist if one eye waters constantly or the lids are sore. Seek urgent care for a watery eye after a chemical splash.
