# Cough Lasting About Three Weeks

Many coughs after a virus last up to three weeks. Around the three-to-four-week mark, a check is sensible if it is not fading, especially if you smoke, lose weight, or cough at night.

Keep a note of colour of mucus, fever, and whether you feel breathless. This helps a clinician more than guessing an antibiotic.

See a doctor for a cough lasting more than three to four weeks. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or coughing large amounts of blood.
