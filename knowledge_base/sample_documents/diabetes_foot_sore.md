# A Sore on the Foot If You Have Diabetes

A blister, crack, or red spot on the foot is more serious if you have diabetes because feeling and healing can be reduced. Do not wait for it to become a large ulcer.

Check both feet daily, wear fitted shoes, and see a clinician promptly for any break in the skin. Do not burst blisters or cut corns yourself.

See a diabetes or foot clinic promptly for any foot sore. Seek urgent care for spreading redness, fever, or a foot that is cold and pale.
