# Cough After Firecracker or Festival Smoke

Smoke from crackers or bonfires can irritate the throat and the lungs. People with asthma are more likely to wheeze.

Stay upwind, wear a mask if smoke is thick, and use a prescribed inhaler only if you already have one.

See a doctor if the cough lasts into the week. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or chest pain.
