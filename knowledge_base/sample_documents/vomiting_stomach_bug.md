# Vomiting From a Stomach Bug

Sudden vomiting with tummy cramps is often a viral stomach bug. It may come with diarrhoea. Most healthy adults improve within one to two days if they can sip fluid.

Wait a short time after vomiting, then sip water or oral rehydration solution often. Restart bland food when the nausea eases. Stay home while you are vomiting.

Seek urgent care if you cannot keep any fluid down, vomit blood, have a severe headache and stiff neck, or there is no urine. Babies and older adults dehydrate faster.
