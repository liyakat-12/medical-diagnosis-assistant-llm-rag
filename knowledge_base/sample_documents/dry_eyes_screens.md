# Dry Eyes From Screens

Gritty, burning, or watery eyes after screen work happen because you blink less. The tear film dries. Air conditioning makes it worse.

Look away every 20 minutes, blink fully, and use preservative-free lubricating drops if a pharmacist agrees. Raise screens so you look slightly down.

See an optometrist or doctor if drops do not help, or you have light pain and reduced vision. Contact-lens wearers should stop lenses until reviewed.
