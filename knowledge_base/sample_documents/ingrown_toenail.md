# Ingrown Toenail

An ingrown toenail grows into the skin beside the nail, usually the big toe. The edge is sore, red, and sometimes weeps.

Soak the foot in warm water, keep it clean, wear roomy shoes, and do not cut a deep V in the nail. Over-the-counter packs can ease pressure.

See a doctor or podiatrist if there is pus, spreading redness, fever, or you have diabetes.
