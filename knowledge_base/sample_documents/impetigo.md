# Impetigo

Impetigo is a contagious bacterial skin infection. Honey-coloured crusts appear around the nose and mouth, more often in children.

A clinician may prescribe antibiotic cream or tablets. Keep nails short and avoid sharing face cloths.

See a doctor for spreading crusts or fever. Stay home until a clinician says it is no longer catching.
