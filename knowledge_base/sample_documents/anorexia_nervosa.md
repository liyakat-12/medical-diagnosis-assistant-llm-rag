# Anorexia Nervosa

Anorexia nervosa involves severely restricting food because of intense fear of weight gain and a distorted view of body size. There is marked weight loss, feeling cold, hair thinning, missed periods, and dizziness.

It is a serious mental health condition, not a lifestyle choice, and has real physical dangers for the heart and bones. Treatment combines psychological therapy, nutrition support, and medical monitoring.

See a doctor or mental health service early, as recovery is better with early treatment. Seek emergency care for fainting, chest pain, or a very slow pulse.
