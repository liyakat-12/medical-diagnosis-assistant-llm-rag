# Cirrhosis of the Liver

Cirrhosis is permanent scarring of the liver from long-term damage such as alcohol, hepatitis, or fatty liver. Early signs are tiredness and poor appetite; later there is yellow skin, swollen legs, a swollen abdomen, and easy bruising.

The scarring cannot be reversed, but stopping alcohol, treating hepatitis, and regular monitoring slow it and prevent complications.

See a doctor for persistent tiredness with known liver risk. Seek emergency care for vomiting blood, black stools, or new confusion and drowsiness.
