# Groin Pull After a Sudden Split

A sudden reach or raid can pull the inner-thigh muscles. Walking and bringing the knees together then hurt.

Rest the split, ice, and walk within comfort. Do not force a full stretch early.

See a doctor if you cannot walk after a few days. Seek urgent care for a groin bulge or severe swelling.
