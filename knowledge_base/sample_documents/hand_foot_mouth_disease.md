# Hand, Foot and Mouth Disease

This is a common childhood viral illness with fever and sore throat first, then painful mouth ulcers and a non-itchy rash of small spots or blisters on the hands, feet, and sometimes the buttocks.

It clears in seven to ten days. Offer cool soft foods and plenty of fluids, and give age-appropriate pain relief for mouth soreness. Antibiotics do not help.

See a doctor if the child refuses all fluids, passes very little urine, or the fever lasts more than three days.
