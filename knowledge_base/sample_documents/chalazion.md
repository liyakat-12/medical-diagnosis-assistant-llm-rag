# Chalazion (Eyelid Lump)

A chalazion is a firm painless lump in the eyelid caused by a blocked oil gland. Unlike a stye it is usually not tender and sits further from the lash line.

Warm compresses several times a day plus gentle massage towards the lash line help it drain. Most clear within a few weeks.

See an eye clinician if the lump lasts more than a month, keeps growing, or presses on the eye and blurs vision.
