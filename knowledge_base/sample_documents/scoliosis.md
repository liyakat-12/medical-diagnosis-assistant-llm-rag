# Scoliosis

Scoliosis is a sideways curve of the spine, most often noticed during the growth spurt in early teens. Signs are uneven shoulders, one shoulder blade sticking out, uneven waist, and leaning to one side.

Mild curves are monitored with regular checks. Moderate curves in growing children may need a brace, and large curves may need surgery.

See a doctor if the back looks uneven when bending forward, and urgently for back pain with leg weakness, numbness, or bladder problems.
