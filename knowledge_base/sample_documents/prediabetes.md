# Prediabetes

Prediabetes means blood sugar is higher than normal but not yet diabetes. There are often no symptoms. A blood test finds it.

Weight loss if needed, walking most days, and a healthier plate can return sugar toward normal.

Ask for a check if you have a family history, extra weight around the waist, or high blood pressure.
