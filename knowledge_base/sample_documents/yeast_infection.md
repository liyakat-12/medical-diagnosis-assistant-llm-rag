# Vaginal Yeast Infection

A vaginal yeast infection causes itch, thick white discharge, and soreness, often after antibiotics.

A clinician can confirm it is not another infection. Antifungal cream or a tablet may be advised. Avoid scented soaps.

See a doctor for a first episode, pregnancy, recurrent infections, or pelvic pain and fever.
