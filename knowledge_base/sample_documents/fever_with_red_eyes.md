# Fever With Red Eyes

Red eyes with a cold can be ordinary. Red eyes with high fever and a spreading rash can be measles or another infection and need advice before you sit in a waiting room.

Rest and fluids. Call the clinic first if you suspect a highly infectious rash illness.

See a doctor for fever with red eyes and a rash. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, a stiff neck, or unusual sleepiness.
