# Pain Around the Belly Button

A cramp around the navel after a big meal, gas, or a stomach bug is common. Pain that starts at the navel and then settles in the lower right can be appendicitis.

Rest, sip fluids, and use a warm cloth. Avoid heavy food until the cramp settles.

See a doctor if navel pain lasts more than a day or keeps returning. Seek emergency care for pain moving to the lower right with fever or vomiting.
