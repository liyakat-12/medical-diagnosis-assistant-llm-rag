# Bloating After a Protein Shake

Some shakes bloat the tummy because of lactose, sugar alcohols, or drinking them too fast. This is usually gas, not a disease by itself.

Sip slower, try a smaller serving, and check whether milk-based powder is the trigger.

See a doctor if bloating is daily with weight loss. Seek urgent care for severe pain, vomiting, or a rigid tummy.
