# Constipation After a Long Flight

Cabin dryness, sitting still, and holding on during a flight commonly slow the bowel. Ankle swelling can happen on the same trip for different reasons.

Walk the aisle when allowed, drink extra water, and eat fruit when you land. Do not ignore the first urge after landing.

See a doctor if constipation lasts more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for a swollen painful calf after a flight, which can be a clot, not constipation.
