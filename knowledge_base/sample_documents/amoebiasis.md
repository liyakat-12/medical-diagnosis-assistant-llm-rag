# Amoebiasis (Amoebic Dysentery)

Amoebiasis is a gut infection from contaminated food or water. Many people have mild symptoms, but it can cause cramping pain with loose stools containing blood or mucus, and low fever.

Diagnosis needs a stool test. Treatment is prescribed antiparasitic medicine, and the full course must be finished to clear cysts from the bowel.

See a doctor for bloody diarrhoea or diarrhoea lasting more than a week. Seek emergency care for severe abdominal pain with high fever, which can mean a liver abscess.
