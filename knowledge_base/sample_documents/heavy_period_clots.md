# Heavy Period With Clots

Passing clots and soaking pads every hour is a heavy period. Tiredness can follow. Some months are heavier, but a sudden change or flooding needs a review.

Use adequate protection and rest if you feel faint. A clinician can discuss treatments and check for anaemia.

See a doctor for flooding or clots that are new for you. Seek urgent care if you soak through every hour and feel faint, or have sudden severe pelvic pain.
