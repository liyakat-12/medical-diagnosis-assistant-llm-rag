# Headache From a Tight Ponytail

A tight band, bun, or braid can pull the scalp and the temples. The pain often eases within an hour of loosening the hair.

Wear a looser style and ease the first hours after a tight braid. Massage the temples gently.

See a doctor if scalp pain lasts without a tight style. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache.
