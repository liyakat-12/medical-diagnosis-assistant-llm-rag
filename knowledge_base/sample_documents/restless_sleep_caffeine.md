# Broken Sleep After Evening Caffeine

Tea, coffee, cola, or energy drinks after mid-afternoon can delay sleep and cause lighter, broken nights. You may not notice the link until you cut back.

Move caffeine to the morning for a week and compare sleep. Keep a regular get-up time.

See a doctor if sleep stays poor after cutting caffeine. Seek urgent care for chest pain that wakes you.
