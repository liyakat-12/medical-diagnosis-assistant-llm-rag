# Aching Bunion

A bony bump at the base of the big toe can rub in shoes and ache after walking. The toe may lean toward the others. This is a bunion, not an infection by itself.

Choose wider shoes, use a bunion pad, and rest from tight fashion shoes. Ice after a long walk can ease the ache.

See a doctor or podiatrist if walking is limited. Seek urgent care for a hot, shiny, extremely painful big toe, which can be gout.
