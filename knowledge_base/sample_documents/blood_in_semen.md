# Blood in Semen

Pink or brown semen can follow an infection, a procedure, or, often in younger men, a short-lived harmless bleed. It still needs a check so other causes are not missed. Pain, fever, or blood in urine matter more.

Avoid sexual activity until you have advice if there is pain. Do not ignore repeated blood.

See a doctor for blood in semen. Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding, fever, or inability to pass urine.
