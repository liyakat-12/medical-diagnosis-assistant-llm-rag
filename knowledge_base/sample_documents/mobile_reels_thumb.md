# Sore Thumb After Long Scrolling

The thumb joint and tendon can ache after hours of scrolling or gaming. A click when you bend the thumb can start.

Switch hands, use the other fingers, and rest the thumb in the evening. Ice after a long session.

See a doctor if the thumb locks. Seek urgent care after an injury if the thumb looks out of place.
