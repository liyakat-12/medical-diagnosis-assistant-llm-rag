# Viral Sore Throat

Most sore throats are viral. The throat feels scratchy, swallowing hurts a little, and a cough or runny nose often comes with it. Fever is usually mild.

Rest, warm drinks, honey for adults and older children, and simple pain relief help. Antibiotics do not treat viruses.

See a doctor for a severe sore throat with high fever and no cough, or if you cannot swallow saliva.
