# Side Strain After Cricket or Throwing

A sudden pull in the side when you bowl, throw, or bat can strain the muscles between the ribs. Breathing and laughing then hurt.

Rest the throw, ice, and support the side when you cough. Return to sport only when a deep breath is easy.

See a doctor if the side stays weak. Seek emergency care for chest pain after a blow, or breathlessness.
