# Lasting Bad Breath

Ongoing bad breath is often from the tongue, gum disease, dry mouth, or food leftover. Occasional morning breath is normal. Lasting odour that others notice needs a dental check.

Brush the tongue, clean between teeth, drink water, and avoid smoking. Sugar-free gum can help saliva if your dentist agrees.

See a dentist if bad breath lasts despite cleaning. See a doctor if you also have a lasting dry mouth, sinus infection, or unexpected weight loss.
