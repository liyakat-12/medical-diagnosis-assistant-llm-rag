# Foreign Body in the Eye

A foreign body such as dust, grit, or an eyelash causes a scratchy feeling, watering, redness, blinking, and light sensitivity. It often feels as if the object is under the upper lid.

Do not rub the eye. Blink repeatedly, then rinse with clean water or saline. Remove contact lenses first. Most loose particles wash out easily.

See an eye clinician if the feeling persists after rinsing. Seek emergency care if the object was travelling at speed, is metal, or is embedded in the eye.
