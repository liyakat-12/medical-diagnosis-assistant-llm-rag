# Bruise (Contusion)

A bruise is blood under the skin after a bump. It looks red or purple, then green and yellow as it heals over one to two weeks.

Rest, ice wrapped in cloth, and raising the area reduce swelling. Pain relief may help if suitable.

See a doctor for bruises with no injury, bruises after starting a blood thinner, or a bruise that keeps growing with severe pain.
