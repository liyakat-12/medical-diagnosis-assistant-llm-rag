# Hoarse Voice in the Morning From Reflux

A croaky first-thing voice that eases after you speak a bit can be overnight reflux or mouth breathing. A voice that stays hoarse all day for weeks needs a check, especially if you smoke.

Raise the head of the bed, avoid a late meal, and rest the voice. Sip water.

See a doctor if hoarseness lasts more than three weeks. Seek urgent care for noisy breathing.
