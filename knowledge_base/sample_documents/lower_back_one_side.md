# Pain on One Side of the Lower Back

People say I have pain on the left side of my lower back or the right. A muscle strain, a stiff facet joint, or sitting twisted can cause one-sided ache. Kidney infection pain is usually higher in the flank and comes with fever or burning urine.

Keep gently moving, use heat, and avoid twisting lifts. Note any fever, urine change, or pain shooting below the knee.

See a doctor if one-sided back pain lasts more than a few weeks or you have fever and burning urine. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or fainting.
