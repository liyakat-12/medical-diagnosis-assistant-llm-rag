# Menopause

Menopause is the end of monthly periods, usually around age 45 to 55. Hot flushes, night sweats, sleep change, and vaginal dryness are common.

A clinician can discuss hormone therapy and non-hormone options. Bone and heart health matter after menopause.

See a doctor for bleeding after 12 months without a period, or if flushes disrupt life.
