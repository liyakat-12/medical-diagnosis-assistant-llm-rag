# Folate Deficiency

Folate deficiency causes a type of anaemia with tiredness, breathlessness on effort, pale skin, mouth ulcers, a sore smooth tongue, and irritability.

Causes include poor diet, pregnancy, alcohol, and some medicines. Treatment is folic acid tablets plus leafy greens, beans, and fortified foods.

See a doctor for persistent tiredness with mouth ulcers. Folic acid is especially important before and during early pregnancy to protect the baby.
