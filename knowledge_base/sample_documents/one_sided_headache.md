# One-Sided Headache

Pain on one temple or behind one eye can be migraine, a severe tension headache, or, rarely, other causes. Cluster headache is a severe one-sided pain around the eye with a watery eye and is less common.

Rest, water, and your usual safe pain relief may help a typical attack. A diary of side, food, and sleep helps a clinician.

See a doctor for a new one-sided headache pattern. Seek emergency care for the worst headache of your life or a weak face or arm.
