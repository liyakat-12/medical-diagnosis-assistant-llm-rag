# Blocked Ear After Swimming

A full ear after a swim can be trapped water or early canal inflammation. Hearing seems muffled. Pain and itching can follow if the skin breaks down.

Tilt the head, dry the outer ear, and keep the ear dry for a few days. Do not use buds.

See a doctor if the ear becomes very painful. Seek urgent care for fever and a tender bone behind the ear.
