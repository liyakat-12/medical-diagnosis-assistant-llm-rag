# Knee Pain After Sitting on the Floor

Cross-legged or kneeling on a hard floor can strain the inner knee or the kneecap. Getting up the first time often hurts most.

Use a cushion, change position often, and straighten the knees between sits.

See a doctor if floor sitting keeps the knee swollen. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight.
