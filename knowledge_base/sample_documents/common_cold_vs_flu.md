# Telling a Cold From Flu-Like Illness

A cold usually builds slowly with a runny nose and sore throat. Flu-like illness often hits faster with fever, marked body aches, and exhaustion. Overlap is common, so labels are a guide only.

Rest and fluids help both. Stay home while feverish. Testing is sometimes used in clinics; most healthy people do not need an antibiotic for either.

See a doctor if you are at higher risk and feel suddenly very ill. Seek emergency care for breathlessness, chest pain, or confusion.
