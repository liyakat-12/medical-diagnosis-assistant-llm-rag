# Common Reasons a Shoulder Can Hurt

Pain when lifting out to the side often involves the rotator cuff. A stiff shoulder that cannot reach behind the back may be a frozen shoulder. Pain after a fall with a square shape can be a dislocation. Patterns guide questions; they do not confirm a label.

Note night pain, recent work overhead, and whether both shoulders are stiff. Keep the shoulder gently moving unless it looks out of place.

See a doctor if you cannot lift the arm after two weeks. Seek emergency care for a shoulder that looks out of place, or chest pain with the shoulder ache.
