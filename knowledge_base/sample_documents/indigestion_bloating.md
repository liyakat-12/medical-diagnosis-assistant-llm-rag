# Bloating After Eating

A tight, swollen tummy after eating is often gas, a large meal, or sensitivity to certain foods such as beans, onions, or fizzy drinks. People say my stomach feels bloated after eating. Occasional bloating is common and not a disease by itself.

Eat more slowly, reduce fizzy drinks, and notice if dairy or very fatty meals make it worse. A short walk after eating can move gas. Sudden persistent bloating, especially in women over 50, needs a medical check.

See a doctor if bloating is daily, you lose weight, or you have blood in the stool. Seek urgent care for sudden severe tummy pain with vomiting and no stool or wind.
