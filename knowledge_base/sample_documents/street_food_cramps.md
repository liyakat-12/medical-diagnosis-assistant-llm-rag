# Cramps After Street Food

Cramps a few hours after street food can be ordinary spice and oil, or a food-borne bug. Fever and repeated vomiting need more care.

Fluids, rest, and a bland diet are the first steps. Do not take leftover anti-diarrhoeal medicine unless a clinician agrees.

See a doctor if cramps last more than two days. Seek urgent care for blood in the stool, high fever, or dizziness.
