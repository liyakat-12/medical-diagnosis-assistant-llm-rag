# When Discharge May Be Ordinary

Clear or white discharge that changes through the month, without itch, smell, or pain, is often ordinary. Itch with a thick white discharge, or a fishy thin discharge, is more likely a problem that needs advice.

Avoid douching and scented soaps. Notice sudden change, odour, or pelvic pain.

See a clinic for a change in discharge with itch, smell, or pain. Seek urgent care for severe pelvic pain and fainting.
