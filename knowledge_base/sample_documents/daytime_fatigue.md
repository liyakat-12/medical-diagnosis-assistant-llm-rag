# Tiredness Most Days

Feeling washed out most days can follow poor sleep, low iron, thyroid problems, low mood, infection recovery, or simply too little rest. Tiredness alone does not name one disease.

A regular sleep window, meals, daylight, and gentle activity are safe first steps. Note any weight change, thirst, or shortness of breath to tell a clinician.

See a doctor if tiredness lasts more than a few weeks or you have pale skin, heavy periods, chest pain, or unexplained weight loss.
