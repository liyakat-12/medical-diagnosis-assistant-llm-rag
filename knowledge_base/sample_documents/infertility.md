# Infertility

Infertility means not conceiving after a year of regular unprotected sex, or after six months if the woman is over thirty-five. Causes can involve either partner, or neither may be identified.

Both partners should be assessed together, with tests of ovulation, tubes, and semen. Treatments range from timing advice and medicines to IVF.

See a doctor after a year of trying, or sooner with irregular periods, known pelvic infection, previous surgery, or age over thirty-five.
