# Aortic Dissection

Aortic dissection is a tear in the main body artery. People feel sudden tearing chest or back pain, often at peak intensity from the first second.

This is a surgical emergency. Do not wait to see if antacids help. Blood pressure control in hospital is urgent.

Call emergency services for sudden tearing chest or back pain, especially with a difference in arm pulses.
