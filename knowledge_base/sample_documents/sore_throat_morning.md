# Sore Throat Worse in the Morning

A raw throat first thing that eases after a drink can be dry air, mouth breathing from a blocked nose, or reflux overnight. A viral sore throat usually hurts all day and may come with fever.

Sip water, use a humidifier if the room is dry, and sleep slightly upright if you have a blocked nose. Honey in warm water can soothe if you are over one.

See a doctor if morning throat pain lasts more than a week or you cannot swallow. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
