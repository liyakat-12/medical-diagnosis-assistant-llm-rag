# Anal Fissure

An anal fissure is a small tear in the skin at the back passage, usually after passing a hard stool. It causes sharp pain during and after a bowel movement and a streak of bright red blood on the paper.

More fibre, plenty of water, a stool softener, and warm sitz baths allow most fissures to heal in a few weeks. Avoid straining and do not delay going.

See a doctor if pain or bleeding lasts more than six weeks, bleeding is heavy or dark, or there is a lump or discharge.
