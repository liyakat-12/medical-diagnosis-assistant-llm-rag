# Hydrocele

A hydrocele is a painless collection of fluid around a testicle, making the scrotum swell smoothly on one side. It feels soft and heavy and can glow when a light is shone through it.

Hydroceles in babies usually disappear by the first birthday. In adults they are harmless but can be drained or repaired surgically if large or uncomfortable.

See a doctor for any scrotal swelling to confirm the cause. Seek emergency care for sudden pain, redness, or rapid swelling.
