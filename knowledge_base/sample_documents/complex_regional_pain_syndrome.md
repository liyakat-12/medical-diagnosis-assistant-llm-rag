# Complex Regional Pain Syndrome

CRPS is severe burning pain in a limb that is far out of proportion to the original injury. The skin changes colour and temperature, swells, sweats, and even light touch becomes painful.

Early physiotherapy to keep the limb moving is the most important treatment, combined with nerve-pain medicine and a pain specialist team.

See a doctor if pain after an injury keeps worsening instead of settling, especially with colour or temperature changes in the limb.
