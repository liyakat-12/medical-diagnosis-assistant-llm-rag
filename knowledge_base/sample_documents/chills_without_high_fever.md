# Chills and Feeling Cold

Shivering or goosebumps can start as a fever is rising, after getting wet, or from anxiety. Chills with a new infection often come before you notice a high temperature.

Warm layers, fluids, and rest help. Check your temperature if you feel unwell. Isolated chills after a cold shower are not an illness.

See a doctor if chills keep returning with night sweats or weight loss. Seek emergency care for chills with a very stiff neck, confusion, or a spreading purple rash.
