# Loose Stools When Worried

Urgent loose stools before an exam or a meeting can be the gut reacting to stress. This can overlap with IBS. Blood, night pain, or weight loss is not explained by stress alone.

Eat a familiar breakfast, allow time for the toilet, and use slower breathing. Do not take leftover antibiotics.

See a doctor if loose stools last more than a few weeks or you have blood or weight loss. Seek urgent care for severe dehydration.
