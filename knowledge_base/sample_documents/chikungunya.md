# Chikungunya

Chikungunya is a mosquito-borne virus. Sudden fever and severe joint pain, often in the hands and feet, are typical. A rash may appear.

There is no specific antiviral. Rest, fluids, and clinician-advised pain relief help. Joint pain can linger for months.

See a doctor after fever and joint pain in a mosquito area. Avoid aspirin if dengue has not been ruled out.
