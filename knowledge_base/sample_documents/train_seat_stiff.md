# Stiff Body After a Long Train Seat

A long sit on a train or bus stiffens the back, hips, and knees. Ankles can swell by the end of the day.

Walk the aisle when it is safe, flex the ankles, and drink water.

See a doctor if one calf is swollen and painful. Seek emergency care for a swollen calf with breathlessness.
