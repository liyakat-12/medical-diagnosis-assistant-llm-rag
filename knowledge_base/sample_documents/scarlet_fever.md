# Scarlet Fever

Scarlet fever follows a strep throat infection. There is sore throat and fever, then a fine pink-red rash that feels like sandpaper, flushed cheeks with a pale ring around the mouth, and a strawberry tongue.

It needs a full course of antibiotics, which shortens the illness, reduces spread, and prevents complications such as rheumatic fever.

See a doctor promptly for a sandpaper rash with sore throat and fever. Seek urgent care for breathing difficulty, severe neck swelling, or a child who becomes very drowsy.
