# Hyperemesis Gravidarum

This is severe pregnancy vomiting, far beyond ordinary morning sickness. There is constant nausea, vomiting many times a day, inability to keep food or fluid down, weight loss, and dehydration.

It needs medical treatment with anti-sickness medicine, fluids given into a vein, and vitamin replacement. It is not something to simply endure.

Seek urgent care for vomiting everything for twenty-four hours, weight loss, very dark urine, dizziness, or a racing heart during pregnancy.
