# Esophagitis

Esophagitis is inflammation of the food pipe, usually from acid reflux, certain tablets taken without water, or infection. It causes burning chest pain, pain on swallowing, and a feeling of food sticking.

Treatment depends on the cause and may include acid-reducing medicine, taking tablets upright with plenty of water, or antifungal treatment.

See a doctor for painful swallowing lasting more than a few days. Seek urgent care if food becomes completely stuck or you vomit blood.
