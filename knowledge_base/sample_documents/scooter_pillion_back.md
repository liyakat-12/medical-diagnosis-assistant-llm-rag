# Back Pain After Riding Pillion

A pillion seat is often narrow and upright. A long ride can stiffen the low back and the neck.

Ask for a short stretch stop, and walk when you arrive. A small lumbar roll can help some people.

See a doctor if the pain lasts. Seek urgent care after a road crash, even if the pain starts later.
