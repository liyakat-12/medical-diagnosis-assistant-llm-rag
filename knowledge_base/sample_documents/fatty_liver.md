# Fatty Liver Disease

Fatty liver is extra fat in liver cells, often linked to weight, diabetes, or alcohol. Most people feel well at first.

Blood tests and scans find it. Weight loss, activity, and alcohol reduction can reverse early disease.

See a doctor for abnormal liver tests. Seek urgent care for jaundice, swelling, or confusion.
