# Loose Stools After a Meal

Sudden loose stools after a meal can be a stomach bug, a very rich meal, or a food you do not digest well, such as a large dairy portion. Most short bursts settle in a few days.

Sip extra fluid, use an oral rehydration drink if you feel dry, and eat small plain meals. Wash hands to protect others. Do not take leftover antibiotics.

See a doctor if diarrhoea lasts more than a week, you see blood, or you have a high fever. Seek urgent care for dizziness, no urine, or confusion.
