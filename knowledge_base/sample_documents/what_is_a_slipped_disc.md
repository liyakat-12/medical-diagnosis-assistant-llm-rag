# What a Slipped Disc Can Feel Like

A disc in the spine can bulge and irritate a nerve. You may get back pain with shooting pain, tingling, or weakness in a leg. Many improve with time and guided activity.

Keep gentle walking if you can, and avoid heavy lifting. Bed rest for days is usually not helpful.

See a doctor for lasting nerve pain or weakness. Seek emergency care for numb buttocks, or new trouble with bladder or bowel control.
