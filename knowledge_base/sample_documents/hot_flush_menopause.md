# Sudden Heat in the Face and Chest

A sudden wave of heat, sweating, and a red face can be a menopausal flush. Palpitations can come with it. Chest crushing pain is not a flush.

Dress in layers, keep the room cooler, and ease extra caffeine and alcohol if they trigger you.

See a doctor for frequent flushes or if periods have changed and you want advice. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain.
