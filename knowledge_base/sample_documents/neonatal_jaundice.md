# Newborn Jaundice

Newborn jaundice is yellowing of the skin and whites of the eyes, usually appearing on day two or three of life. It is common and often harmless, starting on the face and spreading downwards.

Frequent feeding helps clear it. Higher levels are treated with light therapy in hospital, which works quickly and safely.

Seek urgent care for jaundice in the first twenty-four hours of life, jaundice reaching the legs, a baby who is sleepy and feeding poorly, pale stools, or dark urine.
