# Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder

PTSD can follow a terrifying event. People get flashbacks, nightmares, jumpiness, and avoid reminders. It lasts more than a month.

Trauma-focused therapy and, if needed, medicine help. Support from trusted people matters.

See a professional if trauma memories disrupt life. Use emergency or crisis services if you feel unsafe now.
