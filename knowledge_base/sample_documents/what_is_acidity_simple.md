# What Acidity and Heartburn Are

Acidity is the everyday word for acid rising from the stomach. You may feel a burn behind the breastbone, a sour taste, or burps after a meal.

Smaller meals, less late spice and tea, and not lying down after food help. Pharmacy antacids may help if suitable.

See a doctor if heartburn is frequent. Seek emergency care for crushing chest pain, sweat, or pain into the arm.
