# Swollen Ankles by Evening

Both ankles looking puffy after a hot day or a long sit can be ordinary fluid pooling. One-sided swelling, especially with calf pain, can be a clot. Swelling with breathlessness needs urgent care.

Walk regularly, raise the feet in the evening, and avoid sitting still for hours. Compression socks help some people if a clinician agrees they are suitable.

See a doctor for swelling that lasts or you have heart or kidney disease. Seek emergency care for one swollen painful calf or swelling with severe breathlessness.
