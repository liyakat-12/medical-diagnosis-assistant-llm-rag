# Runny Nose on One Side

A cold can drip more from one side. A clear run from only one side after a head injury can be serious. A long-term one-sided run with blockage needs a check.

Saline and time help a typical cold. Do not ignore a one-sided leak after trauma.

See a doctor for a one-sided runny nose that lasts. Seek urgent care after a head injury if watery fluid leaks from the nose.
