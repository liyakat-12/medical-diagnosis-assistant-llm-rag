# What a Boil Is

A boil is a painful infected lump around a hair. It becomes red, hot, and may point with pus. Squeezing it can spread infection.

Use a warm cloth and keep the skin clean. Do not squeeze. Cover it if it drains.

See a doctor for a large boil or one on the face. Seek urgent care for spreading redness, fever, or a very unwell feeling.
