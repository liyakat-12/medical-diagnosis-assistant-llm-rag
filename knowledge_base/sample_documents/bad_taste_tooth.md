# Bad Taste From One Tooth

A sudden foul taste from one area can mean an abscess is draining. The tooth may have ached before. This needs a dentist, not mouthwash alone.

Rinse with warm salt water and arrange dental care. Do not ignore a bad taste with swelling.

See a dentist urgently. Seek emergency care for a swollen face or trouble swallowing.
