# Electric Shock Injury

Electric shock can cause burns at entry and exit points, muscle pain, numbness, confusion, irregular heartbeat, and loss of consciousness. Serious internal injury can exist with little visible skin damage.

Switch off the power at the source before touching the person. Never touch someone still in contact with a live current. Start CPR if they are unresponsive and not breathing.

Call emergency services for any high-voltage shock, lightning strike, shock causing loss of consciousness, burns, or any irregular heartbeat afterwards.
