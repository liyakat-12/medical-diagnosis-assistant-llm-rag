# Common Reasons for a Sore Throat

A sore throat with a runny nose is often viral. A very sore throat without a cough and with tender neck glands can be strep, but only a clinician can decide if a test is needed. Dry air and shouting also make the throat raw.

Rest, cool drinks, and time help most sore throats. Antibiotics are not needed for most viral throats.

See a doctor if you cannot swallow fluids or pain lasts more than a week. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
