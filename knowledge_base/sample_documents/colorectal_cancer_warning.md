# Bowel Cancer Warning Signs

Warning signs of bowel cancer include a lasting change in bowel habit, blood in the stool, a feeling of incomplete emptying, unexplained weight loss, tiredness from low iron, and ongoing abdominal pain.

These symptoms have many harmless causes such as piles, but they should never be assumed harmless. Diagnosis needs stool testing and usually a colonoscopy.

See a doctor without delay for these signs lasting more than three weeks. Seek emergency care for heavy rectal bleeding or a swollen abdomen with vomiting.
