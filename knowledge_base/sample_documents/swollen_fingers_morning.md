# Puffy Fingers in the Morning

Fingers that look a little swollen first thing can be ordinary fluid after a salty meal or early arthritis. Rings feeling tight most mornings for weeks needs a review.

Elevate the hands overnight on a pillow if they are puffy, reduce extra salt if you notice a link, and warm the hands before using a tight grip.

See a doctor if several finger joints stay swollen or morning stiffness lasts more than half an hour. Seek urgent care for a single hot finger with fever.
