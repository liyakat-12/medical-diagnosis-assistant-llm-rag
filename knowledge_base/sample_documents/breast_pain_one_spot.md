# Pain in One Spot in the Breast

Pain in one spot is less typical of hormonal cycle pain, which is usually both sides. Most one-spot pains are still not cancer, but a lump or skin change must be checked.

Note whether pain follows the cycle. A supportive bra can help musculoskeletal breast-wall pain.

See a doctor for pain in one spot, a lump, or skin change. Seek urgent care for a red hot swollen breast with fever.
