# Rubella (German Measles)

Rubella is a viral illness with a mild fever and a fine rash that starts on the face. It is often milder than measles, but it is dangerous in pregnancy.

Rest and fluids. Stay away from pregnant people if rubella is suspected. Vaccination prevents it.

Call a doctor before visiting if you suspect rubella. Seek urgent care for a pregnant contact or for a very unwell child.
