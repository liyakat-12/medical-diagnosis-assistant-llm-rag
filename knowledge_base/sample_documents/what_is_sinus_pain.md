# What Sinus Pain Feels Like

Sinus pain is pressure in the face, often the cheeks or forehead, with a blocked nose. It often follows a cold and can worsen when you bend forward.

Steam, saline, fluids, and time help most viral sinus pain. Antibiotics are not needed for most colds.

See a doctor if face pain lasts more than 10 days or fever returns. Seek urgent care for swelling around an eye or a severe headache.
