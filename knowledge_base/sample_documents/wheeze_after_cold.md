# Wheeze After a Cold

A musical whistle in the chest after a virus can be leftover airway irritation or a first sign of asthma. A wheeze that comes with a tight chest needs a clinical check. Severe wheeze is an emergency.

Sit upright and use a reliever inhaler only if one has already been prescribed for you. Avoid smoke.

See a doctor for a new wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness, blue lips, or being unable to speak.
