# Light Hurting the Eyes

Mild light sensitivity with a tired or dry eye after screens is common. Severe light pain with a red eye, headache, or reduced vision needs urgent care.

Rest in softer light, use lubricating drops if suitable, and wear sunglasses outdoors. This does not treat a serious eye infection.

See a clinician the same day for a painful red eye that hates light. Seek urgent care for vision loss or a chemical splash.
