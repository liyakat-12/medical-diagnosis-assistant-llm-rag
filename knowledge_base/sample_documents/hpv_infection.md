# Human Papillomavirus (HPV) Infection

HPV is a very common virus passed on by skin contact during sex. Most infections clear by themselves with no symptoms. Some types cause genital warts; others can slowly lead to cervical and other cancers.

The HPV vaccine prevents most of these cancers and works best before sexual activity begins. Regular cervical screening finds early changes while they are easy to treat.

See a doctor for genital lumps, and keep up with cervical screening invitations even if you feel completely well.
