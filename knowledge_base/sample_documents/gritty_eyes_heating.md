# Gritty Eyes in Heated Rooms

A sand feeling in the eyes in winter heating is dry-eye irritation. The eyes may water as a reflex. Contact lenses often feel worse.

Use a humidifier if you can, blink fully, and try preservative-free lubricating drops if a pharmacist agrees. Point fans away from the face.

See an optometrist if grit lasts or vision blurs. Seek urgent care for a painful red eye or sudden vision change.
