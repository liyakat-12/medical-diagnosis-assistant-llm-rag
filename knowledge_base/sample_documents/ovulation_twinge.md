# One-Sided Twinge Mid-Cycle

A short one-sided lower-tummy twinge around the middle of the cycle can be ovulation pain. It lasts minutes to a day. Sudden severe one-sided pain with fainting is not ordinary ovulation pain.

Heat and rest help a mild twinge. Note the date in a cycle diary.

See a doctor if mid-cycle pain is severe or you have unusual bleeding. Seek urgent care for sudden severe pelvic pain and fainting.
