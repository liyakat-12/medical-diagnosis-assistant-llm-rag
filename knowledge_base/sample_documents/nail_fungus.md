# Fungal Nail Infection

A fungal nail infection makes the nail thickened, yellow or white, crumbly at the edge, and lifted from the nail bed. It is usually painless and affects toenails most often.

Treatment is slow because nails grow slowly. Antifungal nail paint may work for mild cases, while more extensive infection needs several months of antifungal tablets with liver monitoring.

See a doctor to confirm it with a nail clipping before starting long treatment, and sooner if you have diabetes or poor circulation.
