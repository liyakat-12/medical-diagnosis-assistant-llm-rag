# Dehydration

Dehydration means the body has lost more fluid than it has taken in. Early signs are thirst, dark strong-smelling urine, passing urine less often, a dry mouth, tiredness, and dizziness on standing.

Drink water or oral rehydration solution in small frequent sips, especially during hot weather, vomiting, diarrhoea, or fever. Plain water alone may not be enough after heavy fluid loss.

Seek urgent care for no urine in eight hours, sunken eyes, a very fast pulse, drowsiness, or confusion, and for any unwell baby or older adult.
