# Groin Pain When Walking

Pain in the groin crease when walking or putting on socks often comes from the hip joint itself. A strain of the inner thigh after sport can feel similar but usually follows a sudden stretch.

Shorten your stride, avoid deep sitting, and rest from kicking sports. This pattern needs a check if it lasts, because hip arthritis and hernias can overlap.

See a doctor for groin pain lasting more than a few weeks or a bulge in the groin. Seek urgent care after a fall if you cannot walk, especially if you are older.
