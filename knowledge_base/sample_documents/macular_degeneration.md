# Age-Related Macular Degeneration

AMD affects central vision used for reading and faces. Straight lines may look wavy. Side vision is usually kept.

An eye specialist can diagnose it. Injections or lifestyle advice may slow some types. Smoking raises risk.

See an eye doctor for new distortion on an Amsler grid. Sudden central dark spots need urgent review.
