# Woozy After a Large Meal

Feeling light-headed after a big meal can happen when blood goes to the gut, especially in older adults. A heavy carb meal and alcohol make it more likely. Feeling faint with chest pain is different.

Eat smaller meals, sit for a while after eating, and stand up slowly. Limit extra alcohol with large meals.

See a doctor if you faint after meals. Seek emergency care for chest pain, severe breathlessness, or a faint from which you do not recover quickly.
