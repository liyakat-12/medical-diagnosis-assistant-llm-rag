# Pleurisy

Pleurisy is inflammation of the lining around the lungs. The main sign is a sharp chest pain that gets clearly worse when you breathe in deeply, cough, or sneeze.

It is usually caused by a viral infection and settles once the cause is treated. Anti-inflammatory pain relief and lying on the painful side can ease the discomfort.

See a doctor so the cause can be found, as pneumonia and blood clots can also present this way. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness or coughing up blood.
