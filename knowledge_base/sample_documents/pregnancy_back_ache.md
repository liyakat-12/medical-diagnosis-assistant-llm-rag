# Back Ache in Later Pregnancy

An aching lower back as the bump grows is common. The joints loosen and posture changes. Sudden severe back pain with bleeding or a headache and visual change is not ordinary ache.

Supportive shoes, a pillow between the knees in bed, and gentle walking help many people. A midwife can advise on safe movement.

See a midwife or doctor for back pain that limits walking. Seek urgent care for bleeding, severe headache, or visual change in pregnancy.
