# Seasonal Affective Disorder

SAD is depression that follows a seasonal pattern, usually starting in autumn and lifting in spring. There is low mood, oversleeping, craving carbohydrates, weight gain, and low energy.

Light therapy with a bright lamp each morning, getting outdoors in daylight, regular exercise, and talking therapy all help. Antidepressants are used for more severe cases.

See a doctor if low mood returns every year or stops you working. Seek emergency help for any thoughts of self-harm.
