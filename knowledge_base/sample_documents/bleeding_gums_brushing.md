# Gums That Bleed When You Brush

Pink in the sink after brushing is often gingivitis. Stopping brushing makes it worse. Pregnancy can also make gums bleed more.

Brush along the gum line twice a day and clean between the teeth. See a dentist or hygienist.

See a dentist for lasting bleeding. Seek urgent care for rapidly spreading facial swelling.
