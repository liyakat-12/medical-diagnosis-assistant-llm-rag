# Knee Pain After Running

A sore knee after a longer run or a sudden increase in distance is common. People say my knee hurts after I run. Irritation under the kneecap, a tired iliotibial band on the outer knee, or simple overload can all cause this ache.

Cut mileage for a week, run on softer ground if you can, ice after runs, and strengthen the thighs and hips. New shoes with worn-out cushioning can add load.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if running pain lasts more than a few weeks or the knee swells. Seek urgent care if you cannot take weight after a twist.
