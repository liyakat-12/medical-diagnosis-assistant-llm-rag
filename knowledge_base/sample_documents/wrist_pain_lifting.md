# Wrist Pain When Lifting

Pain at the base of the thumb or across the wrist when lifting a kettle or a child is often tendon irritation or strain. A click with pain after a twist can mean a ligament has been stressed.

Use two hands to lift, keep the wrist in a neutral position, and rest from repeated gripping. A simple wrist splint for short periods can help some people.

See a doctor if lifting pain does not settle in two weeks or the thumb base swells. Seek urgent care for sudden deformity after a fall.
