# Foamy Urine That Lasts

A little foam can be ordinary if the stream is fast. Foam that lasts in the pan and comes with swollen ankles or tiredness can mean protein in the urine and needs a check. This is not a diagnosis from foam alone.

Note swelling, tiredness, and whether foam happens every time. A urine test answers the question.

See a doctor for lasting foamy urine. Seek urgent care for sudden swelling with breathlessness.
