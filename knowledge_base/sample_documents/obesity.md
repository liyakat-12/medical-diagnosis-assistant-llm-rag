# Obesity

Obesity is extra body fat that raises risk of diabetes, heart disease, and joint pain. It is a health condition, not a moral failing.

A clinician can plan food, activity, and, if suitable, medicine or surgery. Small lasting changes beat crash diets.

See a doctor for a weight plan if BMI is high or weight-related illness is present.
