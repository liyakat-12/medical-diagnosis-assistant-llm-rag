# Shoulder Pain After Bowling

Repeated bowling or throwing can inflame the shoulder tendons. Pain in an arc when you lift the arm is typical.

Rest the bowling arm, ice, and ease the next spell. A sudden dead-arm pop needs a check.

See a doctor if you cannot throw after two weeks. Seek urgent care if the shoulder looks out of place.
