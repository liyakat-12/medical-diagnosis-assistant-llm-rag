# Chills Then Sweats

Shivering followed by sweating often means a fever is rising then falling during an infection. You may throw off the covers then feel soaked.

Use layers, drink fluids, and rest. Check your temperature if you feel unwell.

See a doctor if this cycle lasts more than three days. Seek emergency care for a stiff neck, confusion, or a spreading purple rash.
