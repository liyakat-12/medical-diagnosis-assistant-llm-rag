# Parkinson's Disease

Parkinson's is a movement condition. A resting tremor, slowness, stiff muscles, and smaller handwriting can appear gradually.

A neurologist diagnoses it and offers medicines that replace dopamine signalling. Exercise is part of care.

See a doctor for a new tremor or shuffling walk. Sudden chest pain or a fall with a head injury needs urgent care.
