# Blocked or Full Ear

A full or muffled ear after a flight, a cold, or water in the canal is common. Wax, eustachian tube blockage, or water trapped after swimming can all cause the same stuffed feeling.

Yawning, swallowing, and saline for the nose can open the tube. Do not poke cotton buds into the ear. Pharmacy wax drops may help if wax is likely.

See a doctor if the ear stays blocked for more than a week or you have hearing loss, dizziness, or discharge. Seek urgent care after a slap or explosion injury to the ear.
