# Pain When Rolling in Bed

The low back or hip can catch when you roll over at night after a long day of sitting or a strain. A mattress that sags can add to it.

Log-roll with the knees together, and place a pillow between the knees if you lie on your side.

See a doctor if night rolling pain lasts more than a few weeks. Seek emergency care for back pain with numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
