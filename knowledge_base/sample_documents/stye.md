# Stye (Hordeolum)

A stye is a painful red lump on the eyelid edge from a blocked oil gland. The eye may water.

Warm compresses for 10 minutes, three or four times a day, usually bring it to a head. Do not squeeze. Avoid eye makeup until it settles.

See a doctor if vision drops, the whole eyelid swells, or the lump lasts more than two weeks.
