# Muffled Hearing From Wax

A blocked, underwater feeling after swimming or using buds can be wax. Hearing drops on that side. Pain and fever suggest infection rather than wax alone.

Pharmacy wax drops may help if a pharmacist agrees. Do not use cotton buds, which push wax deeper.

See a clinician if hearing stays muffled. Seek urgent care for sudden hearing loss or spinning dizziness with hearing loss.
