# Jaw Tightness and Headache

Clenching the jaw in the day or grinding at night can cause temple headaches and a tired jaw. Stress and chewing gum a lot make it worse. The jaw joint can click without being damaged.

Avoid chewing gum, cut food into smaller pieces, and rest the teeth slightly apart with the tongue on the roof of the mouth. A warm cloth on the jaw can ease tightness.

See a dentist or doctor if the jaw locks, you cannot open fully, or headaches are daily. Seek urgent care for sudden facial weakness.
