# Thyroid Nodule

A thyroid nodule is a lump within the thyroid gland. Most are harmless and found by chance, causing no symptoms, though a large one can press on the windpipe or food pipe.

Assessment is a blood test plus an ultrasound, and sometimes a fine needle sample to check the cells. Most nodules only need monitoring.

See a doctor for any neck lump. Seek prompt review for a hard fixed lump, a hoarse voice, or difficulty swallowing.
