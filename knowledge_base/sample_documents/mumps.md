# Mumps

Mumps causes fever and painful swelling of the salivary glands, making the cheeks look puffy. It spreads in saliva.

Rest and fluids help. There is no specific everyday antiviral. MMR vaccine prevents most cases.

Call a doctor before visiting if you suspect mumps. Seek urgent care for severe headache, stiff neck, or swollen testicles with high fever.
