# Stiff Lower Back in the Morning

A stiff lower back that eases after you walk around is common with mechanical back pain and early wear. Stiffness lasting more than 30 to 60 minutes, especially in younger adults, can be inflammatory and needs a review.

A warm shower, gentle knee-to-chest stretches, and getting out of a soft sagging mattress can help ordinary morning stiffness.

See a doctor if morning back stiffness lasts more than half an hour most days or you have other swollen joints. Seek emergency care for back pain with fever and being unable to bend the neck.
