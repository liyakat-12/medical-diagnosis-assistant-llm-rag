# A Ring-Shaped Rash on the Skin

A round scaly patch with a clearer centre can be ringworm, a fungus, not a worm. It can spread on the body or between people.

Keep the skin dry and use an antifungal cream if a pharmacist agrees it is suitable. Wash clothes and sheets that touch the patch.

See a doctor if the ring spreads or is on the scalp. Seek urgent care for a very painful swollen patch with fever.
