# Restless Legs and Low Iron Possibility

Restless legs can be worse when iron stores are low, including after heavy periods. Not everyone with restless legs has low iron, so a blood test is needed before high-dose iron.

A clinician can check ferritin and advise. Stretching and less evening caffeine help some people.

See a doctor for restless legs that wreck sleep. Seek urgent care for a hot swollen calf.
