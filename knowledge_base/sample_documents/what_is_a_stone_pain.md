# What Kidney-Stone Pain Can Feel Like

A stone can cause waves of severe pain in the side or back, often into the groin, with nausea. You may see blood in the urine.

Sip water if you are not vomiting, and get in-person care rather than waiting it out if the pain is severe.

See a doctor for suspected stone pain. Seek emergency care for fever with this pain, no urine, or vomiting that blocks fluids.
