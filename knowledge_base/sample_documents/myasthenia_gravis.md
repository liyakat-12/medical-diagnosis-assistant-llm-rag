# Myasthenia Gravis

Myasthenia gravis causes muscle weakness that gets worse with use and better with rest. Typical signs are drooping eyelids, double vision, slurred speech, and difficulty chewing, all worse by evening.

It is treated with medicines that improve nerve-to-muscle signalling plus treatments that calm the immune system, supervised by a neurologist.

See a doctor for drooping eyelids or double vision that worsen through the day. Call emergency services for breathing difficulty or choking on food.
