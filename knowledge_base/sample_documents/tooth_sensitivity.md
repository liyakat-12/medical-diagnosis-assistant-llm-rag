# Sensitive Teeth

Sensitive teeth give a short sharp twinge with cold drinks, hot food, or sweet things. It usually comes from worn enamel or receding gums exposing the softer layer underneath.

Use a soft brush, a desensitising toothpaste twice a day, and avoid brushing straight after acidic food or drink. Improvement often takes two to four weeks.

See a dentist if one specific tooth hurts, the pain lingers for more than a minute, or it wakes you at night, which can mean nerve damage.
