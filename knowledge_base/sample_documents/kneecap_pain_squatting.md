# Kneecap Pain When Squatting

A grind or ache at the front of the knee when squatting, kneeling, or getting out of a chair is often kneecap joint irritation. Stairs and sitting with a bent knee can also flare it.

Avoid deep squats for a while, use a cushion when kneeling, and strengthen the thigh without pushing into sharp pain. Ice after activity can settle the ache.

See a doctor if the kneecap swells or you cannot straighten the knee. Seek urgent care after a kneecap that slipped out of place.
