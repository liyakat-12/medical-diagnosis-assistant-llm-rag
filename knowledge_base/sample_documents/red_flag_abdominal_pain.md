# Sudden Severe Tummy Pain Warning

Sudden severe abdominal pain, a rigid tummy, fainting, or vomiting blood needs emergency care. These can be signs of a serious problem such as a burst organ or bleeding.

Do not eat a large meal while waiting if pain is severe. Note whether the pain started suddenly and whether there is blood.

Call emergency services for sudden severe tummy pain, fainting, a rigid abdomen, or vomiting blood.
