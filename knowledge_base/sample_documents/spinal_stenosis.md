# Spinal Stenosis

Spinal stenosis is narrowing of the spinal canal, usually with age. The classic sign is leg pain, heaviness, and numbness that comes on after walking a set distance and eases when sitting or leaning forward.

Physiotherapy, posture work, weight loss, and pain relief help many people. Injections or decompression surgery are considered if walking becomes very limited.

See a doctor for leg pain that reliably appears with walking. Seek emergency care for loss of bladder or bowel control or numbness between the legs.
