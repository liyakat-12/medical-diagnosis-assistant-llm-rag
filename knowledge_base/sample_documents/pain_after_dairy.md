# Tummy Pain After Dairy

Bloating, wind, and loose stools after milk or ice cream can be lactose intolerance. It is not a milk allergy, which can cause hives or breathing trouble. A small amount of cheese or yoghurt is sometimes tolerated.

Try a short dairy-free spell and see if symptoms ease, then reintroduce to check. A clinician can advise if symptoms are severe.

See a doctor if symptoms are severe, you lose weight, or you have blood in the stool. Seek emergency care for breathing difficulty after dairy, which can be allergy.
