# Back Pain After Mopping

Bending and twisting with a mop is a common everyday back strain. The ache often starts that evening.

Keep the mop handle long, move the feet instead of twisting, and ice after a long clean.

See a doctor if the pain shoots down the leg and lasts. Seek emergency care for numb buttocks or new bladder trouble.
