# Car Sickness

Nausea, a cold sweat, and needing air on a winding road is motion sickness. Reading in the car makes it worse. Children are often affected.

Sit in the front, look at the horizon, and stop for fresh air. A pharmacist can advise on travel tablets if they are suitable for you.

See a doctor if motion sickness is extreme or you have spinning vertigo at rest. Seek urgent care for a sudden severe headache with vomiting.
