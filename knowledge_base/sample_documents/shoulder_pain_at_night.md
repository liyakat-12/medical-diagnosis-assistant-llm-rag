# Shoulder Pain at Night

Night shoulder pain that wakes you when you roll onto that side is common with rotator cuff problems and frozen shoulder. People also describe a deep ache after a day of reaching or carrying.

Try sleeping on the other side with a pillow hugged to the sore arm, ease overhead work, and keep the shoulder gently moving so it does not stiffen.

See a doctor if night pain lasts more than a few weeks or you cannot reach behind your back. Seek urgent care for chest pain with the shoulder ache, breathlessness, or sweating.
