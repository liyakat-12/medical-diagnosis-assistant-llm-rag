# Numb Fingers in the Cold

Fingers that go white or blue and numb in the cold, then turn red and throb as they warm, can be Raynaud's phenomenon. It is common and often mild. Other causes of numb fingers include a bent wrist or a neck nerve.

Keep hands warm with gloves, avoid sudden cold, and do not smoke. Warm the hands gradually, not on a scalding radiator.

See a doctor if colour changes are new, painful sores appear on the fingertips, or only one hand is affected. Seek urgent care for a finger that stays white and painful.
