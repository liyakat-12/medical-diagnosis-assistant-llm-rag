# Stiff Ankle in the Morning

A stiff ankle that eases after a few steps can follow an old sprain or early wear in the joint. The first minutes out of bed feel wooden, then walking loosens it.

Gentle circles in bed, supportive shoes, and staying active help. Avoid long periods of sitting with the ankle hanging down if it also swells.

See a doctor if stiffness lasts more than half an hour or the ankle keeps swelling. Seek urgent care for a hot red ankle with fever.
