# Ache on the Right Side of the Tummy

Pain on the right can be wind, constipation, a gallbladder attack after food, a kidney stone, or appendicitis if it moves to the lower right and worsens. These are possibilities, not a self-diagnosis.

Avoid a heavy fatty meal while you arrange advice if pain keeps returning. Do not eat a large meal if you may need urgent assessment.

See a doctor for lasting right-sided pain. Seek emergency care for pain that worsens, fever and vomiting, or a rigid tummy.
