# Panic Disorder

A panic attack is a sudden surge of intense fear with a pounding heart, sweating, trembling, breathlessness, chest tightness, dizziness, and a feeling of losing control. It peaks within ten minutes.

Panic disorder is diagnosed when attacks recur and you start fearing the next one or avoiding places. Breathing techniques, cognitive behavioural therapy, and sometimes medicine work very well.

See a doctor to rule out heart and thyroid causes first. Call emergency services for a first episode of chest pain, especially over forty or with heart risk factors.
