# Soreness After Cycling

A saddle can chafe the sit bones and the skin. Numbness in the groin after a long ride should ease when you stop.

Stand on the pedals, adjust the saddle height, and use padded shorts if you ride often.

See a doctor if numbness lasts or the skin breaks. Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding.
