# Back or Rib Pain After Coughing

A sharp catch in the chest wall or back after a coughing fit is often a strained muscle or irritated rib joint. It hurts more when you cough, laugh, or take a deep breath, and is tender to press.

Support the area with a pillow when you cough. Simple pain relief and time help a muscle strain. This is not the same as breathlessness or crushing central chest pain.

See a doctor if rib pain lasts or you cough blood. Seek emergency care for chest pain with breathlessness, sweating, or pain spreading to the arm.
