# What Asthma Can Feel Like

Asthma tightens the airways. You may wheeze, cough, feel tight in the chest, or get breathless, often at night or after exercise or dust.

Avoid smoke and known triggers. Use inhalers only if they have been prescribed for you.

See a doctor for a new wheeze. Seek emergency care if you cannot speak, the lips look blue, or a reliever does not help.
