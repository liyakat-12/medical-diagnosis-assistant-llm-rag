# Sore Feeling at the Adam's Apple

The front of the neck can feel sore after shouting, reflux, or a viral throat. A rapidly swelling neck or noisy breathing is an emergency.

Rest the voice, sip water, and avoid very hot drinks. Raise the head of the bed if reflux is a trigger.

See a doctor if the front of the neck stays sore or you feel a new lump. Seek emergency care for drooling or trouble breathing.
