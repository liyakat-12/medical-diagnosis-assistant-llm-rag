# Infective Endocarditis

Endocarditis is infection of the heart valves. It causes a fever that drags on for weeks, night sweats, tiredness, weight loss, breathlessness, and sometimes small painful spots on the fingers.

It is more likely in people with valve problems, artificial valves, or injected drug use. It requires several weeks of intravenous antibiotics in hospital.

Seek urgent medical care for unexplained fever lasting more than a week in anyone with a heart valve problem. Call emergency services for sudden breathlessness, weakness on one side, or collapse.
