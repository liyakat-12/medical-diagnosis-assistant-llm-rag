# Nausea Without Vomiting

Feeling sick without being sick can follow a migraine, reflux, pregnancy, a new medicine, anxiety, or a mild stomach bug. Nausea alone does not name one cause.

Sip water, eat small plain snacks if you can, and rest. Fresh air and avoiding strong smells help some people. Do not take leftover antibiotics for nausea.

See a doctor if nausea lasts more than a few days, you cannot keep fluids down, or you are pregnant and cannot eat. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache with vomiting, or chest pain.
