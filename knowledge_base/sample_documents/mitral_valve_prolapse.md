# Mitral Valve Prolapse

In mitral valve prolapse a heart valve bulges slightly when the heart squeezes. Most people have no symptoms, though some notice palpitations, mild chest discomfort, or tiredness. It is often found as a murmur on examination.

Most cases need only periodic monitoring with a heart scan. If the valve begins to leak badly, repair or replacement is considered.

See a doctor for palpitations or a newly heard murmur. Seek urgent care for fainting, worsening breathlessness, or chest pain.
