# Bloodshot Eyes When Tired

Red veins on the white of the eye after a late night, smoke, or dry air are common. Pain, light hurting, or reduced vision is not ordinary tiredness.

Sleep, lubricating drops if suitable, and avoid smoke. Do not use over-the-counter whitening drops for many days.

See a clinician for a painful red eye. Seek urgent care for vision change or a chemical splash.
