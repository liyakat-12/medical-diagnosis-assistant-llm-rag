# Head Lice

Head lice are small insects on the scalp that cause itch. Nits look like tiny white eggs glued to hair near the skin.

A pharmacist or clinician can advise a licensed lotion and wet-combing. Check close contacts. School exclusion rules vary.

See a clinician if treatments fail or the scalp is very sore and crusted.
