# Blood in the Urine (Haematuria)

Haematuria means blood in the urine, which may look pink, red, or cola-coloured, or may only show on a test. It can come with burning or pain, or with no symptoms at all.

Causes range from infection and kidney stones to an enlarged prostate and, less commonly, bladder or kidney cancer. Painless visible blood always needs investigation.

See a doctor for any visible blood in urine, even once and even if it clears. Seek emergency care if you cannot pass urine or you pass large clots.
