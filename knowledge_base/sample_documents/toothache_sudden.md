# Sudden Toothache

A sharp or throbbing tooth pain when biting or with cold drinks often means decay, a cracked filling, or an irritated nerve. Swelling of the gum next to one tooth can be an abscess starting.

Rinse with warm salt water, use simple pain relief if safe, and avoid very hot or cold drinks. See a dentist; antibiotics alone do not fix decay.

See a dentist as soon as you can. Seek urgent care for a swollen face or eye, difficulty swallowing, or a high fever with a dental swelling.
