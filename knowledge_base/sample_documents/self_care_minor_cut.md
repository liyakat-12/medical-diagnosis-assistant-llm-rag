# Looking After a Minor Cut

A small clean cut that stops bleeding with pressure can usually be cared for at home. Dirt in the wound raises infection risk. Deep, gaping, or animal-bite wounds need a clinician.

Wash with clean water, press with a clean cloth, then cover with a plaster. Keep the area clean and dry. Check tetanus status if the cut is dirty.

See a clinician for a cut that will not stop bleeding, looks deep, or is from a bite. Seek urgent care for spurting blood or numbness beyond the cut.
