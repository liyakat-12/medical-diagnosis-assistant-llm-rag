# Erectile Dysfunction

Erectile dysfunction is ongoing difficulty getting or keeping an erection. It becomes common with age and is strongly linked to diabetes, high blood pressure, smoking, obesity, alcohol, stress, and some medicines.

It can be an early warning of narrowed blood vessels, so a clinician checks blood pressure, sugar, and cholesterol. Lifestyle change, reviewing medicines, and prescribed tablets help most men.

See a doctor rather than buying unregulated tablets online, which can be dangerous with heart medicines. Seek emergency care for a painful erection lasting over four hours.
