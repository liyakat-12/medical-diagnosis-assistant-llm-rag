# Itchy Insect Bite

A small raised itchy bump after a mosquito or other bite is a local reaction. Redness of a coin size is common. A sting can be more painful at first.

Wash with soap and water, use a cold pack, and avoid scratching. Pharmacy antihistamine cream or tablets may help if suitable for you.

Seek emergency care for lip or tongue swelling, wheeze, or faintness after a sting. See a doctor if redness spreads over days with fever, which can be infection.
