# What Thyroid Problems Can Feel Like

An underactive thyroid can slow you: tired, cold, constipated, and low in mood. An overactive thyroid can speed you: shaky, hot, and losing weight. A blood test tells them apart.

Do not start thyroid tablets or iodine mixes on a guess. Write your symptoms for the clinician.

See a doctor for this pattern lasting weeks. Seek urgent care for very fast heartbeat, collapse, or severe drowsiness.
