# Sore Ankle After a Football Twist

A twist on the pitch can sprain the outer ankle. Swelling and bruising often show the next day.

Rest, ice, a snug bandage, and raise the foot. Do not play on a wobbly ankle.

See a doctor if you cannot take four steps. Seek urgent care if the ankle looks deformed.
