# Muscle Cramp

A cramp is a sudden tight, painful knot in a muscle, often in the calf or foot at night or after exercise.

Gently stretch and massage the muscle, walk it out, and drink water. Heat can relax a stubborn cramp.

See a doctor if cramps are very frequent, wake you every night, or you have swelling, weakness, or muscle wasting.
