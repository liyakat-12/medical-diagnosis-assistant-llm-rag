# Sore or Tight Foreskin

A tight, sore, or itchy foreskin can be irritation, infection, or a foreskin that does not pull back easily. Do not force it back. Pain with swelling and inability to return the foreskin is an emergency.

Wash with water, avoid scented soaps, and see a clinician rather than using leftover steroid cream unless advised.

See a doctor for lasting tightness or soreness. Seek emergency care if the foreskin is stuck back and the tip of the penis is swollen.
