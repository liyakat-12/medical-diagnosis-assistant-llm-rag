# Runny Nose and Sneezing

A runny nose with sneezing can be a cold or allergies. Itchiness in the nose and eyes without fever points more toward allergy. A cold more often brings a sore throat and lasts about a week.

Saline sprays, tissues, and rest help a cold. For allergies, reducing dust or pollen exposure and pharmacy antihistamines may help if they are suitable for you.

See a doctor if a runny nose lasts more than ten days with facial pain, or you have wheezing. Seek emergency care for swelling of the lips or tongue, or trouble breathing.
