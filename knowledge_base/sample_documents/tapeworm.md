# Tapeworm Infection

Tapeworm infection comes from undercooked pork, beef, or fish. Symptoms are often mild, with vague stomach discomfort, hunger or appetite loss, and flat white segments seen in the stool.

A prescribed single-dose treatment usually clears the worm. Cooking meat thoroughly prevents it.

See a doctor if you notice segments in stool. Seek urgent care for new seizures or severe headache, which can follow a different form of pork tapeworm affecting the brain.
