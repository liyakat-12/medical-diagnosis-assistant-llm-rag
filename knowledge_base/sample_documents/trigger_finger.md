# Trigger Finger

Trigger finger is when a finger catches or locks in a bent position and then snaps straight. There is a tender nodule at the base of the finger in the palm, and it is worst first thing in the morning.

Resting from gripping, splinting at night, and a steroid injection resolve most cases. A small day-case operation releases the tendon if these fail.

See a doctor if the finger locks fully and cannot be straightened, or if symptoms persist after six weeks of splinting.
