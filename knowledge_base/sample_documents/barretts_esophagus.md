# Barrett Esophagus

Barrett esophagus is a change in the lining of the food pipe caused by many years of acid reflux. It often causes no new symptoms beyond long-standing heartburn.

It matters because it slightly raises the risk of cancer, so clinicians arrange regular endoscopy checks and strong acid-reducing medicine.

See a doctor if you have had heartburn for several years, and urgently for difficulty swallowing, weight loss, or vomiting blood.
