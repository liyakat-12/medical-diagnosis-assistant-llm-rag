# Frozen Shoulder

Frozen shoulder slowly stiffens the shoulder so you cannot reach behind your back or lift the arm. Pain can disturb sleep.

It may last many months. Physiotherapy and, if needed, a specialist injection or procedure, restore movement.

See a doctor for a stiff painful shoulder lasting weeks, especially if you have diabetes.
