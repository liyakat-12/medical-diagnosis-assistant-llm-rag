# Urethritis

Urethritis is inflammation of the tube that carries urine out of the body. It causes burning when passing urine, discharge from the tip, itching, and needing to pass urine often. It is often sexually transmitted.

Testing for chlamydia and gonorrhoea is important, and treatment is prescribed antibiotics. Sexual partners also need testing and treatment.

See a doctor for burning urine with discharge. Avoid sex until treatment is finished to prevent passing it on.
