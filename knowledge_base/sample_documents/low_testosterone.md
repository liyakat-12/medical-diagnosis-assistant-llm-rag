# Low Testosterone

Low testosterone causes reduced sex drive, erection problems, tiredness, low mood, loss of muscle, and more body fat. Symptoms are vague and overlap with many other conditions.

Diagnosis needs a morning blood test repeated on two occasions, not symptoms alone. Treatment depends on the cause and may include weight loss, sleep apnoea treatment, or hormone replacement.

See a doctor for these symptoms rather than buying supplements, as untreated causes such as pituitary problems can be missed.
