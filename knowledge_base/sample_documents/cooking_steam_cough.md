# Cough From Kitchen Steam and Spice

Hot oil, chilli fumes, and steam can start a cough while you cook. This often settles when you step outside.

Use a vent or an open window, and take air breaks. Use a prescribed inhaler only if you already have one.

See a doctor if cooking always makes you wheeze. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness.
