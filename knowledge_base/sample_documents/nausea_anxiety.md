# Nausea When Anxious

Butterflies or feeling sick before a stressful event is common. You may have a loose stool as well. New vomiting with a severe headache is not typical anxiety nausea.

Eat a small familiar snack, sip water, and use slower breathing. Fresh air helps some people.

See a doctor if nausea is daily. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache with vomiting, or chest pain.
