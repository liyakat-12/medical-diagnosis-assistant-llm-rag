# Vomiting After a Spinning Ride

Feeling sick or being sick after a spinning ride is motion sickness. A cold sweat and needing to lie still are typical. A sudden severe headache with vomiting is not ordinary motion sickness.

Sit still, look at the horizon, sip water, and get fresh air. A pharmacist can advise on travel tablets for next time if they are suitable.

See a doctor if spinning happens at rest without rides. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache with vomiting.
