# Sudden Double Vision Warning

Seeing two of everything of sudden onset, especially with a drooping lid, headache, or weakness, needs urgent care. Long-standing double vision that a clinician already knows about is different.

Cover one eye to see if the doubling goes. Do not drive.

Seek emergency care for sudden double vision, especially with a weak face or a severe headache.
