# Headache After Crying

Crying can leave a tight forehead, puffy lids, and a drained headache. This is usually short-lived.

Cool the eyes, drink water, and rest in a quiet room. A regular meal can help if you have not eaten.

See a doctor if headaches after crying become frequent with low mood. Seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache or confusion.
