# Red Irritated Eye

A pink or red eye with grit, watering, or mild itch is often conjunctivitis or dryness. Vision is usually normal. Pain, light hurting a lot, or a cloudy spot on the eye is more serious.

Wash hands, do not share towels, and use lubricating drops if they are suitable. Remove contact lenses until the eye is better.

See a clinician the same day for a painful red eye, light sensitivity, or reduced vision. Seek urgent care after a chemical splash or a welding flash.
