# Itchy Hands After Washing Clothes or Dishes

Soap, detergent, and dish water strip oil from the hands. The skin then itches, cracks, and burns.

Wear gloves for wet work, use a bland moisturiser after every wash, and choose a milder soap.

See a doctor if the hands crack and bleed or the rash spreads. Seek urgent care for a rapidly spreading red hot hand with fever.
