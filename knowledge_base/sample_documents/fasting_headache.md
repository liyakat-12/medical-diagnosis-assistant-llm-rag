# Headache During a Fast

A headache while fasting is often dehydration, missed caffeine, or low blood sugar. It usually eases after fluids and food when the fast allows.

Drink water in the hours you can, and ease caffeine a few days before a long fast if you use a lot of tea or coffee.

See a doctor if fasting headaches are severe every time. Seek emergency care for a sudden worst-ever headache, confusion, or fainting.
