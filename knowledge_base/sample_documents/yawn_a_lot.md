# Yawning a Lot and Feeling Tired

Yawning a lot can mean you need sleep, the room is warm, or you are bored. It can also appear with tiredness from a cold. Constant yawning with chest pain is not typical everyday tiredness.

Get regular sleep, daylight, and a short walk. Cool the room if it is stuffy.

See a doctor if you are exhausted most days. Seek emergency care for chest pain or fainting.
