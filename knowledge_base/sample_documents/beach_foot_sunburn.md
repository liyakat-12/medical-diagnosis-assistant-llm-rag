# Sunburnt Feet After the Beach

The tops of the feet burn easily because people forget sunscreen there. The skin is red, hot, and sore that evening.

Cool water, a bland moisturiser, and staying off further sun help. Drink extra water.

See a doctor for large blisters or a child who is very unwell. Seek emergency care for confusion, fainting, or a very high temperature after sun.
