# Migraine With Light and Sound Sensitivity

A pulsing headache that makes light and noise feel unbearable, often with nausea, is a common migraine pattern. Some people see zigzag lights first. This is not a diagnosis from a chatbot, but a well-known pattern.

Rest in a dark quiet room, sip water, and use a treatment plan a clinician has already given you if you have one. Sleep and regular meals reduce some attacks.

See a doctor for new or changing migraine-like attacks. Seek emergency care for a sudden thunderclap headache, weakness, or a headache with fever and a stiff neck.
