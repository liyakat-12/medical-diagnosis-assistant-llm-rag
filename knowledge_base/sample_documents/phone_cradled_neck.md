# Neck Pain From Cradling a Phone

Holding a phone between the ear and the shoulder shortens one side of the neck. The ache can travel to the temple.

Use a headset or speaker, and switch sides. Stretch the side of the neck after a long call.

See a doctor if the arm tingles. Seek emergency care for neck pain with fever or a severe headache.
