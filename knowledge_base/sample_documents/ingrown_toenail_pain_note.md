# Painful Ingrown Toenail

A red, tender edge of the big toenail that catches on socks is an ingrown nail. Tight shoes and curved cutting make it worse. Infection can add pus.

Soak in warm salty water, wear wider shoes, and do not cut a deep V in the nail. A podiatrist can help.

See a clinician if you have diabetes or the toe is getting more red. Seek urgent care for spreading redness and fever.
