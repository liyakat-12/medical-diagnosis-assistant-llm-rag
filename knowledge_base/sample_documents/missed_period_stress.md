# Late Period After Stress

A period can come late after stress, a sudden change in weight or exercise, or illness. Pregnancy is still the first thing to consider if you could be pregnant. One late period is common.

Do a pregnancy test if there is any chance of pregnancy. Keep a simple cycle diary.

See a doctor if you miss more than one period, tests are negative, or you have pelvic pain. Seek urgent care for severe one-sided pain and fainting.
