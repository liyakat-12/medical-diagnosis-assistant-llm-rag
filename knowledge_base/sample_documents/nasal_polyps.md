# Nasal Polyps

Nasal polyps are soft painless growths in the nose lining. They cause a blocked nose that never fully clears, reduced sense of smell, a runny nose, and snoring.

A clinician may prescribe steroid nasal sprays to shrink them. Larger polyps that block the airway sometimes need a small day-case operation.

See a doctor for a blocked nose lasting more than twelve weeks or a lost sense of smell. Seek urgent care for swelling around the eyes or double vision.
