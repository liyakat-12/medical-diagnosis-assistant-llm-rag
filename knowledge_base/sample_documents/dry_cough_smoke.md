# Cough in a Smoky Room

A tickly cough in a smoky or polluted room is airway irritation. People with asthma can wheeze. The cough usually eases in clean air.

Step outside, sit upright, and use a prescribed reliever if you have one and know how. Avoid further smoke.

See a doctor if you cough every time you are around smoke or you want help to quit smoking. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness.
