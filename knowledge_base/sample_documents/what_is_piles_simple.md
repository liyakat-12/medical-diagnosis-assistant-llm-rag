# What Piles Are

Piles are swollen veins around the back passage. You may notice bright blood on the paper, itching, or a lump. Black stools or heavy bleeding is not ordinary piles.

Ease constipation with water and fibre, and do not strain. A pharmacist can advise a suitable cream.

See a doctor for lasting bleeding. Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding or fainting.
