# Soothing a Sore Throat at Home

Most sore throats are viral and improve in a week. Honey in warm water if you are over one year old, cool drinks, and rest make swallowing easier.

Simple pain relief can help if it is safe for you. Avoid smoke. You do not usually need antibiotics for a typical sore throat with a runny nose.

See a doctor if you cannot swallow fluids or the throat is still very sore after a week. Seek emergency care for drooling or noisy breathing.
