# Itchy Scalp After Hair Dye

Dye can irritate or, less often, truly allergically inflame the scalp. Burning, swelling, or widespread hives need a stop and a check.

Rinse well, stop further dye, and use a bland moisturiser on the hairline. Patch-test next time if you dye again.

See a doctor for a very swollen scalp or a spreading rash. Seek emergency care for lip swelling or trouble breathing.
