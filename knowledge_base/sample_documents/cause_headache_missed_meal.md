# Headache After Skipping a Meal

A dull headache with shakiness after missing lunch is often related to a long gap without food or drink. Some people also get this with delayed caffeine.

Eat a regular meal, drink water, and sit quietly. This does not prove low blood sugar disease on its own.

See a doctor if hunger headaches are frequent or you have known diabetes. Seek emergency care for confusion, collapse, or a sudden severe headache.
