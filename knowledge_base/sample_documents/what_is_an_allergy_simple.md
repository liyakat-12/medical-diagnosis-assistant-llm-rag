# What an Allergy Can Feel Like

An allergy is the immune system reacting to something such as pollen, food, or a sting. You may sneeze, itch, or get hives. Swelling of the tongue or trouble breathing is an emergency.

Avoid the trigger if you know it. A pharmacy antihistamine may help mild itch if suitable.

See a doctor for allergies that wreck your day. Seek emergency care for tongue swelling, noisy breathing, or collapse.
