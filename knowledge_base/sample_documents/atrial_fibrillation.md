# Atrial Fibrillation

Atrial fibrillation is an irregular, often fast heartbeat. People may feel palpitations, breathlessness, or tiredness. Some feel nothing.

A clinician confirms it with an ECG. Treatment may include rate control and blood thinners to lower stroke risk.

See a doctor for a new irregular pulse. Seek emergency care for chest pain, fainting, or one-sided weakness.
