# Puffy Front of the Knee After Kneeling

A squashy swelling over the kneecap after kneeling is often an irritated bursa. The knee usually still bends. A hot red knee with fever is more serious.

Kneel on a pad, ice, and rest from kneeling. Do not drain the swelling at home.

See a doctor if swelling lasts or the knee is hot. Seek urgent care for fever with a hot red knee.
