# Sore Nose From Glasses

Heavy frames or tight pads can pinch the nose and start a headache. A new prescription can also strain the eyes for a few days.

Ask an optician to adjust the pads, and take short glasses breaks if the optician agrees it is safe.

See an optician if the pinch or headache lasts. Seek urgent care for sudden vision loss or a painful red eye.
