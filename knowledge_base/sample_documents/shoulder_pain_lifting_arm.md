# Shoulder Pain When Lifting the Arm

Many people say my shoulder hurts when I lift my arm or reach a high shelf. Common possibilities are rotator cuff irritation, a pinched tendon under the shoulder bone, or a stiff capsule. Pain is often on the outer upper arm and worse at night when lying on that side.

Avoid repeated overhead lifting for a few days, use ice after activity, and try gentle pendulum swings. Sleeping with a pillow under the arm can reduce night pain. This is not a diagnosis.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if you cannot lift the arm after two weeks, or pain followed a fall. Seek urgent care if the arm is numb, cold, or the shoulder looks square and out of place.
