# Metabolic Syndrome

Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of findings together: a large waist, high blood pressure, high blood sugar, high triglycerides, and low good cholesterol. It usually causes no symptoms by itself.

It greatly raises the risk of diabetes, heart attack, and stroke. Weight loss, regular activity, less refined sugar, and stopping smoking reverse much of the risk.

See a doctor for a check of blood pressure, sugar, and cholesterol if your waist is large or there is a family history of diabetes or heart disease.
