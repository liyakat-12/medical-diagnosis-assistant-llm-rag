# Achalasia

In achalasia the muscle valve at the bottom of the food pipe fails to relax. Food and liquid build up, causing a stuck feeling in the chest, bringing up undigested food, chest pain, and weight loss.

It does not improve with diet changes alone. Treatment is a procedure to widen or cut the tight muscle, decided after a swallow study.

See a doctor for difficulty swallowing that is getting worse, especially with weight loss or food coming back up at night.
