# A Sprain After School Sports Day

A twist of the ankle or wrist is common on sports day. Swelling often grows over a few hours.

Rest, ice, a snug bandage, and raise the limb. Skip the next race.

See a doctor if the child cannot take four steps. Seek urgent care if the joint looks deformed.
