# What Heat Rash Is

Heat rash is tiny itchy or prickly bumps where sweat is trapped, often on the neck, chest, and creases. It flares in hot humid weather.

Cool the skin, wear loose cotton, and rinse off sweat. Avoid thick oily creams on the rash.

See a doctor if the rash looks infected. Seek emergency care for confusion or fainting in the heat.
