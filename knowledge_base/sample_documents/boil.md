# Boil (Furuncle)

A boil is a painful red lump around a hair follicle that fills with pus and comes to a yellow head. It is usually caused by staph bacteria entering through broken skin.

Apply a warm compress for ten minutes several times a day to help it drain naturally, keep it covered, and wash hands well. Never squeeze or cut it open yourself.

See a doctor if it is on the face or spine, larger than a coin, comes with fever or red streaks, or has not drained within two weeks.
