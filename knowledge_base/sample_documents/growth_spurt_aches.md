# Aches During a Growth Spurt

Teens can ache in the knees, shins, and back while they grow and play more sport. They should still walk and sleep fairly well.

Supportive shoes, rest after sport, and a regular meal pattern help. Pain with a limp or fever is not a simple growth ache.

See a doctor for one-sided swelling or a limp. Seek urgent care if a teen cannot take weight.
