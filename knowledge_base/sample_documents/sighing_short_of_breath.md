# Feeling You Cannot Get a Full Breath

A need to sigh or yawn to get a full breath, especially when anxious and at rest, is often not the same as true air hunger from lung or heart disease. New breathlessness on walking still needs a check.

Slow breathing through the nose, ease extra caffeine, and note whether it happens only at rest. This pattern can still deserve a medical review if it is new.

See a doctor for new breathlessness. Seek emergency care for severe breathlessness, chest pain, or blue lips.
