# Yellow Eyes or Yellow Skin

Yellow whites of the eyes, dark urine, and pale stools can mean jaundice. This is a sign, not a single disease, and needs a clinician, not home guessing.

Note the colour of urine and stools, and any fever or pain under the right ribs, to tell a doctor. Do not start random liver tonics.

See a doctor the same day for yellow eyes. Seek emergency care for yellow skin with confusion, vomiting blood, or severe belly pain.
