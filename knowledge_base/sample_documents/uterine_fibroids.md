# Uterine Fibroids

Fibroids are common non-cancer growths in the womb. They can cause heavy periods, pressure, and a sense of fullness.

A clinician may use ultrasound and offer medicine or procedures if bleeding is heavy.

See a doctor for soaking pads hourly or anaemia symptoms. Sudden severe pain needs same-day care.
